Marla to Square Feet Converter Pakistan

A Pakistan-focused marla to square feet converter that makes the measurement convention explicit instead of hiding a regional assumption.

Why marla conversion needs a convention

There is no single universal square-foot definition of a marla across every Pakistani property market. Two commonly encountered conventions are 272.25 square feet and 225 square feet. Your registry, housing scheme, developer or property documents should determine which one applies.

Formula

Square feet = marla × selected square-feet-per-marla value. A kanal is commonly 20 marla under the same local convention, but verify the standard for your transaction.
