How decimal-to-fraction conversion works

For a terminating decimal, count the digits after the decimal point. Use the decimal digits as the numerator and a power of 10 as the denominator, then reduce the fraction by its greatest common factor. For example, 0.75 has two decimal places, so it becomes 75/100, which simplifies to 3/4.

Negative decimals

The sign is preserved during conversion. For example, −0.5 becomes −5/10 and simplifies to −1/2.

What about repeating decimals?

This calculator is designed for terminating decimal input. Repeating decimals such as 0.333… require a different algebraic method and should not be treated as a finite decimal with an arbitrary number of digits.
