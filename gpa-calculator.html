GPA Calculator

Calculate grade point average from course grade points and credit hours.

Search intent

Targets GPA calculator, grade point average, weighted GPA and semester GPA. Grading scales differ by institution, so enter the grade-point values used by your school.

Formula & assumptions

GPA = Σ(grade points × credits) ÷ Σ credits. This is an estimate based on the values entered; it does not determine official academic standing.
