Formula & assumptions

GPA = Σ(grade points × credits) ÷ Σ credits. This is an estimate based on the values entered; it does not determine official academic standing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GPA weighted by credits? A standard GPA calculation weights each grade point by the course credit hours.

Which grading scale should I use? Use the scale required by your school or university.

How GPA is calculated

A weighted GPA uses each course's grade points together with its credit hours. A course with more credits therefore has a larger effect on the overall average.

Grade scales vary

Schools and countries can use different grading systems, including 4.0 and other scales. Select or enter the scale that matches your institution rather than assuming every school uses the same conversion.

Planning your semester

Use the calculator to model how a new course or changed grade could affect an average. The result is a planning estimate based on the grades and credits you enter.
