Salary Tax on PKR 200,000 in Pakistan (2026–27)

A steady monthly taxable salary of PKR 200,000 equals PKR 2,400,000 annually. Under the published Tax Year 2027 salaried-person slabs, the estimated annual income tax is PKR 156,000, averaging PKR 13,000 per month.

Eligibility and assumptions: This illustration assumes the full PKR 200,000 monthly amount is taxable salary for all 12 months and that the salaried-person slab table legally applies. Under the relevant rule, salary income generally must exceed 75% of taxable income for these rates to apply. This page does not test that condition, determine exemptions or allowances, or calculate every payroll deduction. If you have mixed income, confirm the correct tax treatment using current FBR material.

Worked calculation

Annual taxable salary = PKR 200,000 × 12 = PKR 2,400,000. This falls above PKR 2,200,000 and below PKR 3,200,000. Excess over the lower threshold is PKR 200,000. Tax on that excess is PKR 200,000 × 20% = PKR 40,000. Add the fixed PKR 116,000 from the lower bands: annual tax is PKR 156,000. Divide by 12 for an average monthly tax of PKR 13,000.

How to interpret this estimate for payroll planning

The PKR 13,000 figure is the annual tax estimate divided by 12; it is useful as a simple budgeting average, not a prediction of the exact deduction on every payslip. An employer may need to account for the timing of salary changes, bonuses, taxable benefits, prior deductions and year-end adjustments. Compare the annual estimate with your employer's payroll statement and check the current legal treatment if the two differ.

Marginal rate is not the effective rate

The 20% rate applies only to the portion in this band, not to the whole salary. In this example, the effective rate is PKR 156,000 divided by PKR 2,400,000, or 6.5%.

Allowances, bonuses and taxable salary

This estimate assumes the entire PKR 200,000 monthly amount is taxable and remains unchanged for 12 months. Bonuses, commissions, benefits and allowances can affect annual taxable salary depending on the applicable law. Do not automatically treat every allowance as taxable or exempt; check its legal treatment and use the correct annual taxable amount.

Filer status and withholding tax

Salary income tax is different from withholding tax on specific transactions. Filer status may affect some transaction taxes, but it does not replace the salary slab calculation shown here. This page does not calculate bank, property or other transaction-based withholding taxes.

Frequently asked questions

How much tax is due on PKR 200,000 monthly salary?

Under the assumptions above, PKR 156,000 per year, averaging PKR 13,000 monthly.

Is the take-home amount guaranteed to be PKR 187,000?

No. That subtracts only the average income-tax estimate; other payroll deductions are excluded.

Where can I verify the rates?

Check the FBR Finance Bill 2026 and FBR Tax Year 2027 rate card. Estimates are for educational planning and are not an FBR assessment.
