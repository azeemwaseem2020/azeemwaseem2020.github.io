Advanced potential-energy analysis

Use this page when you need to think carefully about reference height, gravity or changes in gravitational potential energy rather than only entering three numbers.

Core relationship

For motion near Earth's surface, PE = mgh is the standard model. For larger altitude changes or other gravitational systems, the appropriate physical model can differ.

For a straightforward calculation, use the Potential Energy Calculator.

Advanced potential-energy interpretation

Potential energy is defined relative to a reference and is useful because changes in potential energy can be related to work and energy conservation.

Near-Earth versus gravitational models

The simple mgh model is appropriate near Earth's surface when gravitational acceleration can be treated as approximately constant. Large altitude changes may require a gravitational potential model based on distance from Earth's center.

Sanity check

Increasing mass or height increases the magnitude of the near-Earth gravitational energy change when the reference and gravitational acceleration remain fixed.
