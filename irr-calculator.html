How to use the IRR calculator

Enter the cash flows in their correct time order, with the initial investment and later inflows or outflows represented consistently. IRR is the rate that makes the net present value of the cash flows equal to zero.

IRR questions

Use it to estimate an annualized return from irregular cash-flow streams; results depend on the timing and signs of the cash flows.
