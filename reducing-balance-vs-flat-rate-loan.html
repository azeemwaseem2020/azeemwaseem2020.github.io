Reducing Balance vs Flat Rate Loan Calculator

A practical reducing balance vs flat rate loan comparison. Two loans can advertise the same nominal percentage while producing very different total costs.

Reducing balance

Interest is calculated periodically on the outstanding principal, so the interest component generally declines as principal is repaid. The standard EMI formula is used for the estimate.

Flat rate

A simple flat-rate illustration applies the stated annual rate to the original principal for the full term. Actual products can use their own definitions, so this comparison is a mathematical model rather than a quotation.

Why the comparison matters

Compare total repayment, not just monthly payment. A longer term can reduce the monthly amount while increasing the total cost, and a flat-rate quote may not be directly comparable to a reducing-balance quote at the same headline rate.
