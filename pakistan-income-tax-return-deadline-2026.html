Pakistan Income Tax Return Filing Deadline 2026

As of 24 October 2026, news reports citing an FBR circular say the Tax Year 2026 return deadline was extended to 31 October 2026 for taxpayers whose original 30 September deadline had already been extended to 15 October. Verify your own filing category and any later notice directly with FBR before relying on this date.

Check official FBR website Read deadline report (Dawn, 15 Oct 2026)

Who does the reported extension cover?

The reported extension applies to people who were required to file by 30 September 2026 and whose deadline had previously been extended to 15 October. Do not assume every taxpayer, company, special tax year or other filing category automatically has the same due date; check the applicable FBR notice.

What to do before submitting

  1. Sign in to the official FBR IRIS portal using your own credentials.
  2. Confirm the tax year and return type that applies to you.
  3. Reconcile salary certificates, withholding statements, bank profit and other relevant income records.
  4. Submit the return and any required wealth statement, then save the acknowledgement and payment evidence where relevant.
  5. If your category or deadline is unclear, check the latest FBR notice or consult a qualified tax professional.

Where to verify deadlines

FBR’s general due-date page lists standard deadlines, but extensions can be issued through later circulars. A news report is useful for discovery; the latest official circular and your filing category should control.
