How to avoid common time-calculation errors

For a shift that starts in the evening and ends after midnight, use a tool that supports overnight durations or explicitly adjust the end date. Subtract unpaid breaks only once. When converting hours to workdays, do not assume every workplace uses an eight-hour day unless that is your chosen basis. For date ranges, decide whether the start and end dates are counted inclusively.

For payroll, overtime and leave decisions, check the applicable employment contract and local rules. A calculator can perform arithmetic, but it cannot determine which legal rule applies to your situation.

Common questions

How do I calculate hours across midnight?

Use a time calculator that supports elapsed time or enter the correct end date. A shift from 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM lasts eight hours before subtracting breaks.

Are calendar days and business days the same?

No. Calendar days include weekends and holidays. Business-day calculations depend on the workweek and which public holidays are excluded.

Does an overtime calculator determine legal entitlement?

No. It estimates amounts from the inputs you supply. Eligibility, thresholds, exemptions and rates depend on the relevant jurisdiction, contract and current rules.
