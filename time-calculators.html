Time Calculators

Choose the right tool for elapsed time, clock arithmetic, date intervals or age. The key distinction is whether you are calculating a duration on a clock or counting calendar dates.

  • Time Calculator — add or subtract hours, minutes and seconds, or calculate elapsed time across midnight.
  • Date Calculator — add or subtract days from a date and work with calendar dates.
  • Days Between Dates Calculator — count the interval between two dates.
  • Age Calculator — calculate age from a birth date and a reference date.
  • Birthday Calculator — explore birthday-related date calculations.
  • Days to Weeks Calculator — convert a duration in days to weeks and remaining days.
  • Unit Converter — convert supported measurement units.

Which time calculation do you need?

Use a clock-time calculator when you know a start and finish time, such as a shift from 09:00 to 17:30. Use a date calculator when the interval includes actual dates, multiple days or a calendar deadline. For age, calendar rules matter: dividing the number of days by 365 does not always give an exact calendar age because leap years and month lengths vary.

Examples and assumptions

  • 09:00 to 17:30 is 8 hours 30 minutes when no break is excluded.
  • 22:30 to 01:15 is 2 hours 45 minutes if the finish is on the following day.
  • Adding 90 minutes to 10:20 gives 11:50.

When a tool assumes that an earlier finish time means the following day, verify that assumption against the real dates. Clock arithmetic alone does not resolve time zones, daylight-saving changes or a shift spanning several dates.

How to get a reliable result

  1. Choose a calculator matching the question: clock time, calendar date, age or unit conversion.
  2. Enter the correct dates and use the displayed input format.
  3. Check whether the start or end date is included in the interval.
  4. For payroll, legal deadlines, travel across time zones or official records, confirm the applicable rules separately.

Frequently asked questions

Is elapsed time the same as the difference between dates?

Not always. Elapsed clock time measures hours, minutes and seconds; date intervals count calendar boundaries or days according to the tool's convention.

Does the time calculator handle time zones?

The standard clock-time tool does not determine time-zone offsets or daylight-saving transitions. Use a date-aware time-zone tool for those cases.

Why can age differ from days divided by 365?

Calendar age depends on month lengths and leap years, so an exact age calculation should compare calendar dates rather than use a fixed 365-day year.
