FORMULA

Dividend yield

Dividend yield = dividend per share ÷ share price × 100

Example: PKR 5 dividend ÷ PKR 100 share price × 100 = 5% yield.

How the PSX dividend yield calculation works

Dividend yield compares a cash dividend per share with the share price used in the calculation. The formula is dividend per share ÷ share price × 100. Estimated gross cash income for your entered holding is number of shares × dividend per share.

Worked example: yield and estimated cash income

Suppose you hold 100 shares, use a share price of PKR 100, and enter PKR 5 per share as the dividend for the selected period. The yield is 5 ÷ 100 × 100 = 5%, and the estimated gross dividend is 100 × 5 = PKR 500. These are arithmetic examples, not a forecast or a confirmation that a company will declare or pay this dividend.

Choose the dividend period carefully

Select Per declared dividend when the amount entered is for one declared payment. Select Annual total only when the dividend-per-share amount represents the total for a full year. The calculator does not annualise a quarterly or interim dividend automatically, and the yield shown is based on the period represented by your input.

Gross dividend is not necessarily take-home cash

The result is a gross estimate before any applicable withholding tax, brokerage or other charges. Actual cash received can depend on the investor’s tax treatment, eligibility, the company’s final payment and account records. This tool does not determine a personal tax rate or calculate net proceeds.

Price basis and investment risk

Keep the price basis consistent with your purpose: current market price for a current-price comparison, or your own purchase cost for a cost-yield calculation. A falling share price can make the percentage yield appear higher even when the cash dividend has not increased; it does not by itself mean the investment is safer or more profitable. Share prices and future dividends can change, and past payments do not guarantee future income.

Important PSX assumptions

This tool does not fetch live PSX prices, company announcements, eligibility dates, tax rates or brokerage charges. Before relying on an estimate, verify the company’s official dividend announcement, relevant book-closure/eligibility details, payment status and the price basis you intend to use.

Official investor education reference

The PSX Guide to Investors describes dividend yield as a measure relating cash dividend to share price. Use the company’s latest dividend announcement and your relevant share-price basis; this calculator does not fetch live prices, predict future dividends or determine your tax treatment.
