FORMULA

Dividend yield

Dividend yield = dividend per share ÷ share price × 100

Example: PKR 5 dividend ÷ PKR 100 share price × 100 = 5% yield.

How the PSX dividend yield calculation works

The calculator uses the figures you enter. Estimated dividend income is shares × dividend per share. Dividend yield is dividend per share ÷ share price × 100.

Important PSX assumptions

This tool does not fetch live PSX prices, company announcements, tax rates or brokerage charges. For an actual investment record, verify the declared dividend, eligibility date, share price and applicable taxes or charges using current company and broker information.

Dividend yield can change with price

If the dividend per share stays unchanged while the market price moves, the calculated yield changes because the denominator changes. A higher share price produces a lower yield for the same dividend per share, while a lower price produces a higher yield.
