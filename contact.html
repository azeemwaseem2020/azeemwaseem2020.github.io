What happens to a correction report?

We review reported calculation or content issues against the page formula, inputs and expected result. If a correction is needed, the relevant page can be updated so future visitors see the improved calculation or explanation. For changing information, the source and date of the information matter.

Before you send a message

Our guides explain many of the formulas and assumptions behind the tools, while the FAQ covers common questions. You can also review the Disclaimer for important limitations.

Corrections and responsible use

Calcora aims to provide practical, transparent tools, but calculator outputs are estimates and can depend on the assumptions and inputs used. For important financial, electrical, agricultural or investment decisions, verify the result with appropriate primary sources or a qualified professional.
