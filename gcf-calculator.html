Greatest Common Factor Calculator

A clear, focused calculator with validation and a short explanation of the method.

Formula/method: Euclid's algorithm repeatedly replaces (a, b) with (b, a mod b) until the remainder is zero.

Worked example

For 48 and 18: 48 mod 18 = 12, 18 mod 12 = 6, and 12 mod 6 = 0, so the GCF is 6.

How to use

  1. Enter two whole numbers.
  2. Select Calculate GCF.
  3. Use the result to simplify fractions or factor numbers.

FAQ

What is GCF? The largest positive integer that divides both numbers exactly.
