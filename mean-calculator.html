Arithmetic mean = sum of values ÷ number of values.

How to find the arithmetic mean

Add all values together and divide the total by the number of values: mean = sum ÷ count. Enter the values carefully and keep their units consistent when a unit is meaningful.

Mean vs average

In everyday use, “average” often means arithmetic mean. Other averages and statistical measures can behave differently, so choose the measure that matches your data.

How to find the arithmetic mean

The arithmetic mean is calculated by mean = sum of values ÷ number of values. For 8, 12, 15 and 25, the sum is 60 and there are four values, so the mean is 15.

Worked example

Suppose monthly electricity costs are Rs. 8,500, Rs. 9,200, Rs. 7,900 and Rs. 10,400. Their total is Rs. 36,000. Dividing by four months gives an average monthly cost of Rs. 9,000. This is a useful summary, but it does not show how much the bills varied.

A practical Pakistan example

A family in Punjab could calculate the mean of several monthly water, electricity or fuel expenses to establish a planning baseline. For a farm, the same method can summarize repeated measurements such as feed cost per day, but the units and measurement period should remain consistent.

Mean versus median

The mean is sensitive to unusually large or small observations. If five monthly expenses are Rs. 8,000, Rs. 8,500, Rs. 9,000, Rs. 9,500 and Rs. 50,000, the mean rises sharply because of the Rs. 50,000 value. The median may better describe a typical month in a data set with a strong outlier.

Common mistakes

Do not divide by the wrong number of observations, and do not mix units. Missing values should be handled explicitly rather than silently treated as zero unless zero is genuinely the measurement.

Mean FAQ

Can the mean be a value not present in the data? Yes. A mean is a calculated summary and does not have to equal one of the observations.

Does an outlier affect the mean? Yes. Extreme values can pull the mean toward them.

Is mean the same as average? In everyday use, “average” often means arithmetic mean, although other types of averages exist.

When is median better? Median can be more representative when the data are skewed or contain substantial outliers.
