Cash Withdrawal Tax Calculator Pakistan: Formula and Practical Checks

This calculator estimates arithmetic withholding on a cash withdrawal using the amount and rate you enter. It does not determine whether a particular withdrawal is legally taxable, whether an exemption applies, or which taxpayer category a bank should use. Those decisions depend on the current law, your circumstances and the transaction details.

Formula

Estimated withholding = withdrawal amount × applicable rate ÷ 100. Estimated amount after withholding = withdrawal amount − estimated withholding. The second figure is only a simple arithmetic illustration; it does not include bank fees, account restrictions or other deductions.

How to use the calculator

  1. Enter the planned withdrawal amount in Pakistani rupees.
  2. Confirm the applicable rate for your taxpayer status and transaction date using current official guidance or your bank.
  3. Enter that rate as a percentage, not as a decimal fraction.
  4. Compare the estimate with the bank's disclosed treatment before making a transaction.

Worked example

If a hypothetical withdrawal is PKR 100,000 and the verified applicable rate is 0.8%, estimated withholding is 100,000 × 0.8 ÷ 100 = PKR 800. The amount after this assumed withholding is PKR 99,200 before any other fees. The 0.8% is an example for arithmetic only, not a statement of the current Pakistani tax rate.

Important distinctions

Cash withdrawal withholding is not the same as tax on bank profit, income tax on salary, a transfer fee or a card charge. A rate may change between tax years, and rules may distinguish taxpayer status or transaction types. Do not infer your status from an old return receipt alone; check the current Active Taxpayer List where relevant and confirm how the specific rule applies.

Frequently asked questions

Does every cash withdrawal incur tax?

Do not assume that. Eligibility, thresholds, exemptions and rates must be checked against current official rules and your circumstances.

Can I use the rate shown by a friend?

Only if it applies to the same date, taxpayer category and transaction. Verify it independently.

Is this result an official tax assessment?

No. It is an arithmetic estimate based on the values you provide.
