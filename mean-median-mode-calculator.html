Mean, median and mode formulas

Mean = sum of all values ÷ number of values. Median is the middle value after sorting; for an even number of values, it is the average of the two middle values. Mode is the value or values occurring most frequently. Range = maximum − minimum.

Worked example

For 2, 4, 4, 7, 9, the mean is 26 ÷ 5 = 5.2, the median is 4, the mode is 4, and the range is 9 − 2 = 7.

Mean vs median

The mean uses every value and can be strongly affected by extreme observations. The median depends on the ordered middle position and can be less sensitive to outliers.

When there is no mode

If every value occurs only once, there is no mode. A dataset can also have multiple modes when several values share the highest frequency.

FAQ

How do I calculate an average? For the arithmetic mean, add all values and divide by their count.

What is the median of an even-sized dataset? Sort the values and average the two middle values.

Can a dataset have two modes? Yes. If two values tie for the highest frequency, the dataset is bimodal.
