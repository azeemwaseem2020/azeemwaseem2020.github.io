Convert days to weeks

A standard week contains 7 days, so converting days to weeks is a simple division. The calculator shows both the exact decimal-week result and a practical breakdown into complete weeks plus remaining days.

Formula

Weeks = days ÷ 7

For the reverse conversion, days = weeks × 7. The conversion is exact because the week-to-day relationship is fixed at seven days.

Examples

  • 7 days = 1 week
  • 14 days = 2 weeks
  • 30 days = 4.285714 weeks = 4 weeks 2 days
  • 45 days = 6.428571 weeks = 6 weeks 3 days
  • 90 days = 12.857143 weeks = 12 weeks 6 days

For schedules, leave periods, courses, project timelines, or other duration planning, the weeks-and-days format can be easier to read than a decimal.

Common questions

How many weeks are in a day?

One day is 1/7 of a week, or about 0.142857 weeks.

How many days are in 4 weeks?

Four weeks contain 28 days.

What is the difference between decimal weeks and weeks plus days?

They describe the same duration in different formats. For example, 30 days is 4.285714 weeks and also 4 weeks plus 2 days.

Does a month equal four weeks?

Not exactly. Four weeks is 28 days, while calendar months can have 28, 29, 30, or 31 days.
