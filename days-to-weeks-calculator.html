Quick guide: how to use this calculator

Enter the number of days to convert the duration into weeks and any remaining days.

Formula or method

Weeks = days ÷ 7; the remainder represents the days left after complete weeks.

Useful note

For planning schedules, decide whether you need decimal weeks or complete weeks plus remaining days.
