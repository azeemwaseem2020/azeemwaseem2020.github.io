PSX search intent: what can you calculate here?

Investors may search for a PSX profit calculator, share profit calculator Pakistan, stock return calculator, PSX dividend calculator, average share price calculator or break-even share price. These are related but different calculations, so this page keeps each result visible and explains the input assumptions.

Why this PSX calculator is different

Most simple stock calculators stop at buy price, sell price and quantity. This version is designed around the wider questions Pakistani investors actually face: What will I keep after costs? What is my break-even? What happens to average cost after another purchase? How does a bonus issue change the share count? What will a rights subscription cost? What is the dividend yield?

PSX profit and loss formula

Invested value = average buy price × shares. Sale value = current or sale price × shares. Gross profit/loss = sale value − invested value. Net result = gross profit/loss − entered buy costs − entered sell costs − optional estimated CGT.

Average price after buying more shares

New average price = (existing shares × existing average price + new shares × new purchase price) ÷ total shares. This is useful when adding to an existing PSX position at a different price.

Bonus share adjustment

A bonus issue increases the number of shares while the original total cost remains the starting cost basis in this simplified calculation. The adjusted average is therefore original total cost ÷ adjusted share count. Actual tax and cost-basis treatment should be verified for the specific corporate action.

Rights issue calculator

The rights tool estimates the number of rights shares, subscription cash required and a simplified theoretical blended average using your existing average price. It is a planning calculation, not a prediction of the post-issue market price.

Dividend calculator

Gross dividend = dividend per share × shares. Estimated withholding = gross dividend × entered withholding rate. Estimated net dividend = gross dividend − withholding. Dividend yield = dividend per share ÷ reference share price × 100.

Annualized return

For a completed trade, annualized return can help compare a completed holding period. It is highly sensitive to short holding periods and is not a forecast. If the holding period is zero, Calcora does not annualize the result.

Corporate actions matter

Dividends, bonus shares and rights issues can change how investors interpret share count, cost basis and historical price performance. Always check the relevant company and exchange announcement before making a final calculation.

Trading costs matter

Brokerage commission and other transaction charges affect real returns. PSX advises investors to consider applicable taxes and brokerage charges and notes that brokerage commissions can vary by firm.

Live prices and research data

This page intentionally does not fabricate live market data. Use your broker or an authoritative market source for current prices. For company research, investors commonly examine price, change, one-year change, P/E, dividend yield, market value, free float and volume.

Example

If 1,000 shares are bought at PKR 100 and sold at PKR 120, gross profit is PKR 20,000 before costs and tax. Enter actual transaction charges and an applicable tax assumption to make the estimate more realistic.

Important limitation

Calcora is an educational arithmetic tool, not a broker, tax adviser or investment adviser. Tax rules and corporate-action treatment can change. Confirm current requirements with the relevant official source, your broker or a qualified adviser before acting.

How to use the PSX calculator for a real trade

Start with the number of shares, purchase price and expected or actual selling price. Then include brokerage, taxes or other charges when the calculator provides those inputs. Keep the transaction date and price source separate from the mathematical calculation because market prices and applicable charges can change.

  1. Enter the actual quantity of shares.
  2. Enter your purchase price and selling price.
  3. Review gross profit or loss before costs.
  4. Include applicable trading costs and taxes using current figures.
  5. Check the break-even price and percentage return before interpreting the result.

The calculator explains the arithmetic; it does not provide a live PSX quote, investment recommendation or guarantee of future performance.
