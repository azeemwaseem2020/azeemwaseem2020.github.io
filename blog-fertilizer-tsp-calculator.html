TSP Fertilizer Calculator Guide

TSP is a concentrated phosphorus source, so a small change in the target or product analysis can noticeably change the application rate. Keep the nutrient basis and field units consistent from start to finish.

What is TSP?

TSP is a concentrated phosphorus fertilizer. Its guaranteed analysis should always be taken from the actual product label because commercial specifications can vary.

TSP rate formula

Product required = P₂O₅ required ÷ P₂O₅ fraction. For example, if the product is 46% P₂O₅ and the target is 46 kg P₂O₅/ha, the mathematical rate is 100 kg product/ha.

Why the P₂O₅ basis matters

Fertilizer labels commonly express phosphorus as P₂O₅. If a soil or crop recommendation is expressed as elemental phosphorus, convert it before comparing it with a P₂O₅ product analysis. A common conversion is P ≈ P₂O₅ × 0.44.

Field calculation

  1. Confirm the nutrient target and unit.
  2. Enter the exact TSP analysis.
  3. Calculate kg/ha or kg/acre.
  4. Multiply by field area.
  5. Divide total kilograms by bag size.
  6. Add the product price and other costs if required.

Worked example

A 46% P₂O₅ TSP product at 100 kg/ha over 8 hectares requires 800 kg product. With 50 kg bags, the exact requirement is 16 bags. If each bag costs PKR 5,000, product cost is PKR 80,000.

TSP and nitrogen planning

TSP is primarily used for phosphorus. If the crop also needs nitrogen, the nitrogen target must be covered by another suitable source unless the selected product contains nitrogen according to its guaranteed analysis.

Cost comparison

A cheaper bag is not automatically the cheaper nutrient source. Compare cost per kilogram of nutrient, total field requirement and any additional nutrients supplied by competing products.

What Calcora can do

Calcora can calculate fertilizer quantity, nutrient supply, bags and cost from supplied targets and product analysis. It does not select the correct crop-specific phosphorus target. Use soil testing and local agronomic guidance for that decision.

Before you trust the number

Do one quick back-check: multiply the calculated product rate by the nutrient fraction and make sure it returns the intended nutrient rate. Then check the area conversion and the bag or volume conversion separately. Keeping those steps separate makes a wrong unit much easier to spot.

Common mistakes

  • Using 46 instead of 0.46 in the formula.
  • Mixing P and P₂O₅.
  • Mixing acres and hectares.
  • Forgetting the field-area multiplication.
  • Comparing products by bag price only.

FAQ

How much 46% TSP supplies 46 kg P₂O₅?

100 kg of product supplies 46 kg P₂O₅.

Does TSP supply nitrogen?

Use the actual guaranteed analysis. Standard TSP is primarily a phosphorus source and should not be assumed to provide meaningful nitrogen.

Can TSP replace a soil test?

No. The calculator performs arithmetic; it does not measure nutrient status or create a field recommendation.
