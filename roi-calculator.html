Where this calculator fits

ROI connects investment cost, return, profit and percentage return. It is a return-measurement calculation and should remain distinct from future-value investment growth calculations.

Frequently asked questions

Is the calculated result guaranteed?

No. It is an estimate from the inputs. Actual outcomes depend on the product terms and the conditions that apply to the transaction or investment.

How to interpret return on investment

ROI compares the gain or loss from an investment with the amount invested. A positive ROI indicates a gain under the selected assumptions, while a negative ROI indicates a loss.

Worked example

If an investment costs PKR 200,000 and is later worth PKR 240,000, the gain is PKR 40,000 and the simple ROI is 20%, before considering fees, taxes or the time value of money.

ROI is not the same as annual return

A 20% ROI earned over one month is very different from a 20% ROI earned over five years. When comparing investments held for different periods, use a time-adjusted measure such as an annualized return where appropriate.

Include the right costs

A realistic investment comparison should consider purchase costs, selling costs, management fees, taxes and other directly attributable expenses. Excluding material costs can make an investment appear more profitable than it actually was.

ROI FAQ

Can ROI compare investments with different holding periods?

It can describe each investment, but simple ROI alone does not make different holding periods directly comparable. Add a time dimension when the duration differs.

ROI calculator Pakistan and return on investment in PKR

For Pakistan use cases, enter investment cost and proceeds in PKR to calculate simple return on investment. ROI = (return − cost) ÷ cost × 100. The percentage does not by itself describe annualized return, inflation, taxes or transaction costs.

For a PKR-denominated comparison, keep the same currency and time period on both sides of the calculation. This makes the result useful for comparing entered Pakistan investment scenarios without implying a guaranteed return.
