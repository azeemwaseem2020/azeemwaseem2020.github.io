Find the tool by the job, not just the formula

Search intent examples: People often search for the problem they want to solve rather than the mathematical formula. Finance: Start with salary tax, Zakat, EMI, affordability, loan interest or compound growth. PSX: Move from average price and trade P/L to dividend and portfolio allocation. Solar: Start with load, then size panels and battery backup. Agriculture: Move from fertilizer rate and NPK targets to product, bags and cost. Math and science: Use focused tools for percentage, equations, probability, geometry, density and motion.

Build a calculation workflow

Calcora is designed around connected jobs rather than isolated widgets. A visitor can calculate a solar load, estimate panel capacity, then check battery backup; or calculate a PSX average price, review trade P/L and then inspect portfolio allocation. This structure makes each result easier to understand and gives search engines clear topical relationships between related pages.
