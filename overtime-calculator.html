Frequently asked questions

How is overtime pay calculated?

Overtime pay is estimated by multiplying the base hourly rate by the overtime multiplier and overtime hours.

Are the results exact?

The result is an estimate based on the assumptions and inputs you provide. Employer, lender, tax and local rules may change actual amounts.

Can I change the assumptions?

Yes. Enter values that match your own pay, schedule or investment situation before calculating.

How overtime pay is estimated

Overtime pay is usually based on a regular hourly rate multiplied by an overtime multiplier and the number of overtime hours. The correct multiplier depends on the employment rules or contract that applies to the worker.

Example

If the regular rate is PKR 500, overtime is 4 hours and the multiplier is 1.5, the overtime amount is PKR 3,000 before any deductions.

Verify the applicable rule

Payroll rules can differ by jurisdiction, industry, contract and employment status. Treat the calculator as an estimate unless the rate and overtime conditions have been verified for the relevant situation.

Overtime FAQ

Is overtime always paid at 1.5×?

No. The multiplier is not universal. Use the rate required by the applicable employment rule or agreement.
