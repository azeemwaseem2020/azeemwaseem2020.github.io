Weight Calculator — Mass, Gravity & Weight

Calculate weight, mass or gravitational acceleration using W = mg, with reverse solving, units, Earth gravity and clear steps.

Formula

W = mg. Weight is a force caused by gravity, so its SI unit is newtons (N).

Worked example

A 60 kg mass at 9.80665 m/s² has weight ≈ 588.399 N.

Gravity varies

Weight depends on the local gravitational acceleration; mass does not. Use the appropriate g value for another planet or location.
