How to simplify a fraction

A fraction is in simplest form when the numerator and denominator have no common factor greater than 1. Divide both by their greatest common factor (GCF). The value of the fraction stays unchanged because the same factor is removed from both parts.

Worked example

For 18/24, the GCF is 6. Dividing numerator and denominator by 6 gives 3/4. The decimal value is unchanged because 18 ÷ 24 and 3 ÷ 4 are equivalent.

Practical use

Simplifying fractions is useful in school mathematics, recipes, ratios and engineering calculations. Keep the sign with the numerator or denominator consistently; the overall fraction should have only one negative sign.

FAQs

Can a fraction be simplified if the numerator is prime? Yes, if it shares a factor with the denominator. What about zero? 0 divided by a nonzero denominator simplifies to 0. Can the denominator be zero? No; division by zero is undefined.
