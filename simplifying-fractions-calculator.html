Fraction simplifier: complete guide

A fraction represents a number of equal parts. The numerator is the top number and the denominator is the bottom number; the denominator cannot be zero. A fraction is in simplest form when its numerator and denominator have no common positive factor greater than 1. Simplifying changes the appearance of a fraction, not its value.

Method and formula

Find the greatest common divisor (GCD) of the numerator and denominator, then divide both by that same non-zero GCD: simplified numerator = numerator ÷ GCD and simplified denominator = denominator ÷ GCD. The GCD is the largest positive integer that divides both numbers without a remainder.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter the numerator in the first field.
  2. Enter the denominator in the second field; it must not be zero.
  3. Select Calculate to see the reduced fraction.
  4. Check the sign: a negative numerator or denominator represents a negative fraction, and the negative sign can be placed in front.

Worked example

For 18/24, the greatest common divisor is 6. Divide both values by 6: 18 ÷ 6 = 3 and 24 ÷ 6 = 4. The simplified fraction is 3/4. It is equivalent to 18/24 because numerator and denominator were divided by the same factor. A fraction such as 7/9 is already in simplest form because 7 and 9 share no factor greater than 1.

Important cases and common mistakes

A denominator of zero is undefined and cannot be simplified into a valid fraction. A numerator of zero with a non-zero denominator simplifies to 0/1, or simply 0. Negative fractions are valid; keep the sign consistent when reducing. Do not divide the numerator and denominator by different numbers, because that changes the value. The calculator works with numeric inputs and may display a result differently from a mixed number; for example, 7/4 can also be written as 1 3/4, but they represent the same value.

Fraction simplification is useful for arithmetic, ratios, recipes, measurements and algebra. This tool reduces the fraction only; it does not automatically solve an equation or convert every fraction into a decimal or percentage.
