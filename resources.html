FOR PUBLISHERS & EDUCATORS

Citing Calcora in your own work

When a Calcora calculator is useful in an article, classroom note, project or internal document, cite the specific calculator page rather than the homepage. Include the calculator name, the Calcora page title and the date you accessed it.

If the result depends on a current tax rule, market figure, tariff or product specification, cite that underlying source as well. This keeps the calculation reproducible and separates Calcora's arithmetic from the external facts used as inputs.
