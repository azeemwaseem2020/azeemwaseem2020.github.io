Frequently asked questions

How is annual salary estimated from hourly pay?

The estimate multiplies hourly pay by hours per week and weeks per year. Change the assumptions to match your work schedule.

Are the results exact?

The result is an estimate based on the assumptions and inputs you provide. Employer, lender, tax and local rules may change actual amounts.

Can I change the assumptions?

Yes. Enter values that match your own pay, schedule or investment situation before calculating.

Calculation context

Hourly-to-salary estimates convert an hourly rate into annual, monthly or other period earnings using the work-hours and weeks supplied. Actual take-home pay can differ because of taxes, deductions and unpaid time.

Converting hourly pay to annual salary

An annual salary estimate can be calculated by multiplying hourly pay by paid hours per week and the number of paid weeks per year. If you work fewer or more hours, or have unpaid leave, the annual equivalent changes.

Example

At PKR 500 per hour for 40 hours each week and 52 paid weeks, the simple annual equivalent is PKR 1,040,000.

Compare total compensation

When comparing an hourly role with a salaried role, also consider overtime, bonuses, paid leave, insurance, retirement contributions and other benefits.

Hourly-to-salary FAQ

Does annual salary include overtime?

Not automatically. Overtime treatment depends on the job and employment terms, so keep it separate unless it is explicitly included in the calculation.
