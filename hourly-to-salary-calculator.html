Converting hourly pay to annual salary

Hourly pay becomes an annual estimate only after choosing assumptions about hours per week and working weeks per year. This is especially important when comparing employment with freelance or contract work, because paid leave, bonuses and employer benefits can make two headline figures non-equivalent.

Formula

Annual pay = hourly rate × hours per week × working weeks per year. Monthly estimate can then be approximated by annual pay ÷ 12, but actual monthly earnings may vary with payroll schedules.

Worked example

At Rs 750/hour, 40 hours per week and 52 weeks, annual gross pay is 750 × 40 × 52 = Rs 1,560,000. Dividing by 12 gives a simple monthly equivalent of Rs 130,000 before deductions.

Pakistan-specific example

A freelancer in Pakistan comparing a project rate with a salaried role should also account for unpaid downtime, software, internet, taxes, equipment and benefits. The mathematical annualization is useful, but it is not a full compensation comparison.

Common questions

Does this calculate take-home pay? No. It estimates gross pay.

Can I use 48 weeks? Yes, if that reflects the actual paid working year.

What about overtime? Add overtime separately if it is not included in the base hourly rate.

Is monthly pay exactly annual divided by 12? It is an average; payroll timing can differ.
