Friction Calculator — Friction Force, Coefficient & Normal Force

Calculate friction force, coefficient of friction or normal force using f = μN, with static/kinetic context, reverse solving and checks.

Formula

f = μN is the basic friction relationship for kinetic friction and for the maximum static friction threshold when μ represents the relevant coefficient.

Worked example

With μ = 0.30 and normal force 100 N, kinetic friction magnitude is 30 N.

Important static-friction note

Static friction is usually a range, not automatically μN. The maximum is fs,max = μsN; actual static friction adjusts to the applied conditions up to that limit.
