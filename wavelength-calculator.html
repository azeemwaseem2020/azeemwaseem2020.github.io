Wavelength formula

Wavelength is the distance between corresponding points on consecutive waves. For a wave with speed v and frequency f, λ = v/f.

Keep units consistent

If wave speed is measured in metres per second and frequency in hertz, the calculated wavelength is in metres. Convert units before calculating when necessary.
