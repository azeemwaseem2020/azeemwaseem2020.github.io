Wavelength formula

Wavelength is the distance between corresponding points on consecutive waves. For a wave with speed v and frequency f, λ = v/f.

Keep units consistent

If wave speed is measured in metres per second and frequency in hertz, the calculated wavelength is in metres. Convert units before calculating when necessary.

How wavelength is calculated

For a wave moving at speed v with frequency f, wavelength is λ = v ÷ f.

Worked example

A 340 m/s sound wave at 170 Hz has λ = 2 m.

A practical Pakistan example

Students working with sound or electromagnetic waves can use consistent SI units to compare wavelength, frequency and wave speed.

Important assumptions

Keep speed and frequency in compatible units and remember that the wave speed depends on the medium for many wave types.
