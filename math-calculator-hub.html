Math calculators for common questions

Use Calcora for practical questions such as “what is 20% of 150?”, “what is the percentage change?”, “how do I simplify a ratio?”, “what is the average?”, “how do I solve a linear or quadratic equation?”, “what is the probability?” or “how do I find the missing side of a right triangle?” Each calculator is designed around the specific values and method needed for that problem.

How to choose the right math calculator

Use Percentage for percent-of, percent-change and comparison questions. Use Ratio for proportional quantities. Use Average for arithmetic mean. Use Discount for sale-price calculations. Use Linear Equation or Quadratic Equation for algebra. Use Probability for simple chance calculations and Pythagorean Theorem for right-triangle side lengths.

Transparent calculation workflow

  1. Identify the quantity you need.
  2. Enter values carefully, including signs and units where applicable.
  3. Review the formula or method used.
  4. Check the result against a quick hand calculation when practical.
  5. Use the related guide when you need to understand the method rather than only obtain an answer.

Common math mistakes

  • Confusing percentage change with percentage points.
  • Comparing ratios without putting them on a common basis.
  • Using an average when a weighted average is actually required.
  • Forgetting that a quadratic equation can have two real roots, one real root or no real roots.
  • Using the Pythagorean theorem on a triangle that is not right-angled.
