Final grade calculator: complete guide

This calculator estimates the score needed on a remaining assessment to reach a target course grade. It is useful when an exam, project or final assignment has a known share of the total grade. The estimate depends on the current grade for completed work, how much of the course is already completed, and the target grade.

Weighted-grade formula

Target grade = current grade × completed weight + required final score × remaining weight.

Rearranging gives Required final score = (target grade − current grade × completed weight) ÷ remaining weight. Enter weights as decimals in the formula: 70% completed is 0.70 and a final worth 30% is 0.30. The calculator’s input convention should be followed carefully so the completed share and remaining share match the same course total.

How to use it

  1. Enter your current grade for the completed portion of the course.
  2. Enter the weight already completed and the weight of the remaining assessment.
  3. Enter the target overall course grade.
  4. Select Calculate and compare the required score with the assessment’s possible range.

Worked example

Suppose the current grade is 80%, completed work counts for 70% of the course, the final exam counts for 30%, and the target is 85%. Required final score = (85 − 80 × 0.70) ÷ 0.30 = (85 − 56) ÷ 0.30 = 96.67%. The result assumes the current grade accurately represents completed work and no other scores or weights change.

Interpreting the result

A required score from 0% to 100% may be mathematically attainable, but it is not a guarantee. A result above 100% means the target cannot be reached through that assessment alone under the inputs provided. A negative result means the target is already met mathematically even with a zero on the remaining assessment, although course rules may impose minimum exam requirements.

Common mistakes and FAQs

Check that completed and remaining weights reflect the course grading scheme and sum to the expected total. Some schools calculate grades by total points, weighted categories, dropped assignments or special exam rules. Extra credit, rounding, late penalties and minimum final-exam scores can change the official grade. If a teacher reports a current grade only for completed work, do not treat it as the grade for the entire course.

Can I use this for a weighted final exam?

Yes, when the current grade and assessment weights are known.

What if the required score is over 100%?

The target is not reachable from the remaining assessment alone under the assumptions entered; ask the instructor about grade policies and remaining opportunities.

Does this predict my official final grade?

No. It is a mathematical estimate and cannot account for unpublished grading rules or future changes to scores.
