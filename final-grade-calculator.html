Final Grade Calculator

Find the exam or assignment score you need to reach a target overall course grade.

Formula

Required final score = (target grade − current grade × (1 − final weight)) ÷ final weight.

How to read the result

If the required score is above 100%, the target cannot be reached with the stated final-exam weight alone. Check your grading weights and target.
