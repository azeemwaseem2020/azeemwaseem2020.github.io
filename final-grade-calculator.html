Find the exam or assignment score you need to reach a target overall course grade.

Formula

Required final score = (target grade − current grade × (1 − final weight)) ÷ final weight.

How to read the result

If the required score is above 100%, the target cannot be reached with the stated final-exam weight alone. Check your grading weights and target.

Final Grade Calculator

This calculator helps estimate the score you need on a remaining assessment to reach a target course grade. It is especially useful when assignments, quizzes, exams, or projects have different weights.

Weighted-grade formula

For a remaining assessment, the target can be solved from the weighted average: Target = Current grade × completed weight + Required score × remaining weight. The weights are expressed as decimals, such as 0.60 and 0.40.

Worked example

If your current grade is 80% across work worth 70% of the course and the final exam is worth 30%, a target of 85% requires a final-exam score of about 96.7%. This is an estimate based on the supplied weights and assumes no other grades change.

Use the result carefully

Check whether your school calculates grades by weighted categories, points earned, or another system. Extra credit, dropped assignments, minimum exam rules, and rounding can change the actual result.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use this for a weighted final exam?

Yes. Enter the completed portion and the final assessment weight rather than treating all assignments as equal.

What if my required score is above 100%?

That means the target grade is not reachable from the remaining assessment under the supplied assumptions.
