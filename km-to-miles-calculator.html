Formula and notes

This calculator provides a mathematical estimate based on the values entered. Real-world outcomes can differ.

Conversion formula

One kilometer equals approximately 0.6213711922 miles. The calculator divides the entered kilometer value by 1.609344. For example, 10 km is about 6.213712 miles.

Practical use

Use the result when comparing road distances, travel plans, running distances or measurements stated in metric and imperial units. The displayed value is a unit conversion, not a travel-time estimate.

Worked conversion

For 10 km, divide by 1.609344 to get approximately 6.213712 miles. For everyday travel or running, rounding to 6.21 miles may be sufficient; retain more decimals when the calculation is cumulative.

Distance versus travel time

This tool converts distance units only. Travel time also depends on speed, route, stops and traffic, so it should not be inferred from the converted distance alone.

Exact conversion factor

The international conversion is defined as 1 mile = 1.609344 kilometres. Because the factor is exact, differences between calculator displays usually come from rounding rather than from different conversion standards.

Rounding for practical use

For a road distance, two decimal places may be easier to read. For repeated calculations or technical work, keep more precision and round the final result. The conversion itself does not depend on the number of displayed decimal places.
