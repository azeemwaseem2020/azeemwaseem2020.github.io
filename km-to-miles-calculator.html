Converting kilometres to miles

Use miles = kilometres × 0.621371192. The reverse conversion is approximately kilometres = miles × 1.609344. Keeping several decimal places during the calculation reduces rounding error.

Worked example

A road distance of 100 km is 100 × 0.621371192 ≈ 62.14 miles. If a display only needs whole miles, this can be rounded to 62 miles.

A practical Pakistan example

When comparing a Pakistani road trip with an international route guide that uses miles, converting the distance allows the two descriptions to be compared directly without changing the underlying route.

Common mistakes

Do not divide by 0.62137 when converting kilometres to miles; multiplication by that factor is the direct conversion. For reverse conversion, use the reciprocal relationship.

FAQ

How many miles are in 1 km? About 0.621371 miles.

How many kilometres are in 1 mile? Exactly 1.609344 km.

Should travel distances be rounded? Use the precision appropriate for navigation, planning or reporting.

Conversion accuracy

One kilometre equals approximately 0.6213711922 miles. Therefore 100 km is about 62.1371 miles. For road-trip planning, rounding to one decimal mile may be adequate; for mapping or technical work, retain more precision. The conversion is linear, so doubling the kilometres doubles the miles.

Practical example: a 250 km journey is about 155.34 miles. Distance reported by a vehicle, GPS or map may differ slightly because of route geometry and rounding, not because the unit conversion is different.
