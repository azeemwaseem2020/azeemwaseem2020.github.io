Waist-to-Height Ratio Calculator

Calculate waist-to-height ratio from waist and height measurements. Use it as a screening metric, not a diagnosis.

How the ratio is calculated

Waist-to-height ratio is calculated by dividing waist circumference by height using the same units. With a waist of 85 cm and height of 175 cm, the ratio is about 0.486.

How to interpret the result

Waist-to-height ratio is a screening measure used in some health contexts. It should not be treated as a diagnosis or as a complete assessment of health. Measurement technique and individual circumstances can affect interpretation.
