Understanding the Wh ratio

A simple energy-to-capacity comparison can be expressed as Wh ÷ Ah = V when the voltage is appropriate for the battery system.

Worked example

A 12 V, 100 Ah battery has a nominal energy of about 1,200 Wh before efficiency and usable-depth considerations.

A practical Pakistan example

For a small backup system in Pakistan, this helps compare nominal battery capacity with an estimated load.

Important assumptions

Nominal Wh is not the same as guaranteed usable energy; efficiency, discharge limits, temperature and battery chemistry matter.
