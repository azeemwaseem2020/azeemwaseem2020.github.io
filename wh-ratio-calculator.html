Waist-to-Height Ratio Calculator

Calculate waist-to-height ratio from waist and height measurements. Use it as a screening metric, not a diagnosis.

How the ratio is calculated

Waist-to-height ratio is calculated by dividing waist circumference by height using the same units. With a waist of 85 cm and height of 175 cm, the ratio is about 0.486.

How to interpret the result

Waist-to-height ratio is a screening measure used in some health contexts. It should not be treated as a diagnosis or as a complete assessment of health. Measurement technique and individual circumstances can affect interpretation.

Waist-to-height ratio: formula and measurement guide

Waist-to-height ratio (WHtR) compares waist circumference with height. It is used as one screening indicator related to central body fat, but it is not a diagnosis and cannot describe overall health on its own.

Formula

WHtR = waist circumference ÷ height. Use the same units for both measurements. If waist is 85 cm and height is 175 cm, WHtR = 85 ÷ 175 ≈ 0.486. The ratio has no unit because the units cancel.

How to measure and use the calculator

  1. Measure height without shoes using a consistent technique.
  2. Measure waist circumference using a suitable tape and the measurement method recommended by your health professional or local health guidance.
  3. Enter both measurements in centimetres, as the fields specify.
  4. Calculate and record the result with the date and measurement method if tracking changes over time.

Interpretation and limitations

Different guidelines may use different screening thresholds and may interpret results differently for age, sex, pregnancy and certain health circumstances. Tape placement, posture, breathing and measurement technique can also affect the ratio. Do not use one number to diagnose a condition, judge your worth or make major diet or exercise changes without considering broader health context. A clinician can interpret it alongside blood pressure, laboratory tests, history and other measures.

Frequently asked questions

Does the ratio have a unit?

No. When waist and height use the same unit, division cancels the units and produces a ratio.

Can I enter inches instead of centimetres?

The displayed fields specify centimetres. Convert inches to centimetres first, or use a calculator that explicitly accepts inches, so the two measurements are compatible.

Does WHtR replace BMI or medical assessment?

No. It is one screening measure with limitations. BMI, waist measurement, medical history and other factors provide different information and should be interpreted in context.
