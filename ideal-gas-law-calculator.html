Ideal Gas Law Calculator

Calculate pressure, volume, amount of gas or temperature using PV = nRT.

Formula

PV = nRT, where P is pressure, V is volume, n is amount of gas, R is the gas constant and T is absolute temperature.

Worked example

At 1 mol, 101,325 Pa and 293.15 K, the ideal-gas equation gives V = nRT/P ≈ 0.0240 m³, or about 24.0 L.

How to use the ideal gas law calculator

  1. Select pressure, volume, amount or temperature as the unknown.
  2. Enter the other three values in SI units.
  3. Calculate and check the resulting unit.

Assumptions and units

Use consistent SI units unless the calculator explicitly says otherwise. Real physical systems can require additional effects beyond these introductory equations.
