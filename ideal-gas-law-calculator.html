Ideal Gas Law Calculator

Calculate pressure, volume, amount of gas or temperature using PV = nRT.

Formula

PV = nRT, where P is pressure, V is volume, n is amount of gas, R is the gas constant and T is absolute temperature.

Worked example

At 1 mol, 101,325 Pa and 293.15 K, the ideal-gas equation gives V = nRT/P ≈ 0.0240 m³, or about 24.0 L.

How to use the ideal gas law calculator

Using the ideal gas model

The ideal gas law relates pressure, volume, amount of gas and absolute temperature. It is a useful approximation when intermolecular effects are small enough to ignore.

Worked example

For 1 mol of gas at 101,325 Pa and 273.15 K, the ideal-gas model gives a volume of about 0.0224 m³.

Use absolute temperature

Temperature must be expressed in kelvin for the standard ideal-gas equation. Real gases can depart from the ideal model at high pressures or near condensation conditions.

Ideal-gas FAQ

Can Celsius be entered directly?

No. Convert Celsius to kelvin before using the standard ideal-gas relationship.
