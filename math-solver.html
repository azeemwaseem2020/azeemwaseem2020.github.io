6. Quadratic factor check

For a monic quadratic x² + bx + c, this tool checks integer factor pairs. If two integers p and q satisfy p + q = b and pq = c, the factorization is (x + p)(x + q).

Math Solver FAQ

Does the solver show steps?

For linear, quadratic and simultaneous equations it shows the key mathematical transformation or verification information. It is designed to make the calculation checkable rather than simply returning a number.

Can it solve every algebra problem?

No. The current solver supports common numeric expressions and selected equation types. Specialist symbolic problems should use the dedicated Calcora tools or a full computer algebra system.

Why can a quadratic have complex roots?

When the discriminant is negative, the quadratic has no real roots. The solver reports the corresponding complex pair instead of hiding that result.
