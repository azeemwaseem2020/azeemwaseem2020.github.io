OPTICS MODEL CHECK

The thin-lens equation is an idealized optical model

The thin-lens relationship connects focal length, object distance and image distance. Its sign convention and interpretation depend on the convention used by the problem.

Sign convention

Do not mix sign conventions between sources. Object distance, image distance and focal length must follow the same convention throughout the calculation.

Thin-lens boundary

Real lenses have thickness, aberrations and manufacturing limits. The thin-lens model is an approximation rather than a complete optical simulation.

Magnification connection

Image size and orientation can be interpreted alongside image distance and object distance; a distance result alone does not describe the complete image.
