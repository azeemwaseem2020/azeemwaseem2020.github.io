How to use the payback period calculator

Enter the initial investment and expected cash flows. The calculator compares cumulative cash flow with the starting cost to estimate when the investment is recovered.

Payback questions

Use it to compare recovery time between projects. A payback period does not by itself measure total profitability or the time value of money.
