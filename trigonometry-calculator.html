Trigonometry formulas

For an angle θ in a right triangle, sine = opposite ÷ hypotenuse, cosine = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse, and tangent = opposite ÷ adjacent. The identity tan(θ) = sin(θ) ÷ cos(θ) also explains why tangent is undefined when cosine is zero.

Degrees and radians

Degrees and radians are two ways to measure angles. This calculator accepts either. For conversion, radians = degrees × π ÷ 180.

Special-angle examples

At 30°, sin = 0.5, cos ≈ 0.866025 and tan ≈ 0.577350. At 45°, sin and cos are both approximately 0.707107 and tan = 1. At 60°, sin ≈ 0.866025, cos = 0.5 and tan ≈ 1.732051.

Where trigonometry is used

Trigonometric functions are used in geometry, surveying, physics, engineering, navigation, waves, signal processing and coordinate calculations.

FAQ

Why can tangent be undefined? Because tan = sin/cos and division by zero is undefined.

Does this calculator use degrees? Yes. You can switch between degrees and radians.

Are inverse trig functions the same as reciprocal functions? No. asin, acos and atan are inverse functions; 1/sin, 1/cos and 1/tan are reciprocals.

Formula and calculation method

The calculator converts a degree input to radians when needed, then evaluates sin(θ), cos(θ), and tan(θ) = sin(θ) ÷ cos(θ). The tangent is undefined where cosine is zero, such as 90° + k×180° for integer k.

Practical example: 30 degrees

At 30°, sin(30°) = 0.5, cos(30°) ≈ 0.866025, and tan(30°) ≈ 0.577350. Small display differences are caused by rounding.

Frequently asked questions

Should I use degrees or radians?

Use the unit specified by your problem or software. Degrees use 360 units per full turn; radians use 2π radians per full turn.

Why is tangent sometimes undefined?

Tangent divides sine by cosine. When cosine is zero, the division is undefined.

Are inverse sine and cosecant the same?

No. Inverse sine returns an angle from a sine value; cosecant is the reciprocal 1/sin(θ).
