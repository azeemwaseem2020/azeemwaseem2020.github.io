Personal Loan Calculator: Monthly Payment, Interest and Total Cost

A personal-loan estimate helps compare a proposed loan amount, annual interest rate and repayment term before applying.

Monthly repayment formula

For a fixed-rate loan with equal monthly payments, payment M = P × r × (1+r)^n ÷ [(1+r)^n − 1], where P is principal, r is the monthly rate (annual nominal rate divided by 12), and n is the total number of monthly payments. If the rate is zero, payment is P ÷ n.

Worked example

For a hypothetical PKR 500,000 loan at 12% annual interest over five years, r = 0.12 ÷ 12 = 0.01 and n = 60. The standard amortizing payment is approximately PKR 11,122 per month before fees or other charges.

How to use the calculator

  1. Enter the amount you plan to borrow, not the total you hope to repay.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate quoted by the provider.
  3. Choose the repayment term and check that years and months are interpreted correctly.
  4. Calculate and review monthly payment, total repayment and interest together.
  5. Repeat with another rate or term to compare affordability, keeping other assumptions constant.

Useful features and decision checks

Comparing multiple terms shows the trade-off between payment size and total cost. A longer term commonly lowers each instalment but increases the time interest can accrue. A shorter term often costs less in total interest but requires a larger monthly commitment.

Fees, rate types and limitations

The standard formula assumes a fixed rate, regular monthly payments and no additional fees rolled into the balance. Processing charges, insurance or takaful, late fees, variable rates, early-settlement rules and payment timing can change the actual cost. Flat-rate offers are not directly comparable with reducing-balance rates by looking at the headline percentage alone. Ask the provider for the full repayment schedule and total payable amount.

For Pakistan-based borrowing, confirm current eligibility, documentation, pricing and product terms directly with the bank or licensed provider. This calculator does not assess credit approval, recommend a lender, or provide a religious ruling on a financing structure. Treat the result as an estimate and verify the contract before committing.

Frequently asked questions

Does a longer loan term always save money?

It may lower the monthly instalment, but usually increases total interest under a positive fixed rate.

Are fees included in the estimate?

Only if the calculator explicitly asks for them; otherwise compare the estimate with the lender’s written schedule.

What is the difference between flat and reducing-balance rates?

A flat rate typically calculates interest against the original principal, while reducing-balance interest applies to the outstanding balance; compare total payable amounts, not just headline rates.
