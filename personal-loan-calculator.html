Personal loan calculator Pakistan

This calculator can be used with a Pakistani loan offer by entering the amount, annual rate and repayment term in the lender's stated units. Compare monthly payment and total repayment rather than looking only at the advertised rate.

Actual Pakistani personal-loan products can differ in fees, variable rates, eligibility and repayment rules. Calcora does not publish invented bank rates; use the lender's current offer as the source for those inputs.
