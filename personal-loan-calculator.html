What this personal-loan estimate includes

The calculator assumes a fixed rate and equal monthly payments. Actual lender fees, insurance, taxes or variable rates can change the final cost.

Personal loan repayment estimate

A personal-loan payment depends on principal, interest rate, term and payment frequency. For a conventional fixed-rate reducing balance, the periodic payment follows the standard amortization formula. A quoted annual rate must be converted to the payment period before calculating.

Worked example

For a PKR 500,000 loan at 18% annual interest over 3 years with monthly payments, the monthly rate is 0.18/12 and there are 36 payments. The calculator uses those values to estimate the scheduled payment and total interest.

Pakistan context

Compare processing fees, insurance, early-settlement charges and whether the rate is fixed or variable. A bank's advertised rate is not necessarily the same as the total cost of borrowing. For Islamic financing, use the institution's stated structure rather than assuming conventional interest applies.

FAQs

Does a longer term reduce monthly payment? Usually, but total financing cost can rise. Are fees included? Only if entered or modeled. Can rates change? Yes for variable-rate products.
