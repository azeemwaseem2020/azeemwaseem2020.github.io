ABOUT CALCORA

Calculators should show their working.

Calcora is a focused calculator website built around practical calculations and the explanations that make those results easier to check. The aim is simple: help people get a useful number quickly while keeping the formula, units and assumptions visible.

Keep the input focused

A good calculator should not make a simple question feel like a form-filling exercise. We try to keep each tool centred on the values that actually affect its calculation and show the units next to those inputs.

Make the result inspectable

Where a formula, conversion or assumption changes the answer, the page explains it. This also makes it easier to spot when a calculator is not the right fit for a particular real-world situation.

Build for everyday devices

The site is made as a lightweight static website, with responsive layouts and straightforward navigation so the calculators remain usable on phones, tablets and desktop screens.

What Calcora covers

The current tools cover loan and compound-interest calculations, PSX share calculations, basic solar planning, TMR feed batching and fertilizer estimates, alongside growing general mathematics coverage. Supporting guides add worked examples and context around those calculations.

How we approach accuracy

  1. Formula first: each calculator should state the main formula or calculation method used.
  2. Units matter: inputs and outputs should make units explicit so avoidable conversion errors are easier to catch.
  3. Worked checks: important tools are reviewed against hand calculations or known examples before release.
  4. Real-world boundary: calculators do arithmetic from defined inputs; current prices, taxes, market rules, weather, equipment specifications, soil conditions and other changing factors must be verified separately.
  5. Source hierarchy: for changing facts, Calcora prioritizes official, regulatory, manufacturer, university/extension and other primary technical sources where appropriate.
  6. Corrections welcome: reported errors are investigated rather than silently ignored.

Who Calcora is for

Calcora is built for people who want to check a calculation themselves: a borrower comparing repayment scenarios, a household planning electricity use, an investor checking a share calculation, a farmer estimating inputs, or a student working through a maths or science problem. The site is designed to make the arithmetic visible rather than ask the reader to accept a result without context.

Editorial and source policy

Guides are written to explain the calculation, not to manufacture certainty. When a topic depends on current regulations, market data, product specifications or agricultural recommendations, Calcora aims to distinguish sourced facts from calculator assumptions and direct readers toward appropriate current or authoritative sources.

Transparency and methodology

Calcora publishes a dedicated calculator methodology covering formula checks, test examples, changing information, source hierarchy and correction reporting.

How the site is maintained

Calcora is maintained as a focused web project. Calculator changes are intended to be checked against formulas or known examples, while changing external information is kept separate from the mathematical calculation wherever possible. If you find an incorrect result, outdated assumption or broken link, please report it through the contact page with the inputs and result so it can be investigated.

Found something wrong? If a result looks incorrect, a link is broken, or an important assumption is missing, send the details. Including the inputs and the result makes it much easier to investigate.
