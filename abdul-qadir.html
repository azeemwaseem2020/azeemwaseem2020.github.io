Role: Founder & Maintainer · Website: Calcora · Corrections: Contact Calcora

Editorial standard: Calcora aims to make calculations understandable and checkable. Pages identify important assumptions, explain the method and distinguish mathematical results from changing real-world information.

What I do on Calcora

  • Maintain calculator implementation and user-facing tools.
  • Check formulas, units, inputs, boundary cases and representative examples.
  • Maintain explanations so users can understand what a result means and what it does not mean.
  • Review pages for outdated assumptions, broken links, unclear wording and calculation issues.

How calculator work is checked

The review process focuses on the actual calculation: define the inputs and units, implement the formula, test representative examples, check boundary cases, compare the output with an independent calculation, and document assumptions that can change outside the calculator. For pages involving tax, finance, agriculture, markets or equipment sizing, changing external facts are treated separately from the arithmetic.

Editorial independence

Calcora is an independent calculator website. It is not a bank, government department, university or financial institution, and it does not claim professional licences or institutional affiliations that it does not hold.

Corrections

If you find a calculation error, outdated fact or broken link, please contact Calcora with the calculator name, inputs and the reason you believe the result is incorrect.
