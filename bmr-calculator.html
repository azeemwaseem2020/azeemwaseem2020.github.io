Basal metabolic rate

Search intent covered

BMR calculator, basal metabolic rate calculator, BMR formula, calorie metabolism calculator

Formula & assumptions

Mifflin–St Jeor: BMR = 10W + 6.25H − 5A + S, where S is +5 for men and −161 for women.

Worked example

For a 30-year-old man weighing 70 kg and 175 cm: 10(70) + 6.25(175) − 5(30) + 5 = about 1,649 kcal/day.

Results are estimates; activity, body composition and individual physiology affect actual energy use.

Frequently asked questions

What is BMR?

Basal metabolic rate is an estimate of the energy the body uses at rest to support basic physiological functions.

Is BMR the same as daily calorie needs?

No. Daily energy expenditure also includes physical activity, digestion and other energy use.

Which formula does the calculator use?

The calculator applies the formula described on the page using the supplied body measurements and demographic inputs.

Quick guide: how to use this calculator

Enter age, sex, height and weight using the units shown. The result estimates resting energy needs before activity is added.

Formula or method

Mifflin–St Jeor: BMR = 10W + 6.25H − 5A + S, where W is kg, H is cm, A is years and S is +5 for men or −161 for women.

Useful note

BMR is an estimate of resting energy expenditure; daily energy needs also depend on activity and other individual factors.

Before you use the result

The answer depends on the values, units and assumptions entered. Check the method shown on this page before comparing the result with another calculator or source.

How BMR is estimated

This calculator estimates basal metabolic rate with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation using age, sex, height and weight. BMR is an estimate of the energy the body uses at rest; it is not the same as total daily calorie needs.

Reading the result

The BMR figure provides a starting point for estimating daily energy requirements. Actual energy use varies with activity, body composition, health and other factors, so the number should be treated as an estimate.

Before using it for a diet plan

Use consistent units and accurate measurements. If you are changing your diet for a medical condition, pregnancy, an eating disorder or another health concern, use advice from a qualified health professional.
