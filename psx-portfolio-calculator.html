PORTFOLIO RECONCILIATION

Build the portfolio from records, not estimates

A useful PSX portfolio result starts with a consistent record for every holding: shares, acquisition cost, current/reference price and any income or charges you want included. Keep realised trades separate from unrealised positions so the portfolio total does not mix two different measures.

Cost basis

Use the acquisition quantity and cost basis from your own trade records. If you have multiple purchases, reconcile the lots or use the average-price calculation before entering a simplified holding.

Reference price

Enter a price from your chosen market source or broker record. Calcora does not claim that an entered price is a live PSX quote.

Income

Keep dividend assumptions separate from price appreciation. A portfolio return can be interpreted differently depending on whether cash income is included.

Charges & tax

Do not assume one universal brokerage or tax rate. Current charges and applicable tax treatment should be verified against the broker statement and official guidance.

How to calculate PSX portfolio value

For each holding, current value equals shares multiplied by current price. Total portfolio value is the sum of all entered holdings. Invested cost is shares multiplied by average cost for each holding.

PSX portfolio profit or loss and return

Unrealised profit or loss = current portfolio value − total invested cost. Portfolio return = unrealised profit or loss ÷ total invested cost × 100. These are scenario calculations based on the figures you enter.

PSX investment portfolio allocation

Holding allocation = holding current value ÷ total portfolio value × 100. Allocation helps you see whether one PSX security represents a large share of the portfolio and could therefore have a larger effect on the overall result.

PSX dividend income and yield

Estimated annual dividend income = shares × dividend per share, summed across holdings. Dividend yield on cost = estimated dividend income ÷ total invested cost × 100. Check the dividend period and current company announcements before comparing yields.

Why PSX transaction costs and taxes matter

The portfolio calculator focuses on the arithmetic of the entered holdings. Brokerage, taxes, CDC-related charges and other costs can affect the actual realised return. Use your broker's current tariff and contract notes when calculating a completed transaction.

Current prices and data limitations

Calcora intentionally does not invent live market prices. Enter a verified current or valuation price from your broker or an authoritative market source. Corporate actions, dividend declarations, taxes and charges can change the final outcome.

Who should use a PSX portfolio calculator?

This is handy when you have several PSX holdings and want a quick snapshot without opening a spreadsheet. Enter the figures from your own records or broker statement, then compare the results with your actual account.

Common questions

Can I calculate more than one PSX holding?

Yes. Add your holdings one by one and the calculator combines their cost, current value, profit or loss and allocation.

Does it use live PSX prices?

No. Enter a current price you have verified yourself. This keeps the calculator transparent instead of presenting an unverified live price.

Does portfolio return include every trading cost?

No. Brokerage, taxes and other charges can change the final realised return, so use your broker's records for a completed trade.

EXPERT VALIDATION LAYER

Portfolio snapshot discipline

Use holdings and prices from the same valuation point where possible. The calculator is a user-entered snapshot, so its output should not be presented as a live PSX portfolio valuation.

What the calculator proves

Allocation, unrealised P/L and dividend assumptions answer different portfolio questions and should be interpreted separately.

What must be verified externally

Current PSX information, broker charges, transaction records, corporate actions and applicable tax or regulatory treatment are external inputs and can change.

Professional use principle: distinguish a reproducible calculation from the source data and rules used to interpret it.

PORTFOLIO DATA BOUNDARY

What this portfolio calculator measures — and what it does not

The calculator combines holdings that you enter. It does not fetch live PSX prices, infer missing corporate actions or value an investment from a market feed.

Cost basis

Enter the purchase quantity and cost per share for each holding. The tool totals those inputs into invested cost.

Valuation snapshot

Enter the current/reference price you want to analyze. The result is only as current as that price.

Allocation

Allocation is calculated from each holding's entered value divided by the total entered portfolio value.

Income scenario

Dividend income is an entered assumption per share, not a prediction of future distributions.

For live prices or corporate-action details, use current broker/PSX records and then enter the verified figures here.

PORTFOLIO SOURCE CHECK

Keep the portfolio snapshot tied to a date

A portfolio calculation is a snapshot of the quantities, costs, prices and dividend assumptions you enter. Record the date or source of those figures when using the result for portfolio review.
