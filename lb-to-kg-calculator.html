Conversion formula

One pound equals exactly 0.45359237 kilograms. The calculator divides the entered pound value by 2.2046226218 and displays the result in kilograms. For example, 10 lb is about 4.535924 kg.

Practical use

Use this conversion when comparing product weights, luggage limits, body-weight records, recipes or measurements given in different unit systems. Keep the original unit alongside the converted value when accuracy matters.
