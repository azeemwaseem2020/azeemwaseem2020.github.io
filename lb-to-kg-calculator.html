Rounding and practical limits

For luggage or shipping limits, check whether the provider states a maximum in pounds or kilograms and follow its rounding policy. A rounded conversion can sit just above or below a strict limit, so keep the unrounded result until the final comparison. Mass units and everyday language

In everyday use, pounds are often described as weight, while kilograms are units of mass. This page converts mass values using the standard international factor; it does not calculate force or adjust for gravity.

Pounds to kilograms: formula and checked examples

One international avoirdupois pound equals exactly 0.45359237 kilograms. To convert pounds to kilograms, use kilograms = pounds × 0.45359237. For a quick mental estimate, halve the pound value and subtract roughly 10%, but use the exact factor for records, shipping or technical work. The reverse conversion is kilograms ÷ 0.45359237, or kilograms × 2.2046226218.

Worked examples

  • 10 lb: 10 × 0.45359237 = 4.5359237 kg, approximately 4.54 kg.
  • 150 lb: approximately 68.0389 kg.
  • 200 lb: approximately 90.7185 kg.

Use sensible rounding

For a person’s body weight, one decimal place may be sufficient for an everyday conversion; for a package label, follow the carrier’s precision and rounding rules. Keep the unrounded value during multi-step calculations and round at the end. A converted number should not imply greater measurement accuracy than the original scale or measuring device provides.

Mass, weight and context

In everyday use, pounds and kilograms are commonly used for body weight and product mass. In physics, mass and force are different quantities: kilograms measure mass, while newtons measure force. A conversion between lb and kg is a unit conversion, not an assessment of health, fitness or shipping eligibility. For freight, confirm whether a carrier asks for actual mass, dimensional weight or a particular packaging unit.

Frequently asked questions

How many kilograms are 150 pounds?

150 lb is approximately 68.04 kg.

Should I multiply or divide?

Multiply pounds by 0.45359237 to get kilograms. Divide kilograms by that factor to get pounds.

Why can a result differ by a few hundredths?

Different displays round to different decimal places. The underlying conversion factor remains the same.
