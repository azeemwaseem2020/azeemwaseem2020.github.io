Frequently Asked Questions

Which statistic should I use? Mean describes the average, while median is often more resistant to extreme values.

Why check the dataset? Missing or non-numeric values can change the result or make a statistic invalid.

What these statistics mean

The calculator can summarize a data set with measures such as mean, median, minimum, maximum, range and standard deviation.

Mean vs median

The mean uses every value and can be pulled by unusually large or small observations. The median is the middle value after sorting and can be more representative when a distribution is skewed.

Standard deviation

Standard deviation describes the spread of values around the mean. The interpretation depends on whether your data represents a complete population or a sample, so use the appropriate convention for your analysis.
