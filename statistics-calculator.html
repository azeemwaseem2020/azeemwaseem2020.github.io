Formula & assumptions

Mean = sum ÷ N. After sorting the values, the median is the middle value, or the average of the two middle values when N is even. Range = maximum − minimum. Population variance divides the sum of squared differences from the mean by N; population standard deviation is the square root of population variance. Sample variance instead divides by N − 1 and is often used to estimate variability in a larger population from a sample.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter at least one finite number, separating values with commas, spaces or new lines.
  2. Check that every intended value is numeric. The calculator rejects malformed entries instead of silently ignoring them.
  3. Select Calculate Statistics and compare the mean and median with the minimum, maximum and spread.

Worked example

For the dataset 2, 4, 4, 6, the count is 4 and the sum is 16, so the mean is 16 ÷ 4 = 4. The middle values are 4 and 4, so the median is 4. The range is 6 − 2 = 4. Squared deviations from the mean are 4, 0, 0 and 4; their sum is 8. Population variance is 8 ÷ 4 = 2 and population standard deviation is √2 ≈ 1.414.

Choosing the right statistic

The mean uses every value but can be pulled by an unusually large or small observation. The median is based on the middle position and is more resistant to extreme values. The range is easy to interpret but depends only on the two endpoints. Standard deviation describes typical spread around the mean, in the same units as the original measurements.

Population vs sample standard deviation

Use population standard deviation when the entered values are the complete group you want to describe. If the values are a sample intended to represent a larger group, sample standard deviation uses N − 1 in the variance denominator. This page reports population standard deviation and sample standard deviation when at least two values are entered; neither statistic by itself proves that a sample is representative.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which statistic should I use?

Mean describes the arithmetic average, while median is often more resistant to extreme values. Review both if the dataset contains outliers.

What happens if I enter a word or an empty value?

The calculator asks you to correct non-numeric entries rather than silently dropping them, which helps prevent a misleading result.

What is the difference between population and sample standard deviation?

Population variance divides by N; sample variance divides by N − 1 and requires at least two observations.

Does the order of values matter?

No for these descriptive statistics. The calculator sorts a copy of the dataset to find the median, while the mean and variance depend on the values rather than their order.

Can these results prove a conclusion about a wider population?

No. Descriptive statistics summarize the entered data; inference requires appropriate sampling, study design and additional methods.
