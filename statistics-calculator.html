Frequently Asked Questions

Which statistic should I use? Mean describes the average, while median is often more resistant to extreme values.

Why check the dataset? Missing or non-numeric values can change the result or make a statistic invalid.

Understanding the result

Descriptive statistics turn a list of observations into a small set of numbers that is easier to inspect. The mean is the arithmetic average, while the median is the middle value after sorting the data. The range shows the distance between the smallest and largest observations. Calcora also reports population variance and population standard deviation, so the denominator is the full number of observations, N. If your dataset is a sample drawn from a larger population, a sample variance using N−1 may be more appropriate and should be calculated separately.

Formula

Mean = Σx ÷ N. Population variance = Σ(x − mean)² ÷ N. Population standard deviation = √variance. Median is the central ordered value; with an even number of observations it is the average of the two central values.

Worked example

Consider daily milk yields of 10, 12, 15, 18 and 20 litres. The mean is 75 ÷ 5 = 15 L. The median is also 15 L. The range is 20 − 10 = 10 L. These figures tell a different story than the mean alone: the observations span ten litres, so a single average should not be treated as the typical value in every situation.

Pakistan-specific example

A dairy farmer in Punjab could enter five days of recorded milk yield after keeping the same measurement routine. Comparing the median and range can reveal whether an unusually high or low day is pulling the average away from the normal production level. For farm records, keep the unit consistent and record the same milking period each day.

Common questions

Mean or median? Use the mean when every observation should contribute proportionally; median is often more resistant to extreme values.

Does standard deviation show good or bad performance? No. It measures spread, not quality.

Why can results differ from another statistics tool? Check whether that tool uses sample or population variance and how it handles missing values.

Can I mix units? No. Convert measurements to one compatible unit before analysis.
