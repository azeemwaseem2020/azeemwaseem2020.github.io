This tool answers searches such as days between two dates, how many days until a date, date after 30 days, date before 90 days and add days to a date.

How the calculation works

The difference is calculated from the calendar dates using UTC day boundaries to avoid daylight-saving clock changes affecting the day count. Adding or subtracting days uses calendar-date arithmetic.

Inclusive vs exclusive counting

The primary difference reports elapsed calendar days between the two dates. If you need to count both the start and end dates as whole days, add one to the absolute difference.
