Filer vs Non-Filer Tax Rates in Pakistan (2026–27)

Your Active Taxpayer List (ATL) status can change the advance tax deducted on transactions such as buying or selling property, profit on bank deposits and some cash withdrawals. Compare the transaction first, then confirm the exact rate and eligibility conditions in the current FBR rate card.

Filer, non-filer and ATL: what is the difference?

In everyday usage, “filer” usually means someone who has submitted an income-tax return. For many withholding provisions, however, the operative question is whether the person is included in the relevant Active Taxpayer List (ATL) and meets the conditions for that transaction. A return submission and an up-to-date ATL status should not be assumed to be identical in every case.

How to check the right rate

  1. Identify the transaction: bank profit, property purchase, property sale or another activity.
  2. Check the relevant section and taxpayer category in the current FBR Tax Year 2027 rate card.
  3. Confirm the transaction amount, threshold and any special rule before calculating the deduction.
  4. Keep the deduction receipt; ask the bank, registrar or tax professional whether it is adjustable against final liability or treated differently under the applicable law.
