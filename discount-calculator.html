Discount Calculator

Calculate the discount amount, money saved and final sale price from an original price and discount percentage.

How to calculate a discount

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage and divide by 100. Subtract the discount amount from the original price to get the sale price.

Discount formula

Discount amount = Original price × Discount rate ÷ 100

Sale price = Original price − Discount amount

Worked example

For a price of 10,000 with a 20% discount, the saving is 2,000 and the final sale price is 8,000.

Where a discount calculator helps

Use it for sale prices, shopping discounts, marked-down products, promotional offers and quick price comparisons. If tax, shipping or another fee is added after the discount, calculate that additional amount separately.

Discount vs percentage change

A discount reduces an original price by a stated percentage. Percentage change compares an old value with a new value, so the two calculations answer different questions.

Common mistakes

  • Applying the percentage to the wrong price.
  • Confusing the discount amount with the final sale price.
  • Forgetting that a second discount may be applied to the already-reduced price rather than the original price.

What this calculator does

This tool calculates one discount percentage from an original price. It does not calculate stacked discounts, tax-inclusive pricing or shipping charges automatically.

Frequently asked questions

How is a discount calculated?

Discount amount = original price × discount rate ÷ 100. The sale price is the original price minus the discount amount.

What is the difference between discount amount and final price?

The discount amount is how much is reduced from the original price. The final price is what remains after that reduction.

Can I calculate a percentage discount on any currency?

Yes. The arithmetic works with any currency as long as the original price and discount percentage use the same basis.
