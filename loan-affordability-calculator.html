Loan affordability formula

For a standard amortizing loan, the affordable principal is the inverse of the EMI relationship: P = M[1 − (1+r)−n] / r, where M is the monthly payment budget, r is the monthly rate and n is the number of payments. At zero interest, P = M × n.

Worked example

With a PKR 30,000 monthly budget, 10% annual rate and 5-year term, the calculator estimates the principal supported by 60 scheduled payments. Actual lender affordability can differ because fees, income, existing debt and lender rules are separate factors.

What does loan affordability mean?

Loan affordability is the amount you could theoretically finance while keeping the scheduled payment at or below a chosen monthly budget. This calculator reverses the standard EMI formula instead of assuming that a larger approved loan is automatically affordable.

How the calculation works

For a standard amortizing loan, the monthly rate is the annual rate divided by 12 and the number of payments is years multiplied by 12. For a non-zero rate, principal is estimated from the payment using the inverse EMI relationship. At zero rate, principal equals monthly budget multiplied by the number of payments.

Important costs not included

  • Processing and documentation charges
  • Insurance or takaful
  • Taxes and government charges
  • Variable-rate changes
  • Early settlement or late-payment charges
  • Lender-specific affordability and debt-service rules

Example

If your chosen payment ceiling is PKR 30,000 per month, with a 10% annual rate over five years, the calculator estimates the principal that would produce that payment under the stated mathematical assumptions. Compare that amount with your actual income and existing commitments before considering any borrowing.

Common mistakes

  • Using the maximum theoretical payment as your entire household borrowing budget.
  • Ignoring fees, insurance, taxes or variable-rate changes.
  • Assuming calculator affordability equals lender approval.
  • Comparing loans with different terms using only the monthly payment.

What this calculator does

This tool estimates principal from a monthly payment, annual rate and term using a standard reducing-balance model. It does not assess credit eligibility, income verification, debt-service ratios or lender-specific approval rules.
