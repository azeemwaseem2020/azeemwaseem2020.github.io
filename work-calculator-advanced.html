Advanced work calculations

Use this page when a work problem requires attention to force direction, displacement or reverse solving rather than a simple multiplication.

Work relationship

For constant force, mechanical work is W = Fd cosθ. The angle determines how much of the force acts along the displacement.

For a direct calculation, use the Work Calculator.

Work with force at an angle

For a constant force and displacement, mechanical work depends on the component of force parallel to the displacement. The angle between the two vectors therefore matters.

Worked example

A 100 N force acting through a 5 m displacement at 60° to the direction of motion does 250 J of work.

Interpreting the sign

Positive work transfers energy into the object's motion, negative work removes mechanical energy from it, and perpendicular force contributes zero work in the ideal constant-force model.

Advanced work FAQ

What if force changes along the path?

A changing force generally requires integration or a suitable numerical method rather than the single constant-force expression.
