Projectile Motion Calculator — Range, Maximum Height & Flight Time

Solve ideal projectile motion from launch speed and angle when launch and landing heights are equal.

Formulas

  • Range: R = v₀² sin(2θ) ÷ g
  • Maximum height: H = v₀² sin²θ ÷ (2g)
  • Flight time: T = 2v₀ sinθ ÷ g

Worked example

For v₀ = 20 m/s and θ = 45°, the ideal range is about 40.77 m using g = 9.80665 m/s².

Assumptions

Launch and landing heights are equal, acceleration is constant downward, and air resistance, wind and spin are ignored. For a different landing height, use a full projectile-motion model rather than these simplified equations.
