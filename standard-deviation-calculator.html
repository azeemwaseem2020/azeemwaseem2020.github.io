Standard Deviation Calculator

Calculate mean, variance and standard deviation for a list of numbers using either the population or sample method.

Population vs sample

Population standard deviation divides the squared deviations by N. Sample standard deviation uses N − 1 and is commonly used when the data represents a sample from a larger population.
