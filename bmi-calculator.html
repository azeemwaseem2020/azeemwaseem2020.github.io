Adult BMI Calculator

Calculate body mass index from height and weight and view the standard adult BMI category ranges.

Body mass index

BMI formula

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². Convert height from centimetres to metres before calculating.

Worked example

For 70 kg and 1.75 m: 70 ÷ (1.75 × 1.75) = 22.9.

Adult BMI categories

For adults aged 20 and older, standard categories are: underweight below 18.5, healthy weight 18.5 to less than 25, overweight 25 to less than 30, and obesity 30 or higher.

What BMI can and cannot tell you

BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis. It does not directly measure body fat and cannot distinguish fat from muscle or bone. A healthcare professional may consider BMI alongside medical history, physical findings and other health measures.

Important health note

This calculator is for general informational use and is designed for adults. Do not use these adult categories for children or teenagers; their BMI is interpreted using age- and sex-specific growth references. Pregnancy also requires different clinical interpretation.

Frequently asked questions

What is BMI?

BMI is a screening measure calculated from weight and height. It is commonly used to categorize adult weight ranges at a population level.

Is BMI a medical diagnosis?

No. BMI is a screening measure and does not directly measure body fat, muscle mass or overall health.

Who is this BMI calculator for?

This calculator uses standard adult BMI categories. Children and teenagers need age- and sex-specific BMI-for-age assessment.
