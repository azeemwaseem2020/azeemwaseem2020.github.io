Frequently asked questions

What is BMI?

BMI is a screening measure calculated from weight and height. It is commonly used to categorize weight ranges at a population level.

Is BMI a medical diagnosis?

No. BMI is a screening measure and does not directly measure body fat, muscle mass or overall health.

Can the same BMI ranges apply to everyone?

Interpretation can vary by age, sex, population and clinical context. This calculator is for general informational use.

How BMI is calculated

Enter height and weight using the units shown by the calculator. BMI is calculated as weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared: BMI = kg ÷ m².

Understanding the result

For adults, the calculator can show the standard BMI range associated with the calculated value. BMI is a screening measure rather than a diagnosis and does not directly measure body fat, muscle mass or overall health.

Use the number in context

Height, weight, age, muscle mass and other health factors can affect how a BMI result should be interpreted. For children, pregnancy, athletes or medical decisions, use the appropriate professional guidance rather than relying on an adult BMI category alone.
