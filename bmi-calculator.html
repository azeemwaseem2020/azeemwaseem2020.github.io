Body mass index

BMI formula

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². Convert height from centimetres to metres before calculating.

Worked example

For 70 kg and 1.75 m: 70 ÷ (1.75 × 1.75) = 22.9.

Formula and interpretation

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². This is a screening measure for adults, not a diagnosis, and it does not directly measure body fat or account for differences in muscle mass or body-fat distribution.

Frequently asked questions

What is BMI?

BMI is a screening measure calculated from weight and height. It is commonly used to categorize weight ranges at a population level.

Is BMI a medical diagnosis?

No. BMI is a screening measure and does not directly measure body fat, muscle mass or overall health.

Can the same BMI ranges apply to everyone?

Interpretation can vary by age, sex, population and clinical context. This calculator is for general informational use.
