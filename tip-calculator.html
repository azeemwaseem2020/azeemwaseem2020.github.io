How the tip calculation works

A tip is a percentage of a bill, but the correct base can depend on the receipt and local practice. This calculator performs a transparent percentage calculation and then adds the tip to the bill. It can also divide the total equally among a group. It does not decide what percentage a person should pay, whether a service charge is already included, or whether tax should be included in the tip base.

Formula

Tip amount = bill × (tip percentage ÷ 100). Total = bill + tip. Per-person share = total ÷ number of people. The same arithmetic can be used for service or hospitality calculations where a percentage-based addition is appropriate.

Worked example

For a Rs 5,000 bill and a 10% tip, the tip is Rs 500 and the total is Rs 5,500. With four equal diners, the share is Rs 1,375 each. If the receipt already includes a service charge, compare that charge with the intended tip before adding another percentage.

Pakistan-specific example

When settling a restaurant bill in Pakistan, check the printed receipt for taxes and service charges. A displayed percentage may not mean the same thing as a voluntary tip. Use the calculator to verify the arithmetic after deciding which amount should be used as the tip base.

Common questions

Should I tip on tax? There is no universal rule; follow the receipt and the applicable local practice.

Why is the result different from the restaurant receipt? The receipt may use a different base, service charge, tax treatment or rounding rule.
