Tip Calculator: Tip Amount, Total Bill and Fair Splitting

A tip calculator helps you estimate an optional gratuity, the final bill and each person's share. It is especially useful when a group wants to split a restaurant bill evenly. The result depends on the bill amount, chosen tip percentage and number of people; it does not decide what tip is customary or required in a particular place.

Formula

Tip = bill × tip percentage ÷ 100. Total = bill + tip. Equal share per person = total ÷ number of people. If the group wants to tip only on food rather than on tax or service charges, enter the correct base amount instead of blindly using the final receipt total.

Worked example

For a bill of PKR 6,000 and a chosen 10% tip, the tip is PKR 600 and the combined total is PKR 6,600. Split among four people, the equal share is PKR 1,650 each. If the restaurant already added a service charge, check the receipt before adding another gratuity so the group understands what is being paid.

How to use it

  1. Enter the bill amount before the extra tip you want to calculate.
  2. Choose the percentage agreed by the group.
  3. Enter the number of people sharing the total, using a whole number of at least one.
  4. Review the tip, total and per-person amount before collecting money.

Practical splitting choices

An equal split is simple but may not be fair when people ordered very different amounts. You can calculate individual shares separately, split shared dishes evenly and assign personal drinks to the person who ordered them. Currency rounding may leave a small remainder; agree who covers it instead of assuming every share will divide into exact paisas. Keep discounts, coupons, taxes and service charges consistent in the bill base.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator include an existing service charge?

Only if you include that amount in the bill input; check your receipt and avoid double counting.

What if the bill is split unevenly?

Calculate each person's own share and tip basis separately, or agree on a transparent split method.

Is a 15% tip mandatory?

No universal percentage applies everywhere. Follow local practice and the terms shown on the bill.
