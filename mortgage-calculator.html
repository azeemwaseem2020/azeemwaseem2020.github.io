Mortgage payment basics

A mortgage payment estimate answers the principal-and-interest part of a home loan. Taxes, insurance, HOA charges and lender fees can require separate calculations, so compare the mathematical payment with the full housing cost when reviewing a real offer.

Formula and assumptions

For a fixed annual nominal rate converted to a monthly rate r and n monthly payments, the estimated payment is P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1), where P is the financed principal. If the entered rate is zero, payment is P ÷ n. The estimate assumes equal monthly payments and a fixed rate; it does not include fees unless they are added separately.

How mortgage payments are calculated

Enter the loan amount, annual interest rate and repayment term. The calculator estimates the regular payment using the inputs you provide and helps separate the payment into principal and interest.

Worked example: monthly payment versus full home cost

For illustration, a PKR 10,000,000 principal at a hypothetical fixed 12% annual rate over 20 years gives an estimated monthly payment of about PKR 110,108.61 under the monthly amortization formula. Total scheduled payments would be about PKR 26.43 million, including about PKR 16.43 million in interest, before processing fees, taxes, insurance or takaful and legal costs. These numbers demonstrate the formula; they are not a current bank offer or a forecast of future rates.

Changing the term can lower the monthly payment while increasing total interest. Compare both the monthly amount and the full repayment, then verify the lender’s own schedule and how any rate changes are handled.

What to check before using the result

Confirm the interest rate, payment frequency, loan term and any fees or taxes that may apply. A calculator estimate does not replace a lender's offer or amortization schedule.

Home loan and mortgage calculator Pakistan

For Pakistan home finance planning, enter the principal, annual rate and term from the financing offer you are evaluating. Islamic home finance and conventional mortgages can use different pricing and contract structures, so this calculator should be treated as a mathematical payment estimate rather than an official lender quotation.

Compare the result with the lender's schedule and include processing charges, insurance or takaful, taxes, legal costs and other transaction expenses where applicable.
