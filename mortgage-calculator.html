Mortgage payment basics

A mortgage payment estimate answers the principal-and-interest part of a home loan. Taxes, insurance, HOA charges and lender fees can require separate calculations, so compare the mathematical payment with the full housing cost when reviewing a real offer.

Formula & assumptions

For a fixed-rate loan, the monthly payment is based on principal, monthly interest rate and the number of monthly payments. The estimate assumes regular payments and a constant rate; taxes, insurance and lender-specific charges are separate unless explicitly included.

Home loan and mortgage calculator Pakistan

For Pakistan home finance planning, enter the principal, annual rate and term from the financing offer you are evaluating. Islamic home finance and conventional mortgages can use different pricing and contract structures, so this calculator should be treated as a mathematical payment estimate rather than an official lender quotation.

Compare the result with the lender's schedule and include processing charges, insurance or takaful, taxes, legal costs and other transaction expenses where applicable.

Worked example: fixed-rate mortgage

For a hypothetical loan of 200,000 at a 6% annual interest rate over 30 years, the monthly interest rate is 0.06 ÷ 12 = 0.005 and there are 360 monthly payments. The standard fixed-payment formula gives a principal-and-interest payment of about 1,199.10 per month. Total repayment is the monthly payment multiplied by the number of payments, and total interest is total repayment minus the original principal. Figures are illustrative and exclude fees, taxes and insurance.

What can change the result?

  • A higher rate increases the payment when principal and term are unchanged.
  • A longer term usually lowers the monthly payment but can increase total interest.
  • Property taxes, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance, HOA fees and closing costs may add to the real monthly housing cost.
  • Variable rates, payment frequency, lender rounding and fees can make an actual repayment schedule differ from this estimate.

For Pakistan home-finance planning, compare the mathematical estimate with the lender's written repayment schedule. Conventional mortgages and Islamic home-finance products may use different contractual structures; this calculator is not a lender quotation or a recommendation to borrow.

Before comparing offers

Use the same principal, term, payment frequency and fee assumptions for each scenario. Review the total amount payable and any early-settlement or processing charges, not just the advertised monthly payment. A qualified financial professional or the lender can explain the terms of a specific offer.
