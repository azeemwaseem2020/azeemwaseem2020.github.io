How to use this mortgage calculator

  1. Enter the loan amount you expect to borrow.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate and loan term.
  3. Review the estimated monthly principal-and-interest payment and total repayment.

This estimate focuses on the loan itself. Property taxes, homeowners insurance, HOA fees, PMI and lender-specific charges can change the amount you actually pay each month.

What the result means

Your monthly payment is a planning estimate, not a loan offer. The interest rate, compounding method, fees, payment frequency and lender rules can change the final schedule.

Mortgage calculation method

For a standard fixed-rate amortizing loan, the periodic payment is based on the principal, periodic interest rate and number of payments. The calculator separates the financing cost from other homeownership expenses so you can compare scenarios consistently.

Questions to check before comparing mortgages

  • Is the quoted rate fixed or adjustable?
  • Does the lender require mortgage insurance?
  • Are taxes and insurance included in the quoted payment?
  • What fees are paid upfront and which are financed?
