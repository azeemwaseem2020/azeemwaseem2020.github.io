Modern physics

Photon Energy connects frequency or wavelength to photon energy. Mass–Energy calculates rest-energy equivalence. Half-Life models repeated fractional decay.

Optics: write the sign convention down

Lens and mirror calculations can look wrong when the arithmetic is fine but the sign convention is inconsistent. Before entering values, decide how the calculator defines object distance, image distance and focal length. Then use that convention for every value.

Photon-energy example

Photon energy can be found from E = hf or E = hc/λ. If wavelength is used, keep it in metres when using h and c in SI units. A nanometre-to-metre conversion is easy to miss and changes the result by a factor of one billion.

Half-life is repeated decay

After one half-life, 50% remains; after two, 25%; after three, 12.5%. The model is exponential, so it should not be treated as a fixed amount being removed after every interval.
