Simple Interest Calculator

Calculate simple interest and final amount from principal, annual rate and time.

Search intent

Targets simple interest calculator, interest on principal, simple interest formula and total amount.

Formula & assumptions

Simple interest I = P × r × t, where r is the annual rate as a decimal. Total amount = P + I. It does not model compounding.
