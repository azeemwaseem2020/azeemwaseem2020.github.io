Formula & assumptions

Simple interest I = P × r × t, where r is the annual rate as a decimal. Total amount = P + I. It does not model compounding.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is simple interest? Simple interest is I = P × r × t, where the rate is expressed as a decimal.

Does simple interest compound? No. Interest is calculated on the original principal only.
