Simple Interest Calculator

Calculate simple interest and final amount from principal, annual rate and time.

Simple Interest Calculator Guide

Use simple interest when interest is calculated only on the original principal rather than being added back for further interest calculations.

Formula & assumptions

Simple interest I = P × r × t, where r is the annual rate as a decimal. Total amount = P + I. It does not model compounding.

Worked example

For a principal of 10,000 at 10% per year for 2 years, interest is 10,000 × 0.10 × 2 = 2,000, so the total is 12,000.

How to use it

Enter the principal, annual rate and time in years. If your time is in months, convert it to years before calculating.

When simple interest is not appropriate

This tool does not model compound interest, fees, taxes, changing rates or payment schedules. For a real loan or savings product, use the terms supplied by the financial provider.

How this calculation works

Use the calculator with consistent values for the amount, rate and time period. The result is an estimate based on the assumptions entered, so it should be checked against the terms of the actual financial product or investment.

Use consistent assumptions

When comparing scenarios, keep the starting amount, time period, payment pattern and rate basis consistent. A rate quoted annually should not be mixed with a monthly period without converting the assumptions appropriately.

Check costs and real-world conditions

Fees, taxes, inflation, payment timing, compounding and market performance can change the amount you actually receive or pay. Review those factors before making a financial decision.
