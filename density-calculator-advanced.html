Density formula and reverse solving

Density is calculated as ρ = m ÷ V. If density and volume are known, mass is m = ρV; if density and mass are known, volume is V = m ÷ ρ. Keep the mass and volume units compatible before interpreting the number.

How to use the advanced density calculator

  2. Calculate density.
  3. Use the result to compare materials or check a measurement.
  4. For reverse calculations, rearrange the same density relationship.

Worked example

If mass is 500 g and volume is 250 cm³, density = 500 ÷ 250 = 2 g/cm³.

ADVANCED DENSITY MODEL CHECK

Density results depend on how mass and volume were defined

The advanced calculation is most useful when the mass and volume inputs represent the same sample. Density can change with temperature, pressure, composition and void space, so the measurement context matters.

Bulk vs material density

A porous or granular sample can have a bulk density that includes voids; this should not automatically be treated as the intrinsic density of the solid material.

Unit discipline

Mass and volume units must be converted consistently before comparing density values.
