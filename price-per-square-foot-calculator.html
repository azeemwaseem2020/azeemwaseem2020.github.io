Price per square foot

Price per square foot is calculated by dividing the total price by the total area: price per sq ft = total price ÷ area in sq ft. For a rectangular space, area is length × width. Use the same area unit throughout the calculation.

Worked example

A room measuring 20 ft by 15 ft has 300 sq ft. If flooring costs PKR 180,000, the price is 180,000 ÷ 300 = PKR 600 per sq ft.

Pakistan property example

For property or construction comparisons, clarify whether the quoted price uses covered area, carpet area or another measurement. Pakistani property listings may use square feet, square yards, marla or kanal, so convert the area before comparing rates.

FAQs

Does this include installation? Only if the total price includes it. Can I compare two properties? Yes, if their area definitions are consistent. What if the shape is irregular? Break it into measurable sections and add the areas.
