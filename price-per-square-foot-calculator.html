Price per square foot

Price per square foot is calculated by dividing the total price by the total area: price per sq ft = total price ÷ area in sq ft. For a rectangular space, area is length × width. Use the same area unit throughout the calculation.

Worked example

A room measuring 20 ft by 15 ft has 300 sq ft. If flooring costs PKR 180,000, the price is 180,000 ÷ 300 = PKR 600 per sq ft.

Pakistan property example

For property or construction comparisons, clarify whether the quoted price uses covered area, carpet area or another measurement. Pakistani property listings may use square feet, square yards, marla or kanal, so convert the area before comparing rates.

FAQs

Does this include installation? Only if the total price includes it. Can I compare two properties? Yes, if their area definitions are consistent. What if the shape is irregular? Break it into measurable sections and add the areas.

Price Per Square Foot: How to Compare Costs

Price per square foot is calculated by dividing total price by total area: Price per ft² = Total Price ÷ Area in ft². For example, a room or property priced at Rs 3,000,000 with 1,500 ft² of area has a price of Rs 2,000 per ft². The same idea can be expressed per square metre after converting the area unit.

This metric is useful for comparing properties with different sizes, but it should not be treated as a complete valuation measure. Location, condition, floor level, construction quality, parking, utilities and layout can all affect property value. In Pakistan, listings may quote covered area, covered plus open area, or plot size, so compare like-for-like measurements.

If a tile or flooring project costs Rs 180,000 for 900 ft², the material-and-labour cost is Rs 200 per ft². This can be useful for comparing contractor quotations. Confirm whether each quote includes adhesive, wastage, transport, labour and taxes before deciding that one price is directly comparable.

Unit conversion

One square metre equals about 10.7639 square feet. If a property is 100 m², its area is about 1,076.39 ft². Always convert the area before dividing; mixing metres and feet can produce a tenfold error.

FAQs

Does price per ft² include taxes? Only if the total price entered includes them.

Can I compare different cities? Mathematically yes, but local market conditions can make the figures very different.

What area should I use? Use the same area definition for every property being compared.
