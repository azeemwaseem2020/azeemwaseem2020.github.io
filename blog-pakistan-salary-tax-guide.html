PAKISTAN • INCOME TAX • TAX YEAR 2027

Pakistan Salary Tax Guide 2026–27

A salary-tax result is easier to trust when you can see what income is being taxed, which slab applies and why the monthly deduction may differ from a simple annual average.

Start with taxable salary, not just gross pay

Gross salary is the amount shown before deductions. Taxable income is the amount to which the applicable income-tax rules are applied after considering the items that are legally relevant to the calculation. For a calculator estimate, keep the distinction clear: entering gross salary does not automatically mean every rupee is taxable in every situation.

Pakistan's 2026–27 salaried tax slabs

For Tax Year 2027, the Federal Board of Revenue's Finance Bill 2026 sets the salaried rates used by the current Calcora estimate. The published table starts with a zero-tax band up to PKR 600,000 and then applies progressive rates to the amount falling within each higher band.

How a progressive slab calculation works

A higher marginal rate does not mean the entire salary is taxed at that rate. Each band is applied according to the formula for that band. For example, if taxable income is PKR 1,500,000, the calculation uses the fixed amount for the PKR 1.2 million–2.2 million band plus 11% of the amount above PKR 1.2 million. That produces an estimated annual tax of PKR 39,000 before any other legally relevant adjustments.

Why monthly payroll tax can look different

An annual tax divided by 12 is only an average. Employers may calculate deductions using payroll information, expected annual income, bonuses, changes in salary, eligible adjustments and the applicable withholding rules. A promotion in the middle of the year or a bonus can change the expected annual tax even when the employee's normal monthly salary is unchanged.

Gross salary, taxable salary and take-home pay

These are three different numbers. Gross salary is the starting compensation figure. Taxable income is the figure used for the income-tax calculation after applicable rules. Take-home pay is what remains after income tax and other payroll deductions. Calcora's calculator is intended to make the income-tax arithmetic easier to inspect; it is not an employer payroll statement.

Worked example

Suppose taxable income is PKR 1,500,000 for Tax Year 2027. Under the published slab above, the estimate is PKR 6,000 plus 11% of PKR 300,000, giving PKR 39,000 annual tax. The monthly average of that annual amount is PKR 3,250. The monthly payroll deduction can still differ when the employer is accounting for the employee's expected full-year position or other payroll details.

Common mistakes

  • Applying a marginal rate to the whole salary.
  • Confusing gross salary with taxable income.
  • Using an old tax-year slab after a Finance Act changes the rates.
  • Assuming annual tax divided by 12 must equal every monthly payroll deduction.
  • Treating a calculator estimate as an official FBR assessment.

Official reference

The Federal Board of Revenue publishes current tax legislation, finance acts and rate material. Calcora should be used as a calculation aid; official FBR material and professional advice should control where a filing, assessment or complex tax position is involved.
