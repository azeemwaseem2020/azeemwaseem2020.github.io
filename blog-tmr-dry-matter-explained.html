TMR Dry Matter: How to Calculate DM Intake and Moisture

Dry matter removes the water component from a feed and gives a more comparable basis for evaluating ingredients and rations. It is a calculation tool, not a complete ration recommendation.

The basic dry-matter calculation

Dry matter kg = as-fed kg × DM fraction. A 20 kg batch at 40% DM contains 8 kg of dry matter and 12 kg of water.

Why as-fed weight can mislead

Two feeds can weigh the same while supplying very different amounts of nutrients because their moisture content differs. Comparing silage, fresh forage and dry ingredients by as-fed kilograms alone can therefore be misleading.

Calculating a mixed ration

For a TMR, calculate each ingredient's dry matter separately, then add the dry-matter kilograms. Divide total DM by total as-fed weight to estimate the mixed ration DM percentage.

Worked example

Suppose 10 kg forage at 30% DM and 5 kg concentrate at 90% DM are mixed. The forage contributes 3 kg DM and the concentrate contributes 4.5 kg DM, so the mixture contains 7.5 kg DM in 15 kg as-fed feed: 50% DM.

Refusals matter

If delivered feed and refusals are measured, the consumed as-fed amount can be estimated as delivered minus refused. DM intake can then be estimated using the relevant DM percentage, provided the measurements are representative.
