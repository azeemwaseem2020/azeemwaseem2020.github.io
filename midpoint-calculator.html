Finding the midpoint between two points

For points (x1,y1) and (x2,y2), midpoint = ((x1+x2)/2, (y1+y2)/2).

Worked example

The midpoint of (2,4) and (8,10) is (5,7).

A practical Pakistan example

This is useful in coordinate geometry, mapping and design when a point exactly halfway between two known coordinates is required.

Important assumptions

Keep x coordinates together and y coordinates together; do not average across axes incorrectly.

Midpoint interpretation

The midpoint of two points (x₁,y₁) and (x₂,y₂) is ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2). It is the point exactly halfway between the coordinates. For (2,4) and (8,10), the midpoint is (5,7). The formula works independently on each coordinate.

Midpoints are useful in coordinate geometry, mapping and design. If the coordinates represent physical locations, use the same reference system and units for both points. A midpoint in latitude and longitude is not automatically the geographic midpoint over a curved Earth; specialized geodesic methods may be required.
