Voltage Calculator Guide

Voltage is the electrical potential difference that can be calculated from current and resistance with V = IR.

Worked example

If current is 2 A and resistance is 6 Ω, voltage is 2 × 6 = 12 V.

Units and limits

Use amps for current and ohms for resistance. This calculation describes the stated electrical relationship; real circuits may also involve frequency, temperature, component behavior and other effects.
