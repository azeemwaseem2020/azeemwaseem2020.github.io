Variance formula

Population variance is σ² = Σ(x − μ)² ÷ N, where μ is the population mean and N is the number of values. Sample variance is s² = Σ(x − x̄)² ÷ (n − 1) when the data is treated as a sample.

Worked example

For 2, 4, 4, 7, 9, the mean is 5.2. The population variance is the average squared deviation from 5.2. If the same values represent a sample, dividing by n − 1 gives the sample variance instead.

Population vs sample variance

Use population variance when your dataset represents the complete population of interest. Use sample variance when the observations are a sample used to estimate variability in a larger population.

What variance tells you

Variance measures the average squared distance from the mean. Because deviations are squared, variance is expressed in squared units; standard deviation is often easier to interpret because it returns to the original units.

FAQ

Why does sample variance use n − 1? The n − 1 divisor is the common unbiased estimator of population variance under standard sampling assumptions.

Can variance be negative? No. Squared deviations are non-negative, so variance cannot be negative.

What is the difference between variance and standard deviation? Standard deviation is the square root of variance.

Variance and data spread

Variance measures how far observations spread around their mean. Population variance divides the squared deviations by N; sample variance usually divides by n−1. The choice depends on whether the data represent the full population or a sample used to estimate a population parameter.

Worked example

For a small dataset such as 2, 4 and 6, the mean is 4. The squared deviations are 4, 0 and 4. Population variance is 8/3 ≈ 2.67. Sample variance is 8/2 = 4.

Interpretation

Variance is expressed in squared units, which can make standard deviation easier to interpret because it returns to the original unit. Always state whether sample or population variance was used.

FAQs

Can variance be negative? No. Why do sample and population results differ? Their denominators differ. What does larger variance mean? Greater spread around the mean.
