Protein intake estimates

Protein needs depend on body size, age, activity, health status and the goal being considered. A simple planning estimate often multiplies body weight in kilograms by a chosen grams-per-kilogram target. That target is not universal and should be selected for the person's context.

Worked example

If a 70 kg adult uses a planning target of 1.2 g/kg/day, the estimate is 70 × 1.2 = 84 g of protein per day. This is an intake target, not the protein content of a single meal.

Practical context

For diets in Pakistan, protein can come from milk, yogurt, eggs, meat, fish, lentils, chickpeas, beans and other foods. Total diet quality and energy intake matter as well. People with kidney disease or other medical conditions may need individualized advice.

FAQs

Is more protein always better? No. Does body weight determine all protein needs? No. Is this medical advice? No; use a qualified professional for clinical nutrition needs.

Protein intake estimates

Protein needs vary with body weight, age, activity, training, health and the guideline being used. A common planning method multiplies body weight by a target grams-per-kilogram value.

Worked example

At a hypothetical target of 1.6 g/kg, a 70 kg adult would calculate 112 g/day. This is a planning estimate rather than a universal requirement.

Food planning

Protein can come from meat, fish, eggs, dairy, legumes, soy and other foods. In Pakistan, foods such as daal, eggs, milk, yogurt and lean meats can contribute to a varied diet. People with kidney disease or other medical conditions should use individualized professional advice.
