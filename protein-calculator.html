Protein Calculator

Estimate a daily protein target from body weight and an editable protein-per-kilogram target.

This is a general nutrition estimate. Individual needs vary with age, activity, goals and medical conditions.

How the estimate is calculated

The calculator multiplies body weight in kilograms by the protein target in grams per kilogram. For example, 70 kg at 1.2 g/kg gives an estimated 84 g of protein per day.

Important note

This is a planning estimate rather than individualized medical or dietary advice. Protein needs can vary with age, activity, goals, pregnancy, illness and other circumstances. Use an appropriate target for your situation and consult a qualified professional when needed.

Daily protein estimate: formula and guide

This calculator estimates a daily protein amount from body weight and a selected protein target in grams per kilogram. It lets you test different assumptions; it does not decide which target is appropriate for every person.

Formula

Daily protein (g) = body weight (kg) × target (g/kg). For example, a 70 kg person using a target of 1.2 g/kg gets 70 × 1.2 = 84 g per day. The output is an estimate based on the target entered, not a laboratory measurement of nutritional need.

How to use it

  1. Enter body weight in kilograms.
  2. Choose a grams-per-kilogram target that is appropriate to your circumstances.
  3. Select Calculate Protein and review the estimated daily total.
  4. Use the estimate as a planning reference and consider protein across meals and food sources.

Choosing a target carefully

Protein needs vary with age, activity level, training goals, total energy intake, pregnancy, recovery from illness and other factors. Some medical conditions require individualized protein guidance, and more protein is not automatically better. Food quality, overall dietary pattern and adequate energy intake also matter. This tool does not prescribe a diet or assess kidney function, malnutrition or other health conditions.

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculate protein from food?

No. It estimates a daily target. To total protein from meals, use reliable nutrition labels or a food-tracking resource and add the amounts consumed.

What does g/kg mean?

It means grams of protein per kilogram of body weight. Multiplying body weight in kilograms by this rate gives grams per day.

Should everyone use the same target?

No. Requirements depend on individual circumstances. Seek qualified advice if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, are planning a restrictive diet or are unsure which target is appropriate.
