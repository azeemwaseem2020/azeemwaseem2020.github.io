How to calculate a discounted price

First calculate the discount amount by multiplying the original price by the discount rate. Then subtract that amount from the original price to get the final sale price.

Discount amount = Original price × (Discount % ÷ 100)

Sale price = Original price − Discount amount

Worked example

If an item costs $100 and has a 20% discount, the discount is $20 and the final price is $80. The same method works with other currencies because the calculator uses the numbers you enter.

What this calculator is useful for

Use it for shopping discounts, sale prices, invoices, promotions, retail pricing, and quick percentage calculations. A discount of 100% produces a zero sale price; discounts above 100% are not accepted.

Discount price FAQ

How do I find the sale price?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage to find the savings, then subtract the savings from the original price.

Is the discount percentage the same as the final price percentage?

No. A 20% discount means you pay 80% of the original price.

Does this include sales tax?

No. This calculator calculates the discount only. Tax, shipping, coupons, and other charges may need to be calculated separately.
