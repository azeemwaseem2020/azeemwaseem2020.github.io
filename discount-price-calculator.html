How to calculate a discounted price

First calculate the discount amount by multiplying the original price by the discount rate. Then subtract that amount from the original price to get the final sale price.

Discount amount = Original price × (Discount % ÷ 100)

Sale price = Original price − Discount amount

Worked example

If an item costs $100 and has a 20% discount, the discount is $20 and the final price is $80. The same method works with other currencies because the calculator uses the numbers you enter.

What this calculator is useful for

Use it for shopping discounts, sale prices, invoices, promotions, retail pricing, and quick percentage calculations. A discount of 100% produces a zero sale price; discounts above 100% are not accepted.

Discount price FAQ

How do I find the sale price?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage to find the savings, then subtract the savings from the original price.

Is the discount percentage the same as the final price percentage?

No. A 20% discount means you pay 80% of the original price.

Does this include sales tax?

No. This calculator calculates the discount only. Tax, shipping, coupons, and other charges may need to be calculated separately.

Discount price formula and savings calculation

Calculate the discount amount first, then subtract it from the original price: Discount = Original price × (Discount rate ÷ 100); Sale price = Original price − Discount. The savings percentage is measured against the original price, not the reduced price.

Worked shopping example

A jacket marked at PKR 8,000 with a 15% discount saves 8,000 × 0.15 = PKR 1,200. The price before tax or delivery is 8,000 − 1,200 = PKR 6,800. If the store applies tax after discount, calculate that tax using the store's stated rules; this calculator does not infer tax treatment.

Successive discounts are not usually added

A 20% discount followed by another 10% discount gives 100 × 0.80 × 0.90 = 72 from an original 100, so the total reduction is 28%, not 30%. The second discount is applied to the already-reduced price. Compare final payable prices, including shipping, taxes and service fees, before deciding which offer is cheaper.

Step-by-step method for checking an offer

  1. Write down the original marked price and confirm the discount applies to that item.
  2. Convert the percentage to a decimal by dividing it by 100.
  3. Multiply the original price by that decimal to find the savings.
  4. Subtract the savings from the original price, then add any separately charged tax, delivery or service fees.

For a PKR 12,500 appliance discounted by 8%, savings are 12,500 × 0.08 = PKR 1,000 and the reduced price is PKR 11,500 before other charges. A quick check is that an 8% discount must save less than one tenth of the original price. If your answer is PKR 10,000, recheck the arithmetic or whether you accidentally entered an 80% discount.

When comparing two stores, compare the final payable amount rather than the advertised percentage. One store may offer a larger discount but start from a higher list price or charge more for delivery. Also check whether a coupon can be combined with a sale and whether the stated discount applies before or after taxes. This calculator performs the basic percentage arithmetic; the retailer’s terms determine the actual checkout total.

Discount calculator FAQs

What is a 0% discount?

The discount amount is zero and the sale price equals the original price.

What is a 100% discount?

The calculated sale price is zero before any separate fees or charges.

Does a discount percentage tell me the markup?

No. A discount is measured from the original listed price; markup compares a selling price with a cost basis. They answer different questions.
