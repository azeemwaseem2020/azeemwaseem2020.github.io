Electricity Cost Calculator

Estimate electricity cost from power usage, hours and electricity price per kWh.

Search intent covered

electricity cost calculator, energy cost calculator, kwh cost calculator, electricity bill calculator

Electricity cost formula

Energy (kWh) = power (W) ÷ 1000 × hours used. Cost = energy consumed × electricity rate per kWh.

Worked example

A 1,000 W appliance used 5 hours per day consumes 5 kWh/day. At $0.20/kWh, the estimated daily cost is $1.00 and a 30-day estimate is $30.00.

How to use

  1. Enter appliance power in watts.
  2. Enter hours used per day.
  3. Enter your electricity price per kWh.
  4. Compare daily and monthly estimates.
