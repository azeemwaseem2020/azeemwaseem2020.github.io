How commission is calculated

A simple commission is calculated as commissionable sales multiplied by the commission rate. If a salesperson earns 5% on $20,000 of eligible sales, the commission is $1,000.

Tiered commission plans

Some plans apply different rates to different sales bands, while others apply a higher rate to the entire amount after a threshold is reached. The plan documentation should be checked before applying a single-rate calculation.

Commission FAQ

Is commission calculated on total sales?

Not necessarily. Returns, discounts, taxes, excluded products and other plan rules can change the commissionable amount.

Can commission be combined with a base salary?

Yes. In a compensation plan, total gross earnings can include base pay plus eligible commission and other components.
