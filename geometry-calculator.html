Geometry Calculator

Calculate common area and perimeter measurements for basic 2D shapes.

Search intent

Useful for geometry calculator, area calculator, perimeter calculator, rectangle area, circle area and triangle area searches.

How to use the geometry calculator

  1. Select rectangle, circle or triangle.
  2. Enter the required dimensions.
  3. Use the displayed area or perimeter/circumference result.

Worked examples

A 10 × 5 rectangle has area 50 square units and perimeter 30 units. A circle with radius 3 has area 9π ≈ 28.27 square units.

Formula & assumptions

Rectangle area = length × width. Circle area = πr² and circumference = 2πr. Triangle area = ½ × base × height.
