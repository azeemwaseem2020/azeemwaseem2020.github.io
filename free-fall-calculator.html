Free Fall Calculator — Time, Velocity & Distance

Solve idealized vertical free-fall problems using constant gravitational acceleration. Choose the quantity you want to find.

Ideal free-fall formulas

From rest, d = ½gt², v = gt, and v² = 2gd.

Worked example

Ignoring air resistance, a 20 m drop with g = 9.80665 m/s² takes about √(2d/g) = 2.02 s.

Assumptions

This tool assumes the object starts from rest, acceleration remains constant and air resistance is ignored. Real objects may deviate from this ideal model.
