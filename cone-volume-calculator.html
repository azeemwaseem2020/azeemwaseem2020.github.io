Volume of a cone

The volume of a right circular cone is V = ⅓πr²h, where r is the radius of the circular base and h is the perpendicular height.

Worked example

For radius 3 cm and height 10 cm, volume is ⅓π(3²)(10) = 30π cm³, approximately 94.25 cm³.

Cone volume FAQ

Does height mean the slanted side?

No. The formula uses perpendicular height from the base to the apex, not the slant height.

What units does the result use?

The result is in cubic units corresponding to the units used for radius and height.

Calculation context

Cone volume uses the base area and height; for a circular cone, V = πr²h/3. The radius and height must use compatible units so the resulting volume is consistent.
