PSX • CAPITAL GAINS TAX

Capital Gains Tax Calculator Shares Pakistan

A focused capital gains tax calculator shares Pakistan workflow for PSX transactions. The applicable CGT treatment can depend on the security, acquisition date, investor status and tax period, so the rate is an explicit input.

How the estimate works

The arithmetic is CGT = taxable gain × applicable rate. This page does not decide whether the entire entered gain is taxable or which rate legally applies.

PSX costs are separate

Brokerage, exchange and clearing charges, taxes and other transaction costs can reduce net proceeds. Use the PSX Profit Calculator when you need the full trade calculation.

Verify before filing

Use current FBR, PSX, NCCPL and other applicable authoritative material for the relevant tax period. Calcora's result is a scenario estimate from your inputs, not a tax assessment.
