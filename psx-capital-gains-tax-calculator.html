PSX Capital Gains Tax Calculator Pakistan

Estimate the capital gains tax amount from a PSX share gain using the taxable gain and the applicable CGT rate you enter. This page deliberately separates the arithmetic from the legal tax-rate question, because the applicable treatment can depend on the security, acquisition details, investor circumstances and tax year.

Maintained by: Abdul Qadir · Updated: 7 October 2026 · About Calcora

Method boundary: Calcora calculates the scenario you enter; it does not determine your legal tax status or replace an FBR/NCCPL assessment.

How the PSX CGT estimate works

The calculator uses one transparent arithmetic rule:

Estimated CGT = taxable capital gain × applicable CGT rate

For example, if the taxable gain entered is PKR 50,000 and the applicable rate is 15%, the estimated CGT is PKR 7,500. The remaining amount after this entered CGT would be PKR 42,500 before considering any other transaction costs or tax adjustments.

What counts as the gain input?

Use the taxable gain that is appropriate to the transaction and tax rules you are applying. Do not automatically treat the gross difference between sale proceeds and purchase cost as the final taxable gain in every situation. Acquisition records, allowable costs, holding details and the applicable tax framework can affect the amount that should be used.

CGT rate is an explicit input for a reason

Calcora does not hard-code a single permanent PSX CGT rate into this calculator. Tax rates and treatment can change by tax year and can depend on the relevant rules. Enter the rate applicable to your situation after checking current authoritative material.

PSX profit and CGT are related, but not the same calculation

A trade can have a gross or net trading profit before the final tax treatment is considered. Brokerage, exchange or clearing charges and other transaction costs may affect the economic result. For the wider trade calculation, use the PSX Profit Calculator. Use this page when your specific question is the CGT amount from a taxable gain and an applicable rate.

Worked Pakistan example

Suppose a PSX investor has a taxable capital gain of PKR 200,000 and, after checking the applicable tax rules, uses a 20% illustrative rate.

CGT = PKR 200,000 × 20% = PKR 40,000.

This example demonstrates the arithmetic only. It does not state that 20% is the legally applicable rate for every PSX investor or tax year.

How to verify a result before relying on it

  1. Confirm the relevant tax year and security type.
  2. Check the current applicable CGT treatment and rate from authoritative Pakistan tax-market sources.
  3. Reconcile purchase and sale records with your broker statement and transaction records.
  4. Use the taxable-gain figure that matches the applicable rules rather than assuming gross profit is always taxable gain.
  5. Keep the calculation and source date with your records if the result will be used for filing or financial decisions.
Source policy: For tax decisions, verify current material from the Federal Board of Revenue, relevant NCCPL material and your broker's current transaction records. Calcora provides a calculation aid, not an official tax assessment.

PSX CGT calculator FAQs

Does this calculator know my legally applicable CGT rate?

No. The rate is an input because the applicable treatment can vary by tax year and circumstances. Verify the current rule before relying on the result.

Does CGT equal my entire PSX profit?

No. CGT is a tax calculation applied to the relevant taxable gain under the applicable rules. Trading profit, allowable costs and taxable gain are not necessarily identical.

Should I include brokerage in the gain?

Do not guess. Use the treatment required by the applicable tax rules and reconcile the figures with your broker records. This calculator intentionally does not make that legal determination for you.

When should I use the PSX Profit Calculator instead?

Use the PSX Profit Calculator when you want a broader trade view covering purchase cost, sale proceeds, brokerage, return and related calculations.
