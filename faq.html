QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Frequently asked questions about Calcora calculators

A quick reference for using the tools, understanding their formulas and knowing what their results do—and do not—tell you.

Are Calcora calculators free?

Yes. They are designed for free use without an account.

Are the results guaranteed to be exact?

No. Results are estimates based on the assumptions and inputs shown. Real-world rates, fees, losses and local conditions can change outcomes.

Can I use Calcora on my phone?

Yes. The interface is responsive and designed for both smaller and larger screens.

Where can I learn the formulas?

Visit Guides or use the guide link associated with a calculator. The guides explain the calculation in plain language.

Does Calcora send my calculator inputs to a server?

The calculators currently published are designed to perform their calculations in your browser. See the Privacy Policy for the site's current privacy approach.

How does Calcora handle a reported error?

We review the reported inputs, formula and expected result. If the issue is confirmed, the relevant calculator or explanation can be corrected. Please include enough detail to reproduce the problem.

What should I do if I find an error?

Please contact Calcora with the calculator name, inputs and expected result so the issue can be investigated.

Choose a calculator

For finance, try the Loan EMI Calculator or Compound Interest Calculator. For energy planning, use the Solar Load Calculator. For agriculture, see the TMR Feed Calculator and Fertilizer Calculator.

Planning reminder: use important calculator results as estimates and verify consequential financial, electrical or agricultural decisions with appropriate current professional or official guidance.
