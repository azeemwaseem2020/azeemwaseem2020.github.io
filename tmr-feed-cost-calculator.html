Using the feed cost calculator

Enter the quantities and prices you are actually using. The calculator adds the ingredient costs for the batch and then works out the daily group and per-animal cost from the feeding amount you enter.

From a batch price to a daily cost

The calculator totals ingredient cost for the batch and translates it into daily group and per-animal cost from the feeding rate you enter. This is a feed-cost calculation, not a complete dairy profitability model.

What this calculator measures

For each ingredient, enter its kilograms in the batch and current price per kilogram. The calculator multiplies those values to estimate ingredient cost, then totals the batch and translates it to a daily group and per-animal figure based on the feeding rate you enter.

Why current local prices matter

Feed economics can change quickly. A useful cost calculation should use the price actually paid or a clearly dated local market estimate rather than a fixed online price. This tool therefore asks you to enter the price yourself.

Example

If a batch costs Rs 40,000 and the group consumes 400 kg/day, the batch covers 2.5 days and the arithmetic daily group cost is Rs 16,000. For 20 animals, that is Rs 800 per animal/day.

What is not included

The result is feed cost only. It does not include labour, medicine, electricity, equipment depreciation, transport, wastage outside the quantities entered, milk revenue or full farm profitability.
