Urea Fertilizer Calculator: How to Calculate 46-0-0 Urea

Urea calculations become reliable when the nitrogen target, product analysis and field unit are kept on the same basis.

What does 46-0-0 mean?

A 46-0-0 fertilizer contains 46% nitrogen by weight and no labelled P₂O₅ or K₂O. Always confirm the guaranteed analysis on the product bag.

Core Urea formula

Urea required = nitrogen target ÷ 0.46

If a recommendation calls for 46 kg N/ha and the product is 46% N, the mathematical product rate is 100 kg Urea/ha.

Calculate N supplied by Urea

Urea N supplied = Urea weight × 0.46. Therefore 50 kg Urea supplies 23 kg N on the labelled basis, while 100 kg supplies 46 kg N.

Per acre calculation

If the target is 46 kg N/acre, 100 kg/acre of 46% Urea is the corresponding mathematical product quantity. If your recommendation is in kg/ha, convert the area or rate basis before calculating.

How many bags?

For a 50 kg bag, divide total Urea kilograms by 50. A requirement of 250 kg equals 5 exact 50 kg bags. A requirement of 275 kg equals 5.5 bags, so six bags may be needed for purchasing, while the field application requirement remains 275 kg.

Urea cost calculation

Cost = number of bags × price per bag. For better comparisons, also calculate price per kilogram of N rather than comparing bag prices alone.

Urea with DAP or other nitrogen sources

Do not ignore nitrogen already supplied by DAP, compound fertilizers or manure. Add the known N contributions first, then calculate the remaining Urea requirement from the target.

Timing and application context

The correct mathematical rate is only one part of nitrogen management. Crop stage, soil conditions, rainfall or irrigation, placement and local recommendations affect how nitrogen should be managed. This guide does not provide a universal crop recommendation.

Common mistakes

  • Using 46% when the actual product has a different analysis.
  • Mixing kg/ha with acres.
  • Confusing product kilograms with nitrogen kilograms.
  • Ignoring N supplied by DAP or manure.
  • Rounding the application rate too early.

FAQ

How much Urea gives 50 kg nitrogen?

For a 46% N product, 50 ÷ 0.46 = about 108.70 kg Urea.

How much N is in one 50 kg Urea bag?

At 46% N, 50 × 0.46 = 23 kg N.

Can Calcora recommend how much nitrogen my crop needs?

No. Use a trusted soil and crop recommendation, then use the calculator for conversion and planning.

Frequently asked questions

Are calculator results a professional recommendation?

No. The calculator applies the inputs and calculation method you provide. Field, financial or equipment decisions should be checked against current official information and the relevant professional guidance.

Why do units and assumptions matter?

A mathematically correct formula can still produce a misleading result when units, rate conventions, product analysis, efficiency or other inputs are inconsistent.

What should I verify before using the result?

Verify the input values, units, formula assumptions and any fees, product specifications or local conditions that can materially change the result.
