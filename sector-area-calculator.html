Area of a circular sector

A sector is the region bounded by two radii and an arc. For an angle in degrees, its area is A = (θ/360)πr². If the angle is in radians, the formula is A = ½r²θ. Use the version matching the angle unit.

Worked example

For radius 10 cm and central angle 90°, the area is (90/360) × π × 10² = 25π ≈ 78.54 cm². A quarter circle is the special case represented by 90°.

Practical example

Sector areas are useful for circular plots, mechanical parts, fan-shaped layouts and geometry exercises. Confirm that the radius reaches the circle boundary and that the angle is the central angle.

FAQs

Does the formula work for 360°? Yes; it gives the full circle area. What if the angle is in radians? Use the radian formula. What are the units? Square units.

Sector Area: Formula, Example and Practical Use

A circular sector is the portion of a circle bounded by two radii and an arc. If the central angle is measured in degrees, its area is calculated with A = (θ/360) × πr². Here, r is the circle radius and θ is the central angle. The same relationship can be written as A = ½r²θ when θ is expressed in radians. Use one angle convention at a time; mixing degrees and radians is a common source of large errors.

For example, if a sector has a radius of 10 cm and a central angle of 72°, the calculation is (72/360) × π × 10² = 20π, or about 62.83 cm². The arc length for the same sector is (72/360) × 2π × 10, which is about 12.57 cm. Area describes the surface enclosed by the sector, while arc length describes only its curved boundary.

In a practical Pakistan example, a fabricator might need the area of a fan-shaped metal plate before estimating sheet usage. Entering the measured radius in metres and the included angle in degrees gives the sector area in square metres. For costing, multiply that area by the sheet price per square metre, while allowing separately for cutting waste and edge trimming.

Important assumptions

The calculator assumes a true circular sector and a radius measured from the circle centre to the arc. If the shape is an annular sector, ring segment, or irregular curve, a different formula is required. Keep all lengths in the same unit before calculating.

FAQs

Can I use radians? Yes. With radians, use A = ½r²θ.

Does diameter work instead of radius? Convert diameter to radius by dividing by two.

What does a 360° angle mean? It represents the complete circle, so the sector area equals πr².
