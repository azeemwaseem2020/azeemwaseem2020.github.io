Pregnancy Due Date Calculator

Estimate an expected due date from the first day of the last menstrual period (LMP).

How the estimate works

A common estimate uses 280 days (40 weeks) from the first day of the last menstrual period. Cycle length and ovulation timing can shift the actual date.

Health note

This is a date estimate, not medical advice. Pregnancy dating can require clinical assessment, especially when the LMP is uncertain or cycles are irregular. Contact a qualified healthcare professional for personal medical guidance.
