Cash Flow Calculator

Calculate net cash flow for a period from total cash inflows and cash outflows.

Net cash flow formula

Net cash flow = total cash inflows − total cash outflows. A positive result means more cash entered than left during the selected period.

Worked example

If monthly inflows are $25,000 and outflows are $19,000, net cash flow is $6,000.

Cash flow is not profit

Cash movement and accounting profit are different concepts. Loans, asset sales, receivables, inventory and non-cash expenses can make cash flow differ substantially from reported profit.

FAQ

What does negative cash flow mean? It means outflows exceeded inflows during the selected period.

Should starting cash be included? Starting cash is separate from net cash flow; adding it gives the ending cash balance when no other adjustments apply.

Calculation context

Cash-flow analysis compares money entering and leaving a business or project over a defined period. Separating operating, investing and financing assumptions can make the resulting cash-flow picture easier to interpret.

How net cash flow is calculated

Cash flow tracks money actually entering and leaving a business or project during a period. A simple net cash flow calculation is Net cash flow = cash inflows − cash outflows. A positive result means more cash came in than went out during the selected period; a negative result means the opposite.

Worked example

Imagine a small business receives Rs. 500,000 from customers during a month. It pays Rs. 180,000 for stock, Rs. 70,000 in wages, Rs. 40,000 in rent and Rs. 30,000 for utilities and other operating payments. Total cash outflow is Rs. 320,000, so net cash flow is Rs. 180,000.

A practical Pakistan example

A retailer in Multan may have strong sales but still face a cash shortage if customers buy on credit while suppliers require immediate payment. For planning, record the timing of actual receipts and payments rather than assuming that every sale is cash received. This makes the calculation more useful for working-capital decisions.

Cash flow is not profit

Profit and cash flow can differ substantially. A credit sale can increase accounting revenue without increasing cash immediately. Similarly, buying equipment can create a large cash outflow even though the accounting treatment spreads the expense over time. Use the calculator as a cash-planning tool, not as a substitute for full financial statements.

Common mistakes

Keep the period consistent, avoid counting the same transaction twice and separate actual cash movements from non-cash accounting entries. If you are comparing months, use the same definitions for inflows and outflows in each period.

Cash-flow FAQ

Can net cash flow be negative? Yes. Negative cash flow means outflows exceeded inflows for the selected period.

Is positive cash flow the same as profit? No. Accounting profit and cash movement use different concepts and timing.

Should loan proceeds be treated as cash inflow? They can be a financing cash inflow, but the classification depends on the purpose of the analysis.

Why does timing matter? A business can be profitable on paper and still lack cash when payments are due.
