Frequently asked questions

What is an amortization schedule?

An amortization schedule shows how each loan payment is divided between interest and principal over the loan term.

Does the calculator include extra fees?

No. The estimate uses the loan amount, interest rate and payment term. Add applicable fees to the financed amount if appropriate.

Why does the interest portion change over time?

With a standard amortizing loan, the outstanding balance falls after payments, so the interest calculated on that balance generally decreases over time.
