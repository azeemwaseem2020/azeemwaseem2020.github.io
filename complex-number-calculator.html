Quick guide: how to use this calculator

Enter the real and imaginary parts of the complex numbers, then choose the operation required by the calculator.

Formula or method

A complex number has the form z = a + bi, where i² = −1.

Useful note

Keep the real and imaginary parts separate; i represents the imaginary unit, not a second ordinary number.
