Body Fat Calculator

Estimate body fat percentage from body measurements using the U.S. Navy circumference method.

Method

For men, the U.S. Navy circumference equation uses height, waist and neck. For women it additionally uses hip circumference. Measurements should be taken consistently and the result is an estimate.

Important health note

This is an estimate for general informational use and is not a medical diagnosis. Measurement technique and individual body composition can affect accuracy.

Quick guide: how to use this calculator

Enter the measurements requested by the selected method and keep every measurement in the same units.

Formula or method

The calculator uses the body-fat estimation equation associated with the selected measurement method.

Useful note

Body-fat estimates can vary with measurement technique, hydration and the method used, so treat the result as an estimate.

Before you use the result

The answer depends on the values, units and assumptions entered. Check the method shown on this page before comparing the result with another calculator or source.
