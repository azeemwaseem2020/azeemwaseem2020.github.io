2026–27 salary tax slabs

Who qualifies for these slabs? The salaried-person rate table applies where income chargeable under the head “Salary” exceeds 75% of the individual’s taxable income, subject to the applicable law. If salary is 75% or less of taxable income, a different rate table may apply. Because this calculator accepts only one taxable-salary figure, it cannot determine the correct tax treatment for a mixed-income case. The calculator follows the salaried-person slabs for Tax Year 2027. FBR's Finance Bill 2026 lists the first bands as 0% up to PKR 600,000, 1% on the amount above PKR 600,000 up to PKR 1,200,000, and PKR 6,000 plus 11% of the amount above PKR 1,200,000 up to PKR 2,200,000.

How to read a Pakistan salary tax estimate

A salary tax calculator is most useful when you understand the difference between gross salary, taxable salary, tax withheld by an employer and the final annual tax liability. This tool starts from the annual taxable salary you enter and applies the tax-year assumptions shown on the page.

Gross salary is not always taxable salary

Gross pay can include salary, allowances, benefits and other components. Some items may be exempt, deductible or treated differently under the applicable rules. For that reason, entering take-home pay or a monthly bank credit can produce a misleading estimate. Use the taxable-income basis required by the tax year you are checking.

Marginal tax rate versus effective tax rate

The marginal rate applies to the relevant portion of income in the applicable slab. The effective rate is total estimated tax divided by taxable income. These percentages answer different questions and should not be confused when comparing salaries.

Worked example: PKR 1.5 million taxable salary

Suppose your annual taxable salary is PKR 1,500,000 for Tax Year 2027. This falls in the slab above PKR 1,200,000 and up to PKR 2,200,000. The calculation is:

Tax = PKR 6,000 + 11% × (PKR 1,500,000 − PKR 1,200,000)

= PKR 6,000 + 11% × PKR 300,000 = PKR 39,000 per year.

The simple monthly average is PKR 39,000 ÷ 12 = PKR 3,250 per month. The effective tax rate in this example is 2.6% of PKR 1,500,000. This is a worked mathematical example using the stated Tax Year 2027 slab; actual payroll withholding can differ when exemptions, deductions, credits, benefits, arrears or other taxable items apply.

Monthly salary example

If an employee receives a fixed monthly salary, multiply the monthly taxable amount by 12 to obtain a simple annual starting point, then account for any taxable or exempt components required by the applicable rules. The calculator then applies the selected tax-year slab structure and reports an annual result plus a simple monthly average.

Pakistan salary tax FAQ

How much tax is deducted on a PKR 100,000 monthly salary in Pakistan?

If the full PKR 100,000 monthly salary is taxable for all 12 months, annual taxable salary is PKR 1,200,000. Under the Tax Year 2027 slabs shown above, the estimated annual tax is PKR 6,000, or a simple monthly average of PKR 500: 1% × (PKR 1,200,000 − PKR 600,000). This is a slab-based estimate, not a guaranteed payslip deduction; taxable benefits, exemptions, credits, other income and payroll adjustments may change the result.

What is the difference between a filer and a non-filer in Pakistan?

A filer generally means a person whose tax return status qualifies them for the Federal Board of Revenue's Active Taxpayer List (ATL); a non-filer is not listed as active for the relevant purpose. Filer status does not, by itself, replace the salary tax slab calculation shown here. ATL status can affect withholding tax on other transactions—such as certain bank, property, vehicle or investment payments—where the law sets different ATL and non-ATL rates. Check the current FBR Tax Year 2027 withholding tax rate card for the specific transaction.

Is PKR 100,000 monthly salary tax-free?

Not completely under the slabs displayed for Tax Year 2027, if the full amount is taxable. The first PKR 600,000 of annual taxable income is taxed at 0%, while the next band is taxed at 1%. At PKR 100,000 per month (PKR 1,200,000 annually), that produces an estimated PKR 6,000 annual tax. Your actual taxable salary may differ from gross salary.

Should I calculate salary tax using gross salary or take-home pay?

Use annual taxable salary—not take-home pay after deductions. Gross salary, taxable salary and net pay can differ because allowances, benefits, exemptions, deductions and other payroll items may receive different treatment. If you do not know your taxable salary, check your payslip or ask your employer's payroll team before relying on the estimate.

Is this an official FBR tax assessment?

No. It is a calculator estimate based on the stated tax-year assumptions. An official return, assessment or employer payroll calculation can require additional facts.

Should I enter monthly or annual salary?

This calculator asks for annual taxable salary. If you start with a monthly figure, convert it to the appropriate annual taxable amount before entering it.

Do these slabs apply if I also have business or other income?

Not automatically. The applicable salaried-person rule generally requires salary income to exceed 75% of taxable income. This calculator does not compare your salary with your total taxable income, so mixed-income taxpayers should verify the applicable rate table with current FBR guidance or a qualified tax professional.

Why can my employer's monthly deduction differ?

Payroll withholding can reflect exemptions, benefits, credits, bonuses, arrears, prior withholding and employer-specific payroll treatment. The calculator reports a simple annual estimate divided by 12.

Can the tax slabs change?

Yes. Tax legislation and official guidance can change by tax year. Always verify the current Finance Act and FBR material when using the result for filing or a financial decision.

Official verification: Check the current FBR Finance Act 2026 page and the FBR Tax Year 2027 rate-card resources before relying on the estimate for payroll or filing.

Which salary components can affect Pakistan income tax?

For a reliable salary-tax estimate, separate gross pay from annual taxable salary. The name on a payslip does not by itself decide whether an amount is taxable: the applicable Income Tax Ordinance, the nature of the payment, any specific exemption and the employee’s circumstances matter. This calculator applies the slabs to one annual taxable-salary figure; it does not classify each payroll component for you.

Is a bonus taxable as salary in Pakistan?

An annual bonus, performance bonus, sales commission, incentive payment, overtime pay or arrears can change the total salary received during a tax year. Do not automatically calculate tax from basic salary alone if these amounts are part of your taxable employment income. Add the taxable amounts to the annual figure using the treatment that applies to your case, then estimate the tax for the full year. A one-off bonus can also make withholding in a particular payroll month differ from the calculator’s simple annual-tax-divided-by-12 average.

Are house rent, travel, utility and medical allowances taxable?

Review each allowance separately, including house-rent or accommodation allowance, cost-of-living allowance, utility allowance, travel or conveyance allowance, education allowance and medical allowance or reimbursement. An allowance paid as a fixed monthly amount is not automatically tax-free just because it is labelled “travel”, “fuel” or “medical”. The law’s conditions and any applicable exemption matter. A genuine reimbursement of business expenses incurred on the employer’s behalf may be treated differently from a fixed allowance paid as part of salary; retain the supporting policy, bills and payroll records and verify the applicable rule.

How is provident fund treated for salary tax?

Do not treat every provident-fund amount as one identical item. Distinguish your own contribution, the employer’s contribution, profit or interest credited to the fund, and any amount withdrawn or received when employment ends. The tax treatment can depend on the fund’s legal status, the relevant limits and conditions, and the type of receipt. A provident-fund balance or withdrawal should not simply be added to this calculator as ordinary monthly salary without checking the applicable provisions.

Do gratuity, pension or leave encashment belong in this salary calculator?

Gratuity, pension, leave encashment, compensation for termination, golden-handshake payments and other retirement-related receipts may follow rules that differ from routine monthly salary. Exemptions and special treatment, where available, depend on the facts and the governing provisions. If one of these payments is part of your annual tax position, do not assume this salary-only estimate covers it automatically.

Employer-provided benefits and expense reimbursements

Non-cash benefits—such as employer-provided accommodation, a vehicle available for private use, utilities or other perquisites—may need a valuation under the applicable tax rules. Reimbursements also need to be distinguished from personal expenses paid by the employer. Check how the benefit is shown on your salary certificate or annual tax statement before deciding whether it belongs in taxable salary.

Practical checklist: Compare your monthly payslips and annual salary certificate; identify basic pay, taxable allowances, bonus, commission, overtime, arrears and taxable benefits; then check provident-fund, gratuity or pension items separately. Use the resulting annual taxable salary in this calculator. For legal classification, review FBR’s Income Tax Ordinance resources and the current Finance Act 2026.
