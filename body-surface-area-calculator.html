Body Surface Area Calculator

Estimate body surface area from height and weight using the Mosteller BSA formula.

Mosteller BSA formula

The Mosteller formula is BSA = √[(height in cm × weight in kg) ÷ 3600]. The result is expressed in square metres (m²).

Worked example

For 175 cm and 70 kg, BSA = √(175×70÷3600) ≈ 1.844 m².

Why BSA is used

BSA is used in some clinical and research calculations. The appropriate equation and interpretation depend on the application, and a prescribed clinical protocol should take priority.

How to use the BSA calculator

FAQ

Why do BSA calculators differ? Different published equations and rounding methods can produce slightly different estimates.

Is BSA a direct measurement? No. It is an estimate derived from variables such as height and weight.
