Time-and-a-half pay

Time-and-a-half means 1.5 times the normal hourly rate. The overtime rate is regular hourly rate × 1.5. Overtime pay is then the overtime rate multiplied by eligible overtime hours, subject to the applicable employment rules.

Worked example

If the regular hourly rate is PKR 500, time-and-a-half is 500 × 1.5 = PKR 750 per overtime hour. Ten eligible overtime hours would produce PKR 7,500 of overtime pay before other payroll deductions.

Pakistan context

Overtime rules can depend on employment category, working hours, contracts and applicable labour law. The mathematical multiplier should not be treated as a legal determination of what an employer must pay in every situation.

FAQs

Is time-and-a-half 150%? Yes, it is 150% of the regular hourly rate. Is it the same as a 50% raise? For the overtime hours, the pay is 50% above the regular rate. Are taxes included? No; this is gross overtime pay.
