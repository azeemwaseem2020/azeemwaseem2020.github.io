Time-and-a-half pay

Time-and-a-half means 1.5 times the normal hourly rate. The overtime rate is regular hourly rate × 1.5. Overtime pay is then the overtime rate multiplied by eligible overtime hours, subject to the applicable employment rules.

Worked example

If the regular hourly rate is PKR 500, time-and-a-half is 500 × 1.5 = PKR 750 per overtime hour. Ten eligible overtime hours would produce PKR 7,500 of overtime pay before other payroll deductions.

Pakistan context

Overtime rules can depend on employment category, working hours, contracts and applicable labour law. The mathematical multiplier should not be treated as a legal determination of what an employer must pay in every situation.

FAQs

Is time-and-a-half 150%? Yes, it is 150% of the regular hourly rate. Is it the same as a 50% raise? For the overtime hours, the pay is 50% above the regular rate. Are taxes included? No; this is gross overtime pay.

Time-and-a-Half Pay Calculation

Time-and-a-half means an overtime rate equal to 1.5 times the regular hourly rate. If the normal rate is Rs 500 per hour, the overtime rate is Rs 500 × 1.5 = Rs 750 per hour. For 8 overtime hours, the overtime earnings are Rs 6,000 before any deductions.

When starting from an annual salary, first convert the salary to an hourly rate using the work schedule that applies to the employment arrangement. For example, an annual salary of Rs 1,200,000 divided by 2,400 scheduled hours gives Rs 500 per hour; time-and-a-half is then Rs 750. The correct divisor depends on the actual employment contract, working days and paid hours.

In Pakistan, overtime treatment can depend on the applicable employment arrangement, province, industry and contract. This calculator performs the arithmetic only; it does not determine whether a worker is legally entitled to a particular overtime multiplier. For payroll decisions, verify the applicable labour rules and company policy.

Example with regular and overtime hours

If an employee works 40 regular hours at Rs 500 and 6 overtime hours at Rs 750, gross weekly pay from those hours is Rs 20,000 + Rs 4,500 = Rs 24,500. This excludes bonuses, allowances, taxes and other deductions.
