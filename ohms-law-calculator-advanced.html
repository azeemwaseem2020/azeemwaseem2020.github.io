Formula

Ohm's law: V = I × R. Therefore I = V ÷ R and R = V ÷ I.

How to use the advanced Ohm's Law calculator

  1. Enter any two of voltage, current and resistance.
  2. Leave the unknown field empty.
  3. Select Solve and review all three values.

Worked example

With 12 V and 6 Ω, current is 2 A because I = V ÷ R.

FAQ

How many values do I enter? Exactly two positive values.

Can Ohm's law apply to every component? No. The simple linear relationship is appropriate for ohmic conditions; some components have non-linear behavior.
