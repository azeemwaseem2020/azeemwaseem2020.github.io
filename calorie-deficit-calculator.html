Before you use the result

The result depends on the inputs and assumptions shown on this page. Keep units consistent and verify important real-world figures against the relevant source or document.

What a calorie deficit means

A calorie deficit means estimated energy intake is lower than estimated energy expenditure over time. A calculator can estimate a target from the inputs you provide, but actual weight change is affected by activity, appetite, water balance and changes in energy expenditure.

Why a fixed deficit is not a guarantee

Energy needs can change as body weight, activity and routine change. Treat the result as a planning estimate rather than a promise of a specific rate of weight loss.

Calorie deficit FAQ

Is a larger deficit always better?

No. An aggressive restriction can be difficult to maintain and may not be appropriate for everyone. A suitable target depends on individual circumstances.

Why can scale weight change even without a calorie deficit?

Short-term weight changes can reflect water, glycogen, food volume and other factors in addition to changes in body fat.

Calorie deficit formula

A simple daily deficit is Maintenance calories − Intake calories. For example, estimated maintenance of 2,400 kcal and intake of 1,900 kcal gives a calculated deficit of 500 kcal/day.

Why the estimate can change

Energy needs are estimates and can change with body weight, activity, routine, appetite, and adaptation. Scale weight can also move because of water, glycogen and food volume, so a calculator cannot guarantee a specific rate of fat loss.

Practical interpretation

A calorie target should be considered alongside nutrition quality, activity and individual circumstances. Very large restrictions are not automatically better or appropriate for everyone.

FAQ

Is a 500-calorie deficit guaranteed to cause a specific weight loss?

No. Real-world weight change varies and the simple energy model is only an estimate.

Can maintenance calories change?

Yes. Changes in body size, activity and routine can change estimated energy needs.

Calculation context

A calorie-deficit estimate compares an estimated energy requirement with a chosen intake target. The calculation is a planning model; individual energy needs and appropriate targets vary and should be considered with relevant health guidance.
