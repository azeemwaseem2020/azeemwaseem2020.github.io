Quick guide: how to use this calculator

Enter estimated maintenance calories and the target deficit or weight-loss goal, then review the resulting daily calorie target.

Formula or method

Daily target = estimated maintenance calories − chosen deficit.

Useful note

Very large deficits are not automatically better; use a sensible target and consider individual health needs.

Before you use the result

The result depends on the inputs and assumptions shown on this page. Keep units consistent and verify important real-world figures against the relevant source or document.

How to use an estimated deficit over time

The calculator subtracts an intake target from an estimated maintenance value. Maintenance itself is not directly measured here; it may be inferred from an equation or prior tracking and can be inaccurate when activity, routine or body weight changes.

Review patterns over several weeks rather than treating one day's intake or scale reading as proof of success or failure. Water balance, glycogen, digestion, medication and other factors can shift scale weight independently of body-fat change. Reassess the assumptions when the estimate consistently differs from observed trends.

Keep health and sustainability in view

A numeric deficit is not a personalised medical plan. Avoid interpreting a larger number as automatically better, and seek qualified guidance if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, are under 18, or have a history of disordered eating. The tool does not assess nutritional adequacy or determine a safe target for an individual.

Calculation context

A calorie-deficit estimate compares an estimated energy requirement with a chosen intake target. The calculation is a planning model; individual energy needs and appropriate targets vary and should be considered with relevant health guidance.
