Pressure Calculator — Force, Area & Pressure

Calculate pressure, force or area using P = F/A with reverse solving, SI units, worked steps and practical checks.

Formula

P = F/A. Pressure is force distributed over an area. SI pressure is pascals (Pa).

Worked example

A 200 N force over 0.5 m² gives P = 200 ÷ 0.5 = 400 Pa.

Common mistakes

  • Using area in cm² without converting when a result in Pa is required.
  • Confusing force with pressure.
  • Using total surface area when the problem specifies the contact area.
