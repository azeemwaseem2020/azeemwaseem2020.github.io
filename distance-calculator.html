Distance Calculator

Calculate straight-line distance between two points on a coordinate plane.

Distance formula

For two positions on a straight number line, distance is the absolute difference: d = |x₂ − x₁|. The result is non-negative, so the order of the two positions does not change the distance.

What this calculator measures

This page calculates one-dimensional distance from two numeric positions. For a full 2D coordinate calculation, use the Distance Between Points Calculator.

Distance Calculator: Formula and Example

Distance depends on the type of problem. For constant speed, the basic relationship is distance = speed × time. This calculator is intended for straightforward distance calculations where the selected units are consistent.

Worked example

If a vehicle travels at 60 km/h for 2.5 hours, the estimated distance is 150 km. If time is entered in minutes, convert it to hours before using a speed expressed in km/h.

Units and conversions

Distance may be expressed in kilometers, miles, meters, feet, or other units. Speed and time must use compatible units. A result from a mathematical distance formula is not automatically a route distance or travel-time prediction.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the basic distance formula?

For constant speed, distance equals speed multiplied by time.

Does this account for traffic or stops?

No. The basic formula assumes the stated speed applies throughout the time period. Real travel can include stops, changing speed, and route differences.
