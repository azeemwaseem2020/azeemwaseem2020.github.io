Ideal gases

Ideal Gas Law Calculator solves for pressure, volume, temperature or amount of gas under the ideal-gas model. Use absolute temperature and compatible units.

Keep the units visible

Wave speed, frequency and wavelength are linked by v = fλ. If frequency is entered in hertz and wavelength in metres, the calculated speed is in metres per second. For heat, check whether the quantity is mass, specific heat, temperature change or energy before substituting numbers.

One quick wave example

If a wave has a frequency of 5 Hz and a wavelength of 2 m, its speed is 5 × 2 = 10 m/s. If you instead know speed and frequency, rearrange the same relationship to find wavelength.

Temperature deserves extra care

Gas-law calculations require absolute temperature, normally kelvin. A Celsius value should not be inserted directly into PV = nRT. This is a calculation issue, not a rounding issue, so check the temperature scale before anything else.
