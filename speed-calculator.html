Speed Calculator — Distance, Time & Average Speed

Calculate speed, distance or time using d = vt. Useful for introductory physics and everyday distance-and-time problems.

Formula

v = d ÷ t, with distance in metres and time in seconds for m/s.

Worked example

If an object travels 100 m in 10 s, its average speed is 100 ÷ 10 = 10 m/s.

Important distinction

Speed is a scalar quantity. Velocity includes direction and is handled by the Velocity Calculator.

Common mistakes

  • Using minutes or hours without converting when the result is required in m/s.
  • Confusing distance with displacement.
  • Dividing by zero time.
