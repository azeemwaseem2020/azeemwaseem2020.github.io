Frequently asked questions

How is the monthly auto loan payment calculated?

The estimate uses the loan principal, monthly interest rate and number of monthly payments. Taxes, fees and lender-specific charges are not included unless you add them to the financed amount.

Does a down payment reduce interest?

Yes. A larger down payment reduces the amount financed, which generally reduces the interest charged over the loan term.

Can I use this calculator for a car loan?

Yes. Auto loan and car loan payments use the same standard amortization formula when the rate and payment schedule are monthly.

How to compare car loan scenarios

  1. Enter the vehicle price and down payment.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate and loan term.
  3. Compare the estimated monthly payment, interest and total repayment.

A larger down payment usually reduces the amount financed. A longer term can reduce the monthly payment while increasing the total interest paid. Use the calculator to compare the trade-off rather than looking at the monthly payment alone.

Costs not included in the loan payment

Registration, insurance, maintenance, fuel, taxes, dealer fees and other ownership costs can materially change the cost of a vehicle. Check the lender's disclosure for the actual amount financed, APR and fees.
