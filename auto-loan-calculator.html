Formula & assumptions

Results are estimates based on the inputs and standard formulas shown or described on this page. Real-world rates, taxes, fees and eligibility rules can vary by country and provider.

Frequently asked questions

How is the monthly auto loan payment calculated?

The estimate uses the loan principal, monthly interest rate and number of monthly payments. Taxes, fees and lender-specific charges are not included unless you add them to the financed amount.

Does a down payment reduce interest?

Yes. A larger down payment reduces the amount financed, which generally reduces the interest charged over the loan term.

Can I use this calculator for a car loan?

Yes. Auto loan and car loan payments use the same standard amortization formula when the rate and payment schedule are monthly.

Auto loan formula

For a fixed-rate monthly loan, the payment is based on principal P, monthly rate r, and number of payments n: PMT = P·r(1+r)n / ((1+r)n − 1). If the rate is zero, payment = P ÷ n.

Worked example

A $25,000 financed balance at 8% annual interest for 5 years has a standard monthly payment of about $506.91, before any separate taxes, fees, insurance, or other charges.

What affects the payment?

Vehicle price, down payment, interest rate, loan term, fees, taxes, and the amount financed can all affect the actual cost. A longer term can lower the regular payment while increasing total interest.

FAQ

Is this the same as a car loan calculator?

Yes. Auto loan and car loan calculations use the same standard amortization model when the payment schedule and rate assumptions match.

Does a down payment reduce total interest?

A larger down payment reduces the financed principal, which generally reduces interest under the same rate and term.
