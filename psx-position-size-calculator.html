PSX Position Size Calculator Pakistan — Shares, Risk & Stop-Loss

Estimate a position size from the amount of capital you are willing to risk, your entry price, stop-loss price and target price.

Formula, example and guide

Formula

Position size is based on the amount you are willing to risk divided by the price difference between entry and stop-loss. The result is a planning quantity, not a recommendation to trade.

Worked example

If the planned risk is Rs 5,000 and the difference between entry and stop-loss is Rs 10 per share, the basic position size is 500 shares before considering fees, liquidity or account constraints.

How to use this calculator

Enter your account or risk amount, entry price and stop-loss level. Check the resulting position against your actual cash, trading rules, fees and market liquidity.

Plan the number of PSX shares

PSX position size search intent

This page targets PSX position size calculator, share sizing, risk-per-trade and stop-loss planning. Position size is calculated from the amount you choose to risk and the price distance to the stop-loss.

How position sizing works

The idea is simple: decide how much of your capital you are comfortable putting at risk, then work backwards from the entry and stop-loss prices. Maximum rupee risk = available capital × risk percentage. Risk per share = entry price − stop-loss price. The calculator divides those numbers and rounds down to a whole number of shares.

Quick example

If you enter PKR 100,000 as capital, set risk at 1%, buy at PKR 100 and use PKR 95 as the stop-loss, the maximum planned loss is PKR 1,000 and the risk per share is PKR 5. That gives 200 shares before considering brokerage, taxes and other trading costs.

Risk-to-reward ratio

Potential reward per share is target price − entry price. Risk-to-reward compares potential reward with the amount risked per share. It is a planning metric, not a prediction.

Important PSX limitations

Brokerage, taxes, slippage, liquidity, price gaps and execution can make actual results different from this arithmetic estimate. This tool does not recommend a security or tell you where to place a stop loss.

Common questions

What does position size mean?

It is the number of shares that fits a chosen risk amount and the distance between entry and stop-loss.

Does this tell me which PSX stock to buy?

No. It only does the arithmetic for the figures you enter.

Are brokerage and taxes included?

No. Keep those costs in mind when checking the final amount with your broker.

EXPERT VALIDATION LAYER

Execution-risk boundary

The mathematical position size is based on the entered capital, risk percentage, entry and stop. Real execution can differ because of liquidity, slippage, gaps and transaction costs.

What the calculator proves

The result is therefore a sizing calculation, not a statement that the calculated loss is guaranteed.

What must be verified externally

RISK-SIZING BOUNDARY

Make the position-size calculation traceable

The result follows a fixed arithmetic chain: capital × risk percentage → maximum rupee risk → entry-to-stop distance → whole-share quantity. This makes the result easy to audit without turning the calculator into an investment recommendation.

Risk amount

Maximum planned risk is calculated from the capital and percentage you enter.

Risk per share

The entry price minus the stop-loss price defines the assumed rupee risk per share.

Whole shares

The calculator rounds down to a whole number so the calculated position does not exceed the mathematical risk limit before other costs.

Execution check
