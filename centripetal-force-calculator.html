Centripetal Force Calculator — Circular Motion

Calculate the inward net force required for uniform circular motion from mass, speed and radius.

Formula

Fc = mv²/r. The centripetal force is directed toward the centre of the circular path.

Worked example

For m = 5 kg, v = 10 m/s and r = 2 m, Fc = 5×100÷2 = 250 N.

Important concept

Centripetal force is not a separate fundamental force. It describes the net inward force supplied by gravity, tension, friction, a normal force or another physical interaction.

Assumptions

This calculator uses the uniform-circular-motion relationship. It does not model changing speed or non-circular trajectories.

Core relationship

Centripetal force is the inward net force required for circular motion. Its magnitude depends on mass, speed and radius. It is not a new type of force; tension, friction, gravity or another physical force can provide it.

Common mistake

Do not add “centripetal force” as an extra force on a free-body diagram. Identify the actual inward force or combination of forces first.
