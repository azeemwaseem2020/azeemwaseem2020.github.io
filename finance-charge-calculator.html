How to use the finance charge calculator

Enter the balance, annual rate and number of days. The calculator estimates the charge with a simple daily-rate model.

Formula and method

Finance charge = Balance × annual rate × days ÷ 365. The annual percentage is converted to a decimal before calculation.

Worked example

For a balance of 10,000 at 12% per year for 30 days, the estimated charge is about 98.63 using a 365-day basis.

Important limitations

Actual products may use average daily balances, different day-count conventions, promotional rates, transaction-level calculations, fees or other contractual rules. This tool does not model those product-specific terms.

Common mistakes

  • Using the wrong balance when the account uses an average daily balance.
  • Assuming every lender or card issuer uses a 365-day basis.
  • Ignoring separate fees or taxes shown on a statement.
