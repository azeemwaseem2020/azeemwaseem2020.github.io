What the result means

Completed years are the person’s full age. The remaining months and days show the additional time since the most recent birthday. Total days are useful when an exact day count is needed, but calendar-based age is normally the clearest way to state age.

Frequently asked questions

Can this calculator account for leap years?

It calculates age from the selected dates using calendar dates, so leap days and different month lengths are handled by the date logic rather than assuming every year has 365 days.

Can I calculate age on a past or future date?

Yes. Enter the birth date and the date on which you want to calculate the age.

Why can age in years and total days differ?

Years and months are calendar-based, while total days counts the exact elapsed days between the selected dates.
