How to use the age calculator

Enter the date of birth and the date you want to compare it with. The calculator reports completed years, months and days and also shows total elapsed days.

Why calendar age is used

Calendar age accounts for different month lengths and leap years. Dividing total days by 365 is only an approximation.

Age calculation method

Calendar age is determined by comparing the year, month and day of the birth date with the target date. This avoids treating every year as exactly 365 days and handles different month lengths.

Worked example

A person born on 15 March 2000 is exactly 26 years old on 15 March 2026. On 14 March 2026, the completed age is still 25 years.

Important date considerations

Leap years and month lengths can affect total elapsed days. For official eligibility, legal age, benefits, or application deadlines, use the date convention specified by the relevant organization.

How to read years, months and days

The result shows completed calendar years first, followed by the remaining months and days. For example, someone born on 10 January who is checked on 9 February has not completed one calendar month yet, even though several weeks have passed. Calendar arithmetic is more meaningful for birthdays than converting every elapsed day into a fraction of a 365-day year.

Choosing the correct comparison date

Use the current date for everyday age checks, or enter a future or past date when planning an eligibility deadline, school admission, retirement milestone or event. The result is only as accurate as the dates entered. Check the day and month carefully, and make sure the target date is not earlier than the date of birth. If a form or authority uses a special rule for a birthday that falls on 29 February, follow that authority's stated convention.

Elapsed days versus calendar age

Total elapsed days are useful for tracking durations, but they do not replace completed years, months and days. Months have different lengths, and leap years add an extra calendar day. For legal, medical, immigration or official eligibility decisions, treat this calculator as a general estimate and confirm the applicable rules with the responsible organization.

FAQ

Can I calculate age on a specific date?

Yes. Enter that date in the target-date field.

Does dividing days by 365 give exact age?

No. It is only an approximation because calendar years can contain 365 or 366 days.
