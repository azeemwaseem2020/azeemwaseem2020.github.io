How to use the age calculator

Enter the date of birth and the date you want to compare it with. The calculator reports completed years, months and days and also shows total elapsed days.

Why calendar age is used

Calendar age accounts for different month lengths and leap years. Dividing total days by 365 is only an approximation.

Age calculation method

Calendar age is determined by comparing the year, month and day of the birth date with the target date. This avoids treating every year as exactly 365 days and handles different month lengths.

Worked example

A person born on 15 March 2000 is exactly 26 years old on 15 March 2026. On 14 March 2026, the completed age is still 25 years.

Important date considerations

Leap years and month lengths can affect total elapsed days. For official eligibility, legal age, benefits, or application deadlines, use the date convention specified by the relevant organization.

FAQ

Can I calculate age on a specific date?

Yes. Enter that date in the target-date field.

Does dividing days by 365 give exact age?

No. It is only an approximation because calendar years can contain 365 or 366 days.
