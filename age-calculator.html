What the result means

Completed years are the person’s full age. The remaining months and days show the additional time since the most recent birthday. Total days are useful when an exact day count is needed, but calendar-based age is normally the clearest way to state age.

Useful for

You can use the result for birthday planning, forms, eligibility checks and general date calculations. For an official age requirement, follow the definition and reference date specified by the relevant organisation.

How the exact age calculation works

Enter the birth date and the date you want to calculate age on. The calculator works through calendar years, months and days rather than treating every year as exactly 365 days.

What you can use it for

  • Elapsed days between two dates

Calendar calculations can be affected by month lengths and leap years, so an exact calendar-age result is different from simply dividing a number of days by 365.
