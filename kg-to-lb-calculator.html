Convert kilograms (kg) to pounds (lb) using the standard international conversion factor.

Kg to lb formula

1 kilogram = 2.2046226218 pounds. Therefore, pounds = kilograms × 2.2046226218.

Conversion examples

1 kg = 2.2046 lb. 5 kg ≈ 11.0231 lb. 10 kg ≈ 22.0462 lb. To convert pounds back to kilograms, divide pounds by 2.2046226218.

Why kg and lb are different

The kilogram is the SI base unit of mass, while the pound is commonly used as a mass unit in the US customary system. Weight is often used colloquially for mass, but the physical concepts are not identical.

Practical uses

This conversion is useful for body-weight records, shipping, recipes, fitness, luggage, product specifications, and international unit conversion.
