What this salary calculator shows

The result is a gross-pay conversion. It does not automatically deduct income tax, social contributions, benefits, retirement contributions or other payroll deductions.

Formula

Hourly pay = annual salary ÷ (hours per week × paid weeks). Weekly pay = hourly pay × weekly hours. Monthly equivalent = annual salary ÷ 12.

How salary conversion works

This calculator converts a gross annual salary into equivalent monthly, weekly, daily and hourly figures using the work schedule you enter. The result is a mathematical pay conversion, not a payslip or a take-home-pay estimate.

Why the work schedule matters

Hourly pay depends on the number of paid working hours in the year. If two people receive the same annual salary but work different hours per week or different numbers of paid weeks, their equivalent hourly rates will be different. Enter the schedule that best matches the employment arrangement you are comparing.

Worked example

For an annual gross salary of 1,200,000 with 40 hours per week and 52 paid weeks, the calculator treats the year as 2,080 paid hours. The equivalent hourly rate is 576.92, while the monthly equivalent is 100,000. These figures are before tax and other deductions.

Gross salary is not take-home pay

A gross salary conversion does not automatically account for income tax, provident-fund contributions, insurance, benefits, unpaid leave, bonuses, overtime rules or other payroll adjustments. For Pakistan income-tax calculations, use the dedicated Pakistan Salary Tax Calculator and verify the applicable tax year and rules.

Comparing job offers correctly

When comparing two offers, look beyond the headline annual salary. Check working hours, paid weeks, bonuses, allowances, employer benefits and recurring deductions. A higher annual figure does not necessarily produce a higher hourly rate or higher take-home pay.

Salary calculator FAQ

Does this calculate net salary?

No. It converts gross pay between common time periods and does not apply payroll deductions.

How is hourly salary calculated?

Hourly pay is annual gross salary divided by paid hours in the year. Paid hours are weekly hours multiplied by paid weeks.

Why is daily pay based on five days?

The current calculator uses a five-day working week for its daily equivalent. If your schedule differs, use the hourly or weekly figure as the more direct comparison.

Can I use Pakistani rupees?

Yes. The arithmetic is currency-neutral. Enter the salary in PKR when working with Pakistani employment figures and keep the same currency throughout the comparison.
