Salary Calculator

Estimate equivalent annual, monthly, weekly, daily and hourly pay from a gross salary and work schedule.

What this salary calculator shows

The result is a gross-pay conversion. It does not automatically deduct income tax, social contributions, benefits, retirement contributions or other payroll deductions.

Formula

Hourly pay = annual salary ÷ (hours per week × paid weeks). Weekly pay = hourly pay × weekly hours. Monthly equivalent = annual salary ÷ 12.

How to use the salary calculator

  1. Enter the pay amount and choose whether it is annual, monthly, weekly, daily or hourly.
  2. Set the working schedule when the calculation requires it.
  3. Review the equivalent pay periods and check the assumptions.

Gross pay is not take-home pay

A salary conversion can show equivalent gross earnings, but actual take-home pay depends on taxes, social contributions, benefits, deductions, overtime and local payroll rules. Use a dedicated tax calculator when you need a net-pay estimate.

Why hourly and annual pay can differ

An annual salary divided by a simple 52-week or 2,080-hour assumption is only an estimate. Paid leave, unpaid time, working days and contracted hours can change the effective hourly rate.

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculate net salary?

No. Basic salary conversion is different from a tax calculation. Deductions and local payroll rules must be considered separately.

Why does hourly pay depend on working hours?

Because annual salary converted to hourly pay depends on the number of paid working hours used in the assumption.
