Understanding salary calculations

A salary calculator can translate a gross pay figure into a useful monthly, annual or hourly estimate depending on the inputs. Gross salary is not the same as take-home pay: taxes, employee contributions, benefits, unpaid leave and other payroll deductions can change the amount actually received.

Formula

Annual salary can be derived from periodic pay by multiplying by the number of pay periods. Monthly equivalent = annual salary ÷ 12. An hourly equivalent requires a working-hours assumption, such as annual salary ÷ annual working hours.

Worked example

A gross monthly salary of Rs 150,000 corresponds to Rs 1,800,000 over 12 equal months before deductions. If the working-year assumption is 2,080 hours, the approximate hourly equivalent is Rs 865.38.

Pakistan-specific example

For a Pakistani employee, gross salary may include allowances and other components that have different tax or payroll treatment. For current salary-tax calculations, use the dedicated Pakistan Salary Tax Calculator and verify applicable FBR rules.

Common questions

Is this take-home salary? Not unless deductions are explicitly modelled.

Are bonuses included? Only if included in the input.

Can salary be compared with freelance income? Yes as a starting point, but include benefits, downtime and business costs.

Why can payroll differ? Deductions and employer-specific rules can change net pay.
