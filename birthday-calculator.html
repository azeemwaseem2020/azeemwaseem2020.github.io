How birthday age is calculated

Calendar age is based on completed years, months and days between the birth date and the calculation date. Month lengths and leap years matter, so a simple division by 365 is only an approximation.

Worked example

A person born on June 15, 1995 reaches age 30 on June 15, 2025. The exact years, months and days depend on the date on which the calculation is made.

How a birthday calculation works

A birthday calculator compares calendar dates rather than simply subtracting two numbers of days. The result can include completed years, months and days, or the number of days until a future birthday. Calendar length varies by month and leap years, so a simple “365 days per year” shortcut is not always exact.

Worked example

Suppose a person was born on 15 March 2000 and the calculation date is 2 October 2026. The completed age is 26 years. From 15 March to 2 October is another 6 months and 17 days, so the calendar age is 26 years, 6 months and 17 days. The exact day count depends on the calendar dates included in the calculation.

A practical Pakistan example

For a school admission form, job application or identity-document check in Pakistan, the useful question may be whether someone has reached a particular age on a specific date. Entering the actual date of birth and the relevant reference date is safer than estimating from the birth year alone. For travel, examinations or official applications, always follow the age definition used by the organisation.

Leap years and birthdays

Leap years add an extra calendar day in February. A birthday calculator should therefore work from actual dates instead of assuming every year has exactly 365 days. Someone born on 29 February also needs a clear convention when the target year is not a leap year; organisations may define the relevant birthday differently for legal or administrative purposes.

Birthday calculator FAQ

Can I calculate age on a past date? Yes. Use that date as the reference date when the calculator supports it.

Why can an age in days differ from years × 365? Leap years and the exact dates of months change the total number of elapsed days.

Can this determine legal age? It can calculate the calendar age, but legal eligibility depends on the rule and jurisdiction involved.

What about 29 February birthdays? Use the actual birth date and check the relevant organisation's convention for non-leap years.
