How birthday age is calculated

Calendar age is based on completed years, months and days between the birth date and the calculation date. Month lengths and leap years matter, so a simple division by 365 is only an approximation.

Worked example

A person born on June 15, 1995 reaches age 30 on June 15, 2025. The exact years, months and days depend on the date on which the calculation is made.

Next birthday planning

The weekday of a birthday can be determined from the calendar date. A separate countdown can measure the days remaining until the next occurrence.

FAQ

Does a leap year affect age? Calendar calculations account for leap-year dates and month lengths.

Is this the same as a date-difference calculator? It uses calendar dates for age and birthday information, while a date-difference tool may focus only on elapsed time.
