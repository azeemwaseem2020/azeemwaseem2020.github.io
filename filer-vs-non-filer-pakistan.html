Filer vs Non-Filer Pakistan

“Filer” and “non-filer” are common terms in Pakistan. The practical question is whether a taxpayer appears on the current Active Taxpayer List (ATL) and which rule applies to the transaction.

What is a filer in Pakistan?

For everyday tax planning, a filer generally means a taxpayer whose name appears on the relevant Active Taxpayer List. FBR publishes and updates the income-tax ATL and provides official ways to check taxpayer status.

Filer vs non-filer: what can change?

Filer status can affect withholding or advance-tax treatment for some transactions. FBR's ATL guidance lists differences involving areas such as bank profits and cash withdrawals, motor-vehicle registration or transfer, property transactions, securities, dividends and prize bonds. The exact rate is not universal: it depends on the transaction, taxpayer category and rules in force at the time.

This is why a single “non-filer tax rate” is not a reliable shortcut. Identify the transaction first and verify the current official rule.

How to check filer status

  1. Use FBR's official Active Taxpayer verification service.
  2. Check that the status is current for the relevant transaction date.
  3. If the status is unexpected, review your return-filing and ATL position through the official FBR system.
