Torque Calculator — Moment of Force

Calculate torque from force, perpendicular lever-arm distance and angle between the lever arm and force.

Formula

τ = rF sinθ. Torque is measured in newton-metres (N·m).

Worked example

A 50 N force applied 0.4 m from a pivot at 90° produces τ = 0.4×50×1 = 20 N·m.

Why angle matters

Only the component of force perpendicular to the lever arm produces this torque magnitude. At 90° the torque magnitude is greatest for fixed r and F.

How torque is interpreted

Torque magnitude depends on force, the perpendicular lever arm and the angle between the force and position vector. The SI unit is newton-metre (N·m).

Practical check

A force applied farther from the pivot can produce more torque. A force directed through the pivot produces zero torque about that pivot because its perpendicular lever arm is zero.

Torque calculation context

Torque measures the turning effect of a force and depends on force, lever-arm distance and the angle between them. It belongs to rotational mechanics rather than ordinary linear-force calculation.
