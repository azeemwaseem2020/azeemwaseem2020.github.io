Torque Calculator — Moment of Force

Calculate torque from force, perpendicular lever-arm distance and angle between the lever arm and force.

Formula

τ = rF sinθ. Torque is measured in newton-metres (N·m).

Worked example

A 50 N force applied 0.4 m from a pivot at 90° produces τ = 0.4×50×1 = 20 N·m.

Why angle matters

Only the component of force perpendicular to the lever arm produces this torque magnitude. At 90° the torque magnitude is greatest for fixed r and F.
