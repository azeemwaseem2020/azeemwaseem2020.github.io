UAN 28% and 32% Fertilizer Calculator Guide

UAN looks simple on a spreadsheet, but the unit choice matters. Start with the nitrogen target, use the analysis on the actual product, and only then convert the result to litres, gallons or a field total.

What is UAN?

Urea-ammonium nitrate solution is commonly sold in 28% and 32% nitrogen grades. Extension guidance describes UAN as a mixture containing urea, ammonium and nitrate nitrogen. citeturn0search3turn0search10

Basic UAN rate formula

For a dry-weight calculation, fertilizer required = nitrogen required ÷ nitrogen fraction. For example, a target of 56 kg N/ha using UAN 28% gives 56 ÷ 0.28 = 200 kg of UAN product per hectare.

28% versus 32%

At the same nitrogen target, 32% UAN requires less product by weight than 28% UAN. The calculator should use the exact analysis printed on the product label rather than assuming a grade.

Field quantity and units

  1. Choose acres or hectares.
  2. Enter the nitrogen target for the same area basis.
  3. Enter UAN analysis, such as 28 or 32% N.
  4. Calculate total product required.
  5. Convert the result using the actual product density if a liquid-volume rate is needed.

Why density matters for gallons or litres

Liquid UAN is often purchased and applied by volume. A mass calculation cannot safely be converted to litres or gallons without the product density. Do not use a generic density when the supplier label or local specification provides a product-specific value.

Application and nitrogen-loss considerations

UAN can be dribbled, sprayed or injected. Application method affects nitrogen-loss risk and performance, and local soil and weather conditions matter. Extension guidance notes that injection can reduce volatilization risk compared with surface application. citeturn0search3turn0search10

UAN plus sulfur

UAN may be combined with sulfur sources such as ammonium thiosulfate in some systems. Sulfur sources differ in nutrient concentration and behavior, so calculate the nitrogen and sulfur contributions separately and follow product compatibility and application guidance. citeturn0search8

Worked example

Suppose the target is 84 kg N/ha and the selected product is UAN 28%. Product required = 84 ÷ 0.28 = 300 kg/ha. For 5 hectares, total product = 1,500 kg. The volume equivalent must then be calculated from the actual density of the product.

What Calcora calculates

Calcora can calculate nutrient supply, product quantity, field totals, unit conversions, bag or package equivalents and cost when the required inputs are supplied. It does not decide the correct nitrogen target for a crop or replace local agronomic recommendations.

Before you trust the number

Do one quick back-check: multiply the calculated product rate by the nutrient fraction and make sure it returns the intended nutrient rate. Then check the area conversion and the bag or volume conversion separately. Keeping those steps separate makes a wrong unit much easier to spot.

Common mistakes

  • Entering 28 instead of 0.28 in a manual formula.
  • Mixing kg/ha and kg/acre.
  • Converting liquid mass to volume without density.
  • Ignoring nitrogen already supplied by another fertilizer.
  • Treating a calculator result as a crop recommendation.

FAQ

How much UAN 28% supplies 28 kg N?

100 kg of a 28% N product supplies 28 kg N.

How much UAN 32% supplies 32 kg N?

100 kg of a 32% N product supplies 32 kg N.

Can UAN be calculated in gallons?

Yes, but a reliable volume result requires the actual product density and consistent units.
