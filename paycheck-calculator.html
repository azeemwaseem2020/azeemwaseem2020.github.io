Gross pay versus take-home pay

A paycheck calculation starts with gross earnings and subtracts applicable deductions. Gross pay can include salary, hourly wages, overtime, bonuses or allowances. Net pay is what remains after taxes and other deductions. The exact deductions depend on employment terms and applicable law.

Worked example

If gross monthly pay is PKR 100,000 and total modeled deductions are PKR 12,000, the estimated take-home amount is PKR 88,000. This example is purely mathematical; actual payroll deductions depend on the employee's circumstances.

Pakistan context

For Pakistani employees, income-tax treatment depends on the applicable tax year and employment income rules. Other deductions may include provident-fund contributions, advances or employer-specific benefits. Use the calculator as an estimate and verify payroll figures against the employer's payslip.

FAQs

Is gross salary the same as cash received? No. Can bonuses change tax? They may affect taxable income depending on the applicable rules. Why can two employees with the same gross salary receive different net pay? Their deductions and benefits can differ.

Gross pay and take-home pay

A paycheck estimate separates gross earnings from deductions. Gross pay can include salary, overtime, allowances and bonuses. Deductions may include tax, contributions, benefits or other authorized amounts.

Worked example

If monthly gross salary is PKR 150,000 and modeled deductions total PKR 18,000, estimated take-home pay is PKR 132,000. This is arithmetic, not a statement of the exact Pakistani payroll tax for a particular employee.

Pakistan context

Actual withholding depends on the applicable FBR tax year, taxable income and employee circumstances. Bonuses and allowances may have specific treatment, so annual taxable income should not automatically be estimated by multiplying one monthly deduction.
