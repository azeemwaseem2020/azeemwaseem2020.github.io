What this loan-interest calculator is for

This page focuses on the interest and total borrowing cost of a fixed-rate amortizing loan. Use the EMI calculator when your main question is the regular installment, and the loan comparison tool when you need to evaluate two offers side by side.

Loan interest formula

For a fixed-rate amortizing loan, M = P·r(1+r)n / ((1+r)n−1). Total interest = M × n − P, where P is principal, r is monthly rate and n is number of payments.

Worked example

For 500,000 at 10% annual interest over 5 years, the monthly rate is 10% ÷ 12 and n = 60. The calculator uses these values to estimate payment, total repayment and interest.

How to use

  2. Enter annual interest rate.
  3. Enter the term in years.
  4. Review monthly payment and total interest.

Interest and total-cost method

For a fixed-rate amortizing loan, the payment is calculated from principal, periodic rate and number of payments. Total interest is the sum of scheduled payments minus principal. Fees or charges outside the financed amount are not automatically included. Review units and real-world rules before using a result for financial, engineering or business decisions.

Calculation transparency

The calculator converts the annual rate to a monthly rate, uses the stated number of monthly payments, and applies the standard fixed-rate amortization formula. Total interest is scheduled payments minus principal.

Important assumptions

This model assumes a fixed rate and regular payments. It does not automatically include processing fees, insurance, taxes, late charges, early-settlement costs or a variable markup structure.

Verify before relying on the result

Check the lender's rate basis, payment frequency, term and fee schedule. For an actual offer, the documented repayment schedule is more authoritative than a generic calculator estimate.
