Percentage formulas

Percent of a number: (percent ÷ 100) × value. Percentage change: ((new − original) ÷ original) × 100. Keep the original value clear when calculating a change.

Percentage calculations made simple

Percentage questions usually fall into a few different patterns: finding a percentage of a number, finding what percentage one number is of another, calculating a percentage increase or decrease, or reversing a percentage change. Choosing the correct pattern matters more than the arithmetic itself.

Percentage of a number

To find x% of y, convert x to a decimal and multiply by y. For example, 15% of 200 is 0.15 × 200 = 30. This is useful for discounts, taxes, commissions, tips and many everyday comparisons.

Percentage change

Percentage change compares the difference with the original value. The direction matters: an increase from 80 to 100 is calculated relative to 80, while a decrease from 100 to 80 is calculated relative to 100. The same absolute difference can therefore produce different percentage changes.
