Common percentage calculations

Percentage questions can look similar but use different formulas. This calculator handles what is X% of Y. If you need to find what percentage one number is of another, compare an original and new value for percentage change, calculate a percentage increase or decrease, or find percentage difference, use the dedicated tool for that calculation rather than forcing the wrong formula onto the inputs.

What is X% of Y?

Example: what is 15% of 200? Enter 15 and 200 to get 30.

What percent is A of B?

This is a different problem: divide A by B and multiply by 100.

Percentage increase or decrease

Compare an original value with a new value when you need the relative change.

Percentage difference

Use the appropriate difference formula when neither value is the reference value.

Percentage formula

X% of Y = (X ÷ 100) × Y

How to calculate a percentage of a number

  1. Enter the percentage you want to find.
  2. Enter the number you want to calculate it from.
  3. Divide the percentage by 100.
  4. Multiply the result by the number.

Worked example

15% of 200 = (15 ÷ 100) × 200 = 30.

Common uses

Percentages are useful for discounts, markups, commissions, tips, tax estimates, exam scores and everyday comparisons. For regulated taxes or financial decisions, verify the applicable current rate separately.

What this calculator does

This tool calculates X% of Y. It does not currently calculate percentage change or determine what percentage one number is of another; those are different percentage problems that require different inputs.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate X% of a number?

Divide X by 100 and multiply by the number. For example, 15% of 200 is 30.

What is the formula for percentage?

For X% of Y, the formula is (X ÷ 100) × Y.

Can this calculator find percentage change?

No. This page currently focuses on finding X% of Y. Percentage change uses different inputs.

Practical percentage examples

This calculator is useful when you already know the percentage and want to find that portion of a number. For example, you can use it to check a discount amount, estimate a tip, calculate a commission, or work out a percentage of a total.

Try these examples

  • What is 20% of 500? The result is 100.
  • What is 7.5% of 240? The result is 18.
  • What is 12% of 1,250? The result is 150.
