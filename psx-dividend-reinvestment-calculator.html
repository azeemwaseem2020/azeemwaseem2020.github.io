PSX Dividend Reinvestment Calculator — Shares, Income & Growth Scenario

Model how repeated dividend reinvestment could change share count and value when the dividend per share and reinvestment price are held constant.

Formula, example and guide

Formula

Dividend reinvestment is calculated by converting the dividend cash into additional shares at the assumed reinvestment price, then adding those shares to the existing holding.

Worked example

Suppose an investor owns 1,000 shares and receives a Rs 5 dividend per share. A Rs 5,000 dividend reinvested at Rs 100 per share buys 50 additional shares before considering taxes, fees or fractional-share rules.

How to use this calculator

Enter the current holding, dividend per share, reinvestment price and any relevant assumptions. Use different prices to compare scenarios rather than treating one projection as a guaranteed return.

PSX dividend calculator search intent

This tool covers PSX dividend reinvestment, dividend growth scenarios, added shares and estimated ending value. The model is scenario-based and should not be treated as a forecast of future dividends or share prices.

How the model works

Each period calculates dividend cash from the current share count, subtracts the entered withholding rate, then uses the remaining cash to buy additional shares at the entered reinvestment price. Fractional shares are allowed in the model so the compounding effect can be shown clearly.

A simple way to think about reinvesting dividends

Reinvesting a dividend means using the cash payout to buy more shares. If those extra shares later receive dividends too, the share count can grow over time. The calculator lets you see that effect using your own assumptions.

Why reinvestment can matter

When dividends are reinvested, future dividends can be calculated on a larger share count. The effect depends on the actual dividend, share price, timing, taxes and whether dividends are paid consistently.

Common questions

Does this predict my future dividend income?

No. It is a scenario calculation. Future dividends and share prices can change.

Can I enter a different reinvestment price?

Yes. Use the share price at which you want to model each reinvestment scenario.

Does the calculator include dividend tax?

You can enter a withholding percentage. Actual tax treatment can depend on your circumstances and current rules.

Important limitation

This is a scenario model, not a forecast. Dividend declarations and share prices change, and actual broker rules may not permit the exact fractional-share treatment shown here.
