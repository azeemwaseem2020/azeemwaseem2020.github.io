Finding a percentage of a value

To find a percentage of a value, use result = percentage ÷ 100 × value. For example, 15% of 200 is 0.15 × 200 = 30.

Worked example

Suppose a Rs. 80,000 purchase has a 7.5% fee. The fee is 0.075 × 80,000 = Rs. 6,000. The final amount depends on whether the fee is added to or deducted from the original value.

A practical Pakistan example

A shopkeeper or small business owner may use the calculation to estimate a percentage-based commission, discount or service charge. Always check the written terms to determine which base amount the percentage applies to.

Common mistakes

Convert the percentage to a decimal before multiplying, and distinguish “15% of 200” from “200 increased by 15%.”

FAQ

What is 20% of 500? 100.

Does adding 10% and then subtracting 10% return to the original? No. The second 10% is applied to a different base.

Can the percentage be above 100%? Mathematically yes, when the context allows it.

Percentage from a known value

To find a percentage of a value, convert the percentage to a decimal and multiply. For example, 15% of PKR 20,000 is 0.15 × 20,000 = PKR 3,000. This is different from finding what percentage one number represents of another, which uses division.

For Pakistan budgeting, discounts, salary adjustments and tax estimates, confirm whether the percentage is applied to the original amount or to a previously adjusted amount. Applying 10% twice is not the same as applying a single 20% increase.
