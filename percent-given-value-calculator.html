What percent is one value of another? Formula and examples

This calculator answers the question “A is what percent of B?” It compares a part or amount with a reference whole. The result can be below 100%, equal to 100%, or above 100% depending on the two values.

Formula

Percentage = (A ÷ B) × 100. A is the value being compared and B is the reference value. The reference value must not be zero because division by zero is undefined.

Worked examples

If A = 25 and B = 200, then (25 ÷ 200) × 100 = 12.5%. If A = 150 and B = 100, the result is 150%, which means A is one and a half times B. The calculator displays the result to four decimal places, so trailing digits may be rounded.

How to use the calculator

  1. Enter the amount you want to compare as A.
  2. Enter the reference or total amount as B.
  3. Choose values in compatible units, such as dollars compared with dollars or kilograms compared with kilograms.
  4. Select Calculate and interpret the percentage in context.

Common mistakes and interpretation

Make sure A and B refer to the intended quantities and use compatible units. Comparing 500 grams with 2 kilograms directly would be misleading unless you first convert them to the same unit. A percentage above 100% is valid and means the first value exceeds the reference value. If B is negative, the mathematical result can be calculated, but whether it is meaningful depends on the context—for example, financial changes and signed measurements need careful interpretation.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find what percent 30 is of 120?

Divide 30 by 120 and multiply by 100. The result is 25%, because 30 is one quarter of 120.

Why can the answer be greater than 100%?

If A is larger than B and B is positive, the percentage exceeds 100%. For example, 150 compared with 100 is 150%.

Is this the same as calculating a percentage of a value?

No. “What percent is A of B?” finds the percentage when both values are known. “What is 20% of B?” finds an amount from a known percentage and whole. Choose the calculation based on the missing value.
