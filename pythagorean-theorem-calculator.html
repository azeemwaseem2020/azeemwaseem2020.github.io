Pythagorean Theorem Calculator

Find the hypotenuse or a missing leg of a right triangle and see the calculation steps.

Find a missing side

Pythagorean theorem formula

For a right triangle, a² + b² = c², where c is the hypotenuse. If c is unknown, c = √(a² + b²). If one leg is unknown, a = √(c² − b²).

How to use the calculator

  1. Enter the two known sides.
  2. Leave c blank to calculate the hypotenuse.
  3. Or enter a valid hypotenuse to calculate the missing leg.
  4. Review the result and calculation steps.

Worked example

With legs 3 and 4, 3² + 4² = 9 + 16 = 25, so c = √25 = 5.

How to find a missing leg

If the hypotenuse and one leg are known, rearrange the theorem: the missing leg equals the square root of the hypotenuse squared minus the known leg squared. The hypotenuse must be longer than either leg.

Common uses

The theorem is useful in geometry, construction measurements, school mathematics and distance problems where two perpendicular sides form a right triangle.

Important limitation

This calculator assumes a right triangle. It does not solve general triangles; those require other geometry methods such as the law of sines or law of cosines.
