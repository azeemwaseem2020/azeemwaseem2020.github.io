Amps to watts formula

Electrical power depends on current, voltage and the circuit's power factor. For a DC circuit, watts = volts × amps. For single-phase AC, real power can be estimated as volts × amps × power factor. Three-phase systems require the appropriate three-phase relationship.

Worked example

At 230 V and 5 A in a DC or unity-power-factor example, power is 1,150 W. If the power factor is below 1 in an AC load, real power is lower than the simple volts × amps apparent-power figure.

Amps to watts FAQ

Can I convert amps to watts without voltage?

No. Voltage is required, and AC real-power calculations may also require power factor and phase information.

Why does an appliance rating differ from the calculation?

Appliances can have changing loads, power-factor effects, starting current and manufacturer-specific operating conditions.
