Amps to Watts Calculator

Convert amps to watts using voltage.

Enter values and calculate.

How to use

Enter the current in amps and voltage in volts, then select Calculate. For a simple DC load, power is voltage multiplied by current.

Formula and notes

For DC, P = V × I. For AC loads, real power can depend on power factor; for a single-phase load, a common relationship is P = V × I × PF.

Example

At 230 V and 5 A, the simple power calculation is 1,150 W. Actual AC real power can be lower when power factor is below 1.

Quick guide: how to use this calculator

Useful note
