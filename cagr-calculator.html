Frequently asked questions

What is CAGR?

CAGR, or compound annual growth rate, is the constant annual rate that would turn an initial value into a final value over a specified period.

What does CAGR not show?

CAGR smooths growth into one annualized rate and does not show year-to-year volatility or the path taken between the starting and ending values.

Where is CAGR useful?

It is commonly used to compare the annualized growth of investments, revenue, sales or other values across a multi-year period.

Formula or method

CAGR = (Ending value / Starting value)^(1/n) − 1.

Useful note

CAGR describes a smoothed annual growth rate; it does not show the ups and downs that occurred between the starting and ending dates.
