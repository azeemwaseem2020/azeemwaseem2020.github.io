Calculating triangle area

For a known base and perpendicular height, A = ½ × base × height.

Worked example

A triangle with base 8 m and height 5 m has area 20 m².

A practical Pakistan example

This can help estimate the area of a triangular plot or design element before accounting for real-world measurement irregularities.

Important assumptions

The height must be perpendicular to the selected base; a sloping side is not automatically the height.
