Savings Account Profit Tax Calculator Pakistan

Use this savings account profit tax calculator to model tax on an entered savings or deposit profit amount. The tax rate is editable because withholding and final tax treatment can depend on the account, taxpayer and current rules.

What to enter

Enter the profit amount relevant to the tax treatment you are checking, not the original deposit principal. Keep bank withholding records separate from the final annual tax position.

Important Pakistan tax note

Rates and treatment can change. Check your bank statement and current FBR guidance before using the result for filing or financial decisions.
