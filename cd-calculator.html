CD Calculator

Estimate a certificate of deposit maturity value using compound growth assumptions.

CD maturity formula

For a nominal annual rate compounded n times per year, A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt), where r is the annual rate as a decimal and t is years.

Worked example

For $10,000 at 5% compounded monthly for 2 years, the estimated maturity value is about $11,046.57 before taxes or penalties.

APY and nominal rate

APY incorporates compounding into an annualized yield, while a nominal annual rate needs the compounding frequency to calculate the effective growth. Use the institution's disclosure for the actual product terms.

Early withdrawal and taxes

CDs can have early-withdrawal penalties, and interest may be taxable depending on jurisdiction. This calculator does not model those unless explicitly included.

FAQ

Does this calculator use monthly compounding? It lets you specify the number of compounding periods per year.

Is the result guaranteed? No. It is an estimate based on the inputs and assumes the stated rate and compounding continue for the full term.

Calculation context

A certificate-of-deposit calculation connects principal, annual rate, compounding and term to estimated interest and maturity value. Actual bank products can use different rates, compounding rules or early-withdrawal terms.

Certificate of deposit growth

A CD calculation estimates how a fixed deposit grows over its term using the stated annual yield and compounding convention. If an institution quotes APY, use it as an effective annual yield rather than treating it as a simple nominal rate. For a nominal rate compounded periodically, the future-value formula must use the correct number of periods.

For Pakistan fixed deposits or similar savings products, verify the quoted rate, term, tax treatment, early-withdrawal rules and whether profit is paid monthly or at maturity. The calculator provides a mathematical estimate and not a bank quotation.
