How a certificate of deposit grows

A CD calculator estimates the maturity value of a deposit from the principal, annual rate, term and compounding assumptions. The actual product's disclosure controls when interest is credited and whether the quoted rate is nominal or effective.

Early withdrawal matters

Many CDs have an early-withdrawal penalty or restrictions. A maturity calculation assumes the deposit remains under the stated terms until maturity unless the calculator explicitly models a penalty.

CD calculator FAQ

Is APY the same as the nominal rate?

No. APY generally incorporates the effect of compounding, while a nominal annual rate may require a compounding frequency to determine the effective annual return.

Does the calculator include tax?

Only if a specific tax input is provided. Interest taxation depends on jurisdiction and personal circumstances.
