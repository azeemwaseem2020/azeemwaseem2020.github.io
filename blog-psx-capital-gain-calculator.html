PSX Capital Gain & CGT Calculator Guide — Profit, Tax & Break-Even

A PSX capital gain calculation should separate gross price gain, trading costs and any verified capital gains tax assumption. This guide explains the arithmetic without hard-coding a tax rate that may not apply to every investor or tax year.

What is a PSX capital gain?

For a simple completed share trade, gross capital gain is the increase between the purchase and sale values before transaction costs and tax. Gross capital gain = (sale price − purchase price) × shares. If 1,000 shares move from PKR 100 to PKR 120, the gross gain is PKR 20,000 before costs.

How to calculate PSX share profit

Start with the purchase value and sale value, then account for the actual costs shown on your broker records. A simple net trading result is gross gain minus entered buy-side costs, sell-side costs and any deliberately entered tax estimate.

PSX CGT: why the tax rate should not be guessed

Capital gains tax treatment can depend on the applicable rules, tax year, acquisition details, security and investor circumstances. For that reason, Calcora does not present an unverified universal CGT percentage as though it applies to every PSX transaction. Use the rate or tax result supported by the current applicable official rules or your qualified tax adviser.

PSX CGT calculation workflow

  1. Record the actual acquisition price, sale price and number of shares.
  2. Calculate the gross capital gain or loss.
  3. Enter applicable brokerage and other verified transaction costs.
  4. Determine the relevant CGT treatment from current official rules.
  5. Apply a verified tax assumption if you are using the calculator for an estimate.
  6. Reconcile the estimate with your broker statement and tax records.

Capital gain versus total investment return

Capital gain measures the change in the value of the shares sold. Dividends are a separate component of investment return. For a broader scenario, review price gain, dividends, transaction costs and taxes separately rather than treating one number as the complete return.

PSX break-even sale price

A simplified break-even calculation asks what sale price is needed to recover the invested amount plus known costs. The exact result depends on the charges and tax treatment relevant to the transaction, so actual broker records should be used whenever possible.

Common PSX CGT and profit-calculation mistakes

  • Treating gross price movement as final net profit.
  • Ignoring brokerage and other transaction charges.
  • Using a tax rate from an outdated tax year.
  • Assuming every security and investor has identical tax treatment.
  • Ignoring corporate actions that changed the share position.
  • Using a current market price as though a sale had already occurred.

FAQ

Is a PSX capital gain the same as dividend income?

No. Capital gain comes from a change in the value of a share position. Dividend income is a separate distribution and should be analysed separately.

Can I use Calcora for a tax filing?

No. Use current official tax rules, broker records and the required tax documentation. Seek qualified professional advice when necessary.

Does the calculator know the current PSX CGT rate?

No. The calculator uses an entered tax assumption so that users can apply a rate appropriate to their circumstances and the relevant rules instead of relying on a potentially outdated hard-coded value.
