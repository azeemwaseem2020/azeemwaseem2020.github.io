Car Loan Calculator — Payment, Interest & Total Cost

Estimate the financed amount, monthly payment, total interest and repayment for a fixed-rate car loan.

How the car loan estimate works

Subtract the down payment from the vehicle price to get the amount financed. The calculator then applies the standard fixed-rate monthly payment formula. Compare both the regular payment and total interest because a longer term can lower the monthly payment while increasing the total borrowing cost.

Costs to check separately

Taxes, registration, insurance, dealer charges, lender fees and optional products may not be included in the financed amount. Check the actual loan offer before relying on the estimate.

How the car loan estimate works

Enter the vehicle price, down payment, interest rate and loan term. The calculator estimates the financed amount, regular payment, total payments and total interest using the loan inputs you provide.

What to compare

A lower monthly payment can result from a longer term, but a longer term may increase the total interest paid. Compare both the monthly payment and the total repayment rather than looking at the instalment alone.

Costs not included automatically

Registration, insurance, taxes, dealer fees, maintenance and other ownership costs may not be part of the loan calculation unless you include them in the financed amount. Check the lender’s actual offer before making a purchase decision.
