Frequently asked questions

What does this calculator do?

A confidence interval gives a range around an estimate based on sample information and a selected confidence level.

Are the results exact?

The result is an estimate based on the values and assumptions entered. Check the formula and units shown on the page.

Can I use different inputs?

Yes. Enter your own values and recalculate to compare different scenarios.

Quick guide: how to use this calculator

Enter the sample size, sample mean and variability information requested, then select the confidence level.

Formula or method

For a mean with known standard error, an interval is estimate ± critical value × standard error; the exact formula depends on the selected method.

Useful note

A confidence interval describes uncertainty in an estimate; it does not mean there is a fixed probability that the true value is inside one already-computed interval.

Before you use the result

The result depends on the inputs, units and assumptions shown on this page. Check those details before comparing the result with another source or applying it to a real-world decision.
