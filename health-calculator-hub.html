How to use Calcora health calculators

  1. Choose the calculation that matches your question.
  2. Enter the units and measurements requested by the tool.
  3. Read the result together with the formula and assumptions.
  4. For medical, nutrition or treatment decisions, confirm the result with a qualified professional.

Why the tools are separated

BMI, BMR, TDEE, body-fat percentage and calorie estimates answer different questions. Keeping them as separate calculators makes each result easier to interpret and reduces the risk of treating one number as a complete health assessment.
