EPS formula

For this simplified calculator, EPS = net income ÷ shares. In formal financial reporting, basic EPS can also account for items such as preferred dividends and weighted-average shares, so a company’s reported EPS may differ from this quick estimate.

EPS example

If net income is $100,000 and the calculation uses 10,000 shares, EPS = 100,000 ÷ 10,000 = $10.00 per share.

What does EPS mean?

Earnings per share expresses a company’s profit attributable to common shareholders on a per-share basis. Investors often compare EPS across reporting periods or use it with other measures such as the price-to-earnings ratio. EPS alone does not describe a company’s complete financial position.

How to use this calculator

Enter the relevant net income and number of shares, then select Calculate. Use consistent units for both inputs and make sure the share figure represents the basis you intend to analyze.

FAQ

What does a higher EPS mean? A higher EPS indicates more earnings per share under the same calculation basis, but EPS should be interpreted alongside other financial information.

Is this the same as reported EPS? Not necessarily. Reported basic EPS can use weighted-average shares and adjustments required by the applicable accounting rules.
