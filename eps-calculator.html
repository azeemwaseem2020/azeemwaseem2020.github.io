EPS Calculator — Earnings Per Share

Estimate basic earnings per share from net income and shares using a simple, transparent calculation.

EPS formula

For this simplified calculator, EPS = Net income ÷ Shares. Formal basic EPS may use weighted-average shares and adjustments required by the applicable accounting framework.

Worked examples

If net income is $100,000 and 10,000 shares are used, EPS = $10.00 per share. If earnings are $2 million and 500,000 shares are used, EPS = $4.00 per share.

What does EPS mean?

Earnings per share expresses earnings on a per-share basis. It can be compared across periods and alongside measures such as P/E, but EPS alone does not describe a company's complete financial position.

Basic vs diluted EPS

This tool gives a simplified basic-EPS estimate. Reported basic EPS can use weighted-average common shares and preferred-dividend adjustments, while diluted EPS can include potentially dilutive securities.

EPS FAQ

What is EPS?

EPS means earnings per share and expresses earnings on a per-share basis.

Is higher EPS automatically better?

Not by itself. EPS should be considered with profitability, cash flow, share-count changes, valuation, and the reporting period.

Is this diluted EPS?

No. The calculator does not model potentially dilutive securities.
