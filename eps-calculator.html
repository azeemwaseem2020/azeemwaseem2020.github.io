EPS Calculator — Earnings Per Share

Estimate basic earnings per share from net income and shares using a simple, transparent calculation.

EPS formula

For this simplified calculator, EPS = Net income ÷ Shares. Formal basic EPS may use weighted-average shares and adjustments required by the applicable accounting framework.

Worked examples

If net income is $100,000 and 10,000 shares are used, EPS = $10.00 per share. If earnings are $2 million and 500,000 shares are used, EPS = $4.00 per share.

What does EPS mean?

Earnings per share expresses earnings on a per-share basis. It can be compared across periods and alongside measures such as P/E, but EPS alone does not describe a company's complete financial position.

Basic vs diluted EPS

This tool gives a simplified basic-EPS estimate. Reported basic EPS can use weighted-average common shares and preferred-dividend adjustments, while diluted EPS can include potentially dilutive securities.

EPS FAQ

What is EPS?

EPS means earnings per share and expresses earnings on a per-share basis.

Is higher EPS automatically better?

Not by itself. EPS should be considered with profitability, cash flow, share-count changes, valuation, and the reporting period.

Is this diluted EPS?

No. The calculator does not model potentially dilutive securities.

Earnings per share: formula and interpretation

Earnings per share (EPS) expresses a company's profit attributable to ordinary shareholders on a per-share basis. A simplified formula is EPS = net income available to ordinary shareholders ÷ weighted average ordinary shares outstanding. Using weighted average shares matters when the share count changes during the reporting period.

Worked example

If a company reports PKR 120 million of profit attributable to ordinary shareholders and has 30 million weighted average shares, EPS is PKR 4.00 per share. A higher EPS can result from higher profit, fewer shares, or both, so EPS should not be interpreted without checking the underlying numbers.

Pakistan investing example

When reviewing a PSX-listed company, compare EPS with the company's own previous periods and with other companies in the same industry. Do not compare raw EPS across businesses with very different share counts and capital structures. For valuation work, EPS can be combined with a market price to calculate a price-to-earnings ratio.

Important notes

Reported EPS may be basic or diluted. Stock splits and bonus issues can also affect share counts and historical comparability. This calculator is a mathematical aid; use the company's published financial statements for official reported EPS.

FAQs

What does EPS mean? Profit attributable to ordinary shareholders per weighted average share. Can EPS be negative? Yes, if attributable earnings are negative. Is EPS the same as dividend? No. EPS measures earnings; a dividend is the portion distributed to shareholders.
