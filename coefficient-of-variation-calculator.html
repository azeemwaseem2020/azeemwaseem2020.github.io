Quick guide: how to use this calculator

Enter the dataset or the mean and standard deviation requested by the calculator to express variation relative to the mean.

Formula or method

CV = standard deviation / mean × 100%.

Useful note

CV is most useful when the mean is meaningful and positive; results can become misleading when the mean is near zero.
