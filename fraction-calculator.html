Fraction Calculator Guide

Use this tool to add, subtract, multiply or divide two fractions and reduce the result to its simplest form.

How the operations work

For addition or subtraction, convert the fractions to a common denominator. Multiplication uses numerator × numerator and denominator × denominator. Division is multiplication by the reciprocal of the second fraction.

Worked example

For 1/2 + 1/3, a common denominator is 6: 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6.

Important check

A denominator cannot be zero, and division by a fraction with a zero numerator is undefined. Keep negative signs with the correct part of the fraction when checking the result.

Formula & assumptions

For a/b ± c/d use a common denominator. Multiplication is ac/bd and division is ad/bc. The result is reduced by the greatest common divisor.
