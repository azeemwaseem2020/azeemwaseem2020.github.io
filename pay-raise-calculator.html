Calculate a pay raise

A percentage pay raise is calculated from the original pay: raise amount = current pay × raise rate ÷ 100, and new pay = current pay + raise amount. To find the percentage increase from two salaries, divide the difference by the original salary.

Worked example

A monthly salary of PKR 80,000 with a 10% raise produces a PKR 8,000 increase and a new gross salary of PKR 88,000. This is gross pay; take-home pay may change differently after tax, provident-fund contributions or other deductions.

Pakistan workplace example

When comparing an annual increment, promotion and inflation adjustment, keep the base salary and effective date clear. A raise paid for only part of a year will not produce the same annual cash increase as a full-year change.

FAQs

Is a 10% raise always 10% more take-home pay? No. Deductions can change. Can I calculate the old salary? Yes, by dividing the new salary by 1 + the raise rate as a decimal. What if the raise is a fixed amount? Add that amount directly.

Pay Raise Calculation: Formula and Example

A percentage pay raise is calculated from the old salary using New Salary = Old Salary × (1 + Raise%/100). The increase itself is Old Salary × Raise%/100. For example, a Rs 60,000 monthly salary with a 10% raise increases by Rs 6,000, giving a new gross salary of Rs 66,000 per month.

For an annual salary, the same percentage applies to the annual figure. If annual gross pay is Rs 720,000 and the raise is 8%, the increase is Rs 57,600 and the new annual gross is Rs 777,600. The calculator should not be confused with take-home pay because tax, pension, provident-fund contributions and other deductions can change the amount received.

In Pakistan, salary discussions often quote monthly gross pay even when an employment contract is annual. When comparing an old and new offer, check whether the stated figure includes allowances, bonuses, fuel, medical benefits or other components. A 10% increase in basic salary is not necessarily a 10% increase in total compensation.

Comparing raises

If a salary receives multiple sequential raises, apply each percentage to the updated salary rather than simply adding the percentages. A 5% raise followed by a 5% raise produces a total increase of 10.25%, not exactly 10%, because the second raise applies to the already increased amount.

FAQs

Does the calculator show net salary? No. It calculates the gross pay change unless deductions are separately considered.

Can I calculate a negative raise? A negative percentage represents a pay reduction and can be calculated mathematically.

Is an annual raise the same as a bonus? No. A raise changes recurring pay; a bonus is usually a separate payment.
