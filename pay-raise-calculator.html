How to calculate a pay raise

A pay raise compares your old compensation with the proposed new compensation. The percentage increase is the difference between the new and old pay divided by the old pay, multiplied by 100.

Example

If monthly pay rises from PKR 60,000 to PKR 66,000, the increase is PKR 6,000 and the raise rate is 10%. The new amount is the figure to compare against future monthly or annual budgeting.

Annual impact

Convert both amounts to the same pay period before comparing them. A monthly raise can be multiplied by 12 for a simple annual gross comparison, while bonuses, allowances, tax and deductions may change the actual take-home difference.

Pay-raise FAQ

Is a 10% raise the same as 10% more take-home pay?

Not necessarily. Taxes, pension or other payroll deductions can mean the change in net pay differs from the gross raise percentage.
