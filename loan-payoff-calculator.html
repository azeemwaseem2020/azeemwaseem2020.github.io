Using the payoff estimate

Enter the balance that remains, the annual rate and the payment you can actually make each month. The result assumes the rate remains unchanged and payments continue on schedule. A lender's payoff quote may also include accrued interest, fees or an early-settlement charge.

Loan payoff formula

For a positive monthly rate, estimated payment periods are based on n = −ln(1 − Pr/M) ÷ ln(1+r), where P is balance, r is monthly interest rate and M is payment. The payment must exceed the monthly interest.

How payoff time is estimated

The estimate uses the remaining balance, periodic interest rate and regular payment. If the payment is too small to cover accruing interest, the balance may not decline as expected. Actual payoff quotes can include accrued interest or lender charges.

Results are estimates based on the inputs and standard formulas. Check applicable local rules, rates and terms for real-world decisions.

Frequently asked questions

What does a loan payoff calculator show?

It estimates how long a loan may take to repay and the interest paid based on the balance, rate and payment amount.

Can extra payments reduce payoff time?

Yes. Paying more toward principal can reduce the outstanding balance faster and may reduce total interest, subject to the lender's terms.

Are lender fees included?

Not unless they are included in the amount you enter. Actual payoff figures should be confirmed with your lender.
