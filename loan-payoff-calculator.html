Frequently asked questions

What does a loan payoff calculator show?

It estimates how long a loan may take to repay and the interest paid based on the balance, rate and payment amount.

Can extra payments reduce payoff time?

Yes. Paying more toward principal can reduce the outstanding balance faster and may reduce total interest, subject to the lender's terms.

Are lender fees included?

Not unless they are included in the amount you enter. Actual payoff figures should be confirmed with your lender.
