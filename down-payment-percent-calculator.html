Down Payment Percentage Calculator: How It Works

A down payment percentage tells you how much of a purchase price you pay upfront. The remaining amount is the financed balance. This calculator is useful for comparing home, auto, and other financed purchases before you choose a loan amount.

Formula

Down payment = Purchase price × Down payment percentage ÷ 100
Financed amount = Purchase price − Down payment.

Worked example

For a $300,000 purchase with a 20% down payment, the upfront amount is $60,000 and the estimated financed amount is $240,000. Actual lender requirements can differ.

What to check before borrowing

A larger down payment generally reduces the amount financed, but it also uses more cash upfront. Compare the loan term, interest rate, taxes, insurance, fees, and any mortgage or financing requirements rather than looking only at the percentage.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a 10% down payment?

Multiply the purchase price by 0.10, or multiply by 10 and divide by 100.

Does the down payment percentage change the loan amount?

Yes. A higher percentage produces a lower financed amount for the same purchase price.
