Roots and square roots

A root reverses an exponent. The square root of x is the number that, when multiplied by itself, gives x. More generally, the nth root satisfies yn = x. For real square roots, the input must be nonnegative.

Worked example

The square root of 144 is 12 because 12² = 144. The cube root of 125 is 5 because 5³ = 125. When a root is not an integer, the calculator can provide a decimal approximation.

Practical use

Roots appear in geometry, physics, statistics and engineering. For example, the distance formula and standard-deviation calculations often contain square roots.

Accuracy notes

Do not round intermediate values unnecessarily. For even roots, negative real inputs do not have ordinary real-number results.

Roots: Formula, Examples and Interpretation

A root reverses an exponent. The square root of a number x is the value y for which y² = x. More generally, the nth root is written as ⁿ√x and satisfies yⁿ = x. The principal square root is non-negative, so √25 = 5 even though both 5 and -5 square to 25 in an equation.

For example, √144 = 12 because 12 × 12 = 144. For a non-perfect square such as √50, the calculator returns an approximation of about 7.071. The exact expression can also be simplified as 5√2 because 50 = 25 × 2. Exact forms are useful in algebra because they preserve precision instead of rounding early.

For a practical engineering example, if a square area is 64 m² and you need the side length, take √64 = 8 m. This inverse relationship is useful when converting an area into a square dimension. In Pakistan property or construction work, confirm whether the given area is in square feet, square metres or another unit before interpreting the result.

Domain and common mistakes

Real-valued even roots require a non-negative radicand. Odd roots can accept negative inputs, such as ∛(-8) = -2. Avoid confusing a root with division by the root index; √25 is not 25/2. When using approximate roots in later calculations, keep sufficient decimal precision until the final step.

FAQs

What is a square root? It is a number that produces the original value when squared.

Can square roots be negative? The principal square root is non-negative; equations can have positive and negative solutions.

Why is √2 irrational? It cannot be represented exactly as a ratio of two integers, so decimal values are approximations.
