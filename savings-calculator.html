Savings Calculator

Estimate future savings from an initial balance, regular monthly deposits and an annual interest rate.

Future savings

Formula and assumptions

The calculator uses a monthly rate calculated as the entered annual percentage divided by 12, then compounds over the nearest whole number of months. Monthly deposits are assumed to arrive at the end of each month. Taxes, fees, inflation and changing account rates are not included.

Frequently asked questions

What does the savings calculator estimate?

It estimates future savings from an initial balance, regular contributions, an assumed interest rate and a selected period.

How often is interest compounded?

The calculation uses monthly compounding for the displayed estimate unless the page states a different assumption.

Are the results guaranteed?

No. Actual savings growth depends on the account rate, compounding rules, taxes, fees and contribution timing.

Savings Calculator: Starting Balance, Deposits and Compound Growth

This savings calculator estimates how an initial balance and regular monthly deposits may grow under an assumed annual interest rate. It can help compare savings goals, but it is not a bank quote. Actual account returns may use a different compounding schedule and may be reduced by fees, taxes or changing rates.

Formula and timing

For monthly rate i and n months, the initial balance grows to P × (1 + i)n. If a fixed deposit C is made at the end of every month, its future value is C × (((1 + i)n − 1) ÷ i). When i is zero, total savings are simply P + C × n. This page uses a monthly-rate approximation based on the annual percentage entered.

Worked example

Suppose you start with PKR 50,000, add PKR 5,000 at the end of each month and assume a 6% annual rate for five years. Your personal contributions would be PKR 50,000 + (PKR 5,000 × 60) = PKR 350,000. The projected balance is higher in the positive-return scenario because interest compounds over time. Treat the difference as an estimate, not a promised profit.

How to use the calculator

  1. Enter the current savings balance and monthly deposit you realistically expect to maintain.
  2. Enter the annual rate offered by the account or a scenario rate for comparison.
  3. Set the number of years and calculate.
  4. Compare total deposits with the projected balance, then repeat using lower rates.

Choose a useful savings target

Break a large goal into an amount and date, then check whether the required monthly deposit fits your budget. Keep emergency savings accessible and separate from money needed for near-term expenses. Inflation affects purchasing power, so a nominal balance does not show what goods and services it will buy in the future. For Pakistan-based accounts, review the bank's product terms, profit calculation method, tax treatment and any balance requirements rather than assuming all savings products work alike.

Frequently asked questions

Is the rate guaranteed for the full term?

Not unless the account terms explicitly guarantee it; many rates can change.

Does the result include tax or fees?

No. Subtract applicable charges and consider the relevant tax rules separately.

Are deposits assumed at the start of each month?

This formula assumes end-of-month deposits, so earlier deposits may produce a different outcome.
