How to use the Calories Burned Calculator

  1. Enter body weight in kilograms.
  2. Enter the activity's MET value.
  3. Enter exercise duration in minutes.
  4. Press Calculate and treat the result as an estimate.

MET calorie formula

Calories ≈ MET × 3.5 × body weight (kg) ÷ 200 × duration (minutes).

Worked example

At 5 METs, 70 kg and 30 minutes: 5 × 3.5 × 70 ÷ 200 × 30 ≈ 184 kcal.

About the estimate

Calories burned are estimated from body weight, activity intensity (MET) and duration. MET values are population-level approximations, so the result should be treated as an estimate rather than an exact measurement.

Quick guide: how to use this calculator

Formula or method

Useful note

Actual energy expenditure varies between people and with exercise intensity, terrain and efficiency.

Before you use the result

Estimating calories burned during exercise

Exercise calorie estimates commonly depend on body weight, activity type and duration. They are useful for comparing sessions but should not be treated as precise measurements of energy expenditure.

Why the estimate can differ from a tracker

Wearables and calculators use different models, sensors and assumptions. Heart rate, fitness level, movement efficiency and exercise intensity can all affect the actual energy cost.

Calories burned FAQ

Does heavier body weight always mean more calories burned?

For the same activity and intensity, many estimation equations predict higher energy expenditure at higher body weight, but the relationship is not exact for every person.

Can I use exercise calories to plan food intake?

Use them as an estimate rather than assuming every displayed calorie has been precisely measured.
