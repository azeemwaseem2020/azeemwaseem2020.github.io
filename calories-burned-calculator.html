How to use the Calories Burned Calculator

  1. Enter body weight in kilograms.
  2. Enter the activity's MET value.
  3. Enter exercise duration in minutes.
  4. Press Calculate and treat the result as an estimate.

MET calorie formula

Calories ≈ MET × 3.5 × body weight (kg) ÷ 200 × duration (minutes).

Worked example

At 5 METs, 70 kg and 30 minutes: 5 × 3.5 × 70 ÷ 200 × 30 ≈ 184 kcal.

About the estimate

Calories burned are estimated from body weight, activity intensity (MET) and duration. MET values are population-level approximations, so the result should be treated as an estimate rather than an exact measurement.

Understand the MET estimate

The common MET method estimates energy expenditure from activity intensity, body weight and duration. The calculation shown by this tool is a population-level approximation; the chosen MET value may not match your actual pace, terrain, technique or effort. Two people completing the same named activity can therefore receive different estimates or expend different amounts of energy.

Use exercise estimates cautiously

Wearables and calculators may use different formulas, sensors and definitions, including gross versus active calories. Do not assume their results should match exactly, and avoid automatically eating back every displayed calorie as though it were a precise measurement. Consider trends and your broader health context rather than treating a single session estimate as exact.

This tool does not diagnose fitness, prescribe an exercise programme or replace clinical advice where health conditions affect activity.

Calculation context

Calories-burned estimates connect an activity, duration and body-weight assumption with an estimated energy expenditure. Results are model-based and can vary with intensity, individual physiology and the equation used.
