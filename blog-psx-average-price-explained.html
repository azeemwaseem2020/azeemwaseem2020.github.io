PSX Average Price After Buying More Shares

When you buy the same stock at different prices, the correct average cost is weighted by the number of shares in each purchase. A simple average of the two prices is usually wrong.

The weighted-average formula

Average price = total purchase value ÷ total shares. If you own 1,000 shares at Rs. 100 and buy 500 more at Rs. 120, total cost is Rs. 160,000 and total shares are 1,500. The weighted average is Rs. 106.67.

Why averaging the prices fails

(100 + 120) ÷ 2 = Rs. 110 assumes both purchases have the same number of shares. They do not. The Rs. 100 purchase is twice as large, so it must have twice the influence on the result.

What about brokerage and taxes?

For a basic educational calculation, many examples use trade value only. A portfolio system intended to track actual cost should define which transaction costs are capitalized into cost and apply the same rule consistently.

Adding a third purchase

Do not average the old average with the new price. Instead, carry forward total shares and total cost, add the new transaction, then divide the new total cost by the new total shares. This avoids rounding errors.

When average price is useful

The figure is useful for tracking a position's arithmetic cost basis and for understanding break-even calculations. It does not predict the future market price and should not be treated as a forecast.

Include transaction costs when tracking a PSX position

The basic weighted average is total purchase value divided by total shares. If you want an estimate of the position's cost including transaction charges, add eligible purchase-side brokerage and fees to the relevant purchase values before dividing by the share count. Keep the gross trade price, transaction costs and any tax treatment visible as separate line items so the calculation can be reconciled with the broker's contract note.

Worked example

Buying 100 shares at PKR 50 costs PKR 5,000 before charges. Buying another 50 at PKR 40 costs PKR 2,000. The weighted average trade price is PKR 7,000 ÷ 150 = PKR 46.67 per share before charges. If total eligible purchase-side charges were PKR 150, the illustrative cost basis including those charges would be PKR 7,150 ÷ 150 = PKR 47.67 per share. Actual contract-note treatment and applicable taxes or fees should be verified from current official and broker documents.

Keep records consistent

  • Use the same share quantity and trade values as the contract notes.
  • Do not treat a sell transaction as another purchase when calculating the remaining holding's average cost.
  • Track dividends separately from purchase cost unless you are intentionally modelling reinvestment.
  • This calculation does not predict future share prices or recommend buying or selling a security.
