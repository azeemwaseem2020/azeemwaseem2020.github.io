How to verify a prime result

A prime number greater than 1 has exactly two positive divisors: 1 and itself. A composite number has at least one additional positive divisor.

Practical check

To test whether an integer n is prime, potential divisors only need to be checked through the square root of n. If no divisor in that range divides evenly, n is prime.

Important boundary cases

Zero and one are not prime numbers. Negative integers are not prime under the usual elementary definition.
