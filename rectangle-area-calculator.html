Calculating rectangle area

Rectangle area is A = length × width.

Worked example

A room 5 m long and 4 m wide has an area of 20 m².

A practical Pakistan example

For flooring or room planning in Pakistan, keeping both dimensions in metres produces a result directly in square metres.

Important assumptions

Use the same units for both dimensions and distinguish floor area from perimeter.

Area and practical measurement

Rectangle area is length × width. A 12 m by 8 m room has 96 m² of floor area. If measurements are in feet, the result is square feet. Convert units before multiplying rather than mixing metres and feet. For flooring, painting or property estimates, subtract major openings when appropriate and keep waste allowances separate from the base area.

Rectangle area and perimeter: when to use each

Area measures the two-dimensional surface inside a rectangle and is length × width. Perimeter measures the distance around its boundary and is 2 × (length + width). Area is expressed in square units, such as m² or ft²; perimeter is expressed in linear units, such as m or ft.

Worked example

A room measuring 5 m by 4 m has an area of 20 m² and a perimeter of 18 m. If you are estimating floor covering, area is the starting measure. If you are estimating skirting around the room, perimeter is more relevant, though door openings and waste must be considered separately.

Measurement tips

  • Measure length and width in the same unit before calculating.
  • Convert units before multiplying; multiplying metres by centimetres produces a mixed unit, not m².
  • For a real flooring or paint project, account for cut waste, openings, irregular walls and the product's coverage instructions.
  • Use the actual inside dimensions for a floor plan, not an outside dimension that includes wall thickness, unless that is what you need.

Frequently asked questions

Can a rectangle have the same area but a different perimeter? Yes. Different length-width pairs can have equal products but different sums.

Is perimeter measured in square units? No. Perimeter is a length, while area uses square units.

Can this calculator measure an irregular room? Not directly. Divide the shape into simpler rectangles, calculate each area, and handle overlaps or cut-outs carefully.
