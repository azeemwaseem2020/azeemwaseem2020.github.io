Calculating rectangle area

Rectangle area is A = length × width.

Worked example

A room 5 m long and 4 m wide has an area of 20 m².

A practical Pakistan example

For flooring or room planning in Pakistan, keeping both dimensions in metres produces a result directly in square metres.

Important assumptions

Use the same units for both dimensions and distinguish floor area from perimeter.

FAQ

What formula should I use? Use the formula stated for the selected shape or electrical model.

Do units matter? Yes. Convert inputs to compatible units before calculating.

Is the result exact? It is only as reliable as the inputs, assumptions and measurement precision.

Area and practical measurement

Rectangle area is length × width. A 12 m by 8 m room has 96 m² of floor area. If measurements are in feet, the result is square feet. Convert units before multiplying rather than mixing metres and feet. For flooring, painting or property estimates, subtract major openings when appropriate and keep waste allowances separate from the base area.
