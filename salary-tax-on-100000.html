Salary Tax on PKR 100,000 in Pakistan (2026–27)

For a steady monthly taxable salary of PKR 100,000, annual taxable salary is PKR 1,200,000. Under the Tax Year 2027 salaried slabs, the estimated annual tax is PKR 6,000, or an average PKR 500 per month.

How the PKR 6,000 tax is calculated

Annual taxable salary = PKR 100,000 × 12 = PKR 1,200,000. The first PKR 600,000 is taxed at 0%. The remaining PKR 600,000 is taxed at 1%: PKR 600,000 × 0.01 = PKR 6,000. Dividing the annual tax by 12 gives an average of PKR 500 per month.

This monthly figure is an annual average, not a guarantee that an employer's actual monthly withholding will always be identical. Payroll timing, bonuses, changing salary and employer adjustments can affect deductions.

Taxable salary, allowances and bonuses

Use annual taxable salary for the slab calculation. Depending on the applicable rules, salary-related items may include bonuses, commissions, perquisites and allowances. Do not automatically treat every allowance as exempt or every gross-pay item as taxable; verify its legal treatment and include relevant taxable amounts in your estimate.

Does filer status affect salary tax?

This estimate concerns salary income tax under the salaried-person slab structure. Filer status is a separate issue from the slab arithmetic and may affect some transaction-based withholding taxes. This page does not calculate those transaction taxes.

Frequently asked questions

How much tax is deducted from a PKR 100,000 salary?

Under the stated assumptions, the estimate is PKR 6,000 annually, averaging PKR 500 monthly.

Is the entire salary taxed at 1%?

No. The first PKR 600,000 of annual taxable salary is in the 0% band; the 1% rate applies only to the portion above that threshold within the relevant slab.

Is PKR 99,500 the guaranteed take-home salary?

No. It is the monthly salary after subtracting only the estimated average income tax. Other payroll deductions are not included.

Official reference and limitations

These estimates use the salaried-individual slab structure published for Tax Year 2027. The relevant eligibility rule, taxable benefits, exemptions and personal circumstances can affect the final liability.
