Pension and Gratuity Calculator Pakistan: Formula and Scheme Checks

Pension and gratuity are employee benefits, but the calculation rules can differ between federal or provincial government service, autonomous bodies, private employers and individual employment contracts. This page offers two transparent illustrations: gratuity based on eligible salary, service and an entered number of days per service year, and a monthly pension estimate based on final pensionable salary and an entered percentage. These inputs are assumptions, not a claim that one formula applies to every employee.

Gratuity formula used here

Illustrative gratuity = eligible monthly salary ÷ 30 × gratuity days per service year × eligible service years. For example, salary of PKR 100,000, 30 days per year and 10 eligible years gives 100,000 ÷ 30 × 30 × 10 = PKR 1,000,000. Actual schemes may define eligible pay differently, apply ceilings or exclusions, or use a different divisor and service rule.

Pension estimate formula

Illustrative monthly pension = final pensionable salary × entered pension percentage ÷ 100. If pensionable salary is PKR 100,000 and the selected percentage is 50%, the arithmetic result is PKR 50,000 per month. This does not calculate commutation, survivor benefits, medical allowances, tax, annual increases or scheme-specific eligibility.

How to use the calculator

  1. Identify the exact pension or gratuity scheme and the definition of eligible salary.
  2. Enter completed eligible service years and the formula factor stated in your rules or HR documents.
  3. For pension, enter the applicable percentage rather than assuming a universal rate.
  4. Compare the estimate with the official pension statement, service book or employer calculation.

Documents to check

Keep appointment and retirement dates, service history, last pay or pensionable-pay statement, leave or service adjustments, scheme rules and any official commutation illustration. A difference between this estimate and an official amount may reflect eligibility rules rather than an arithmetic error.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official government pension calculator?

No. It is an illustrative calculator and does not determine official entitlement.

Does every employee get 30 days per year?

No. The days factor and formula must come from the applicable scheme or employment terms.

Does the pension estimate include commutation?

No. It estimates the monthly amount from the entered salary and percentage only.
