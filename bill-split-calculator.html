Bill Split Calculator — Split a Bill & Tip Per Person

Calculate the tip, total bill, and equal share for a group. Useful for restaurant bills, shared expenses, dinners, trips, and group payments.

Bill split formula

Tip = Bill × (Tip % ÷ 100)

Total = Bill + Tip

Amount per person = Total ÷ Number of people

This assumes an equal split. For itemized orders, calculate each person's items and applicable tax or tip separately.

Worked example

A $100 bill with a 15% tip produces $15 tip and a $115 total. Split between 2 people, each person pays $57.50.

Tax, tip and shared expenses

Check whether tax, service charges or other fees are already included before adding a tip. If a restaurant receipt lists tax separately, decide whether the tip should be calculated on the food subtotal or the amount shown on the receipt. Local customs and venue policies vary, so the calculator does not assume a particular tax or tipping rule.

How to split a bill fairly

An equal split works well when everyone has similar orders or agrees to share the cost evenly. If one person paid for an extra item, entered a discount, or used a different service, an equal division may not reflect what each person actually owes. In that situation, calculate the individual items first and then add any agreed shared charges.

For example, a PKR 6,000 restaurant bill with a 10% tip has a PKR 600 tip and a PKR 6,600 total. For four people sharing equally, the cost is PKR 1,650 per person. The currency symbol is not built into the calculation, so you can use the same method for PKR, USD, GBP or another currency.

Rounding and small differences

Currency is normally settled to two decimal places, but dividing a total among several people can create a rounding difference of a few cents or paisa. If the exact total cannot be divided evenly, one practical approach is to assign the remaining smallest unit to one person so the collected amounts add back to the full bill.

Bill Split FAQ

Does everyone pay the same amount?

Yes, this calculator divides the final total equally.

Can I include a tip?

Yes. Enter the tip percentage before splitting the total.

What if people ordered different items?

An equal split may not be appropriate. Use an itemized split when individual orders differ significantly.

Before you split the bill

Confirm the bill amount, number of people and tip percentage before calculating. A tip of 15 means 15%, not 0.15%. The calculator treats zero tip as valid and requires at least one person. It is intended for equal-share calculations and does not determine tax rates, service charges or individual item responsibilities.

Quick check

After calculating, multiply the displayed amount per person by the number of people. Because the result is rounded to two decimal places, the multiplied amount can differ slightly from the exact total when the division does not end evenly.
