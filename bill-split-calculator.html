Bill Split Calculator — Split a Bill & Tip Per Person

Calculate the tip, total bill, and equal share for a group. Useful for restaurant bills, shared expenses, dinners, trips, and group payments.

Bill split formula

Tip = Bill × (Tip % ÷ 100)

Total = Bill + Tip

Amount per person = Total ÷ Number of people

This assumes an equal split. For itemized orders, calculate each person's items and applicable tax or tip separately.

Worked example

A $100 bill with a 15% tip produces $15 tip and a $115 total. Split between 2 people, each person pays $57.50.

Tax, tip and shared expenses

Check whether tax and service charges are already included before adding another percentage. The same method can be used for shared travel, groceries, events, or household expenses.

Bill Split FAQ

Does everyone pay the same amount?

Yes, this calculator divides the final total equally.

Can I include a tip?

Yes. Enter the tip percentage before splitting the total.

What if people ordered different items?

An equal split may not be appropriate. Use an itemized split when individual orders differ significantly.

Calculation context

Bill splitting connects the total bill, tip or service amount and number of people to an estimated per-person share. Equal splitting is distinct from weighted or item-by-item expense sharing.
