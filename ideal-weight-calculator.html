Ideal Weight Calculator

Estimate ideal body weight using several established height-based formulas. Compare results rather than treating one estimate as a medical target.

How to use the ideal weight calculator

  1. Select sex as required by the chosen reference formula.
  2. Enter height in centimetres.
  3. Calculate the Devine reference estimate.
  4. Compare the estimate with other health measures rather than treating it as a target.

Worked example

For a 175 cm adult, the Devine formula gives approximately 70.5 kg for males and 66.0 kg for females.

Method

This tool uses the Devine formula as a reference estimate. Different formulas can produce different results, and body composition is not captured by height alone.

Why formula results differ

Reference-weight formulas use height and, in some cases, sex-specific constants. They do not directly measure body fat, muscle mass, bone structure or individual health needs.

How to interpret the estimate

Treat the result as a mathematical reference rather than a personal medical target. For health decisions, consider the method’s limitations and obtain individualized guidance when needed.

How the Devine formula works

The Devine reference formula uses height in inches and the number of inches above 5 feet. The commonly used equations are 50 kg + 2.3 kg for each inch above 5 feet for males, and 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg for each inch above 5 feet for females. This page uses height to estimate a reference value; it does not measure body composition or determine an individual's healthy weight.

Why other calculators may differ

Several published reference formulas use different constants and can return different estimates for the same height. Results can also be interpreted differently for children, teenagers, pregnancy, older adults and people with unusual body composition. This calculator is intended as a general adult reference and should not be treated as a diagnosis or treatment target.

Consider context, not one number

Height-based ideal-weight formulas do not account for muscle mass, frame size, body-fat distribution, medical history or personal goals. BMI, waist measurements, clinical assessment and other health indicators answer different questions and also have limitations. If weight change is connected to a medical condition or nutrition concern, discuss appropriate targets with a qualified health professional.

Ideal-weight FAQ

Is the estimate a recommended goal?

No. It is a formula-derived reference value, not an individualized recommendation.

Why does the sex selection affect the result?

The Devine equations use different constants for males and females. The selection reflects the historical formula definition, not a complete assessment of an individual's physiology.
