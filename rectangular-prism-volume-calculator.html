Rectangular prism volume

The volume of a rectangular prism is length × width × height. All three dimensions must use compatible units. The result is expressed in cubic units, such as cm³, m³ or ft³.

Worked example

A storage box 2 m long, 1.5 m wide and 1 m high has volume 2 × 1.5 × 1 = 3 m³. If you need litres, remember that 1 m³ equals 1,000 litres.

Practical example

This formula can estimate room volume, tanks, boxes, concrete forms and storage capacity. For real-world containers, usable capacity may be smaller than geometric volume because of walls, fittings or required free space.

FAQs

Can dimensions be in feet? Yes, if all are in feet. Why is the answer cubic? Three lengths are multiplied. Can this estimate liquid capacity? Yes, after accounting for usable internal dimensions.
