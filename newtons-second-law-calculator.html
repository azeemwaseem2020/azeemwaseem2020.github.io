Newton's second law and net force

Newton's second law connects net force, mass and acceleration. The word net is important: when several forces act on an object, their vector sum determines the acceleration.

Worked example

A 20 kg object with a net force of 60 N has an acceleration of 3 m/s². If opposing forces are present, they must be included before calculating the net force.

Free-body thinking

For more realistic problems, identify all relevant forces first, choose consistent directions and then sum their components.

Newton's-law FAQ

Can a moving object have zero net force?

Yes. With zero net force, acceleration is zero, so an object can continue moving at constant velocity in an ideal inertial frame.
