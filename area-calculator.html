Area formulas

Rectangle: A = length × width.

Circle: A = πr².

Triangle: A = ½ × base × height.

Worked examples

An 8 m × 5 m rectangle has area 40 m². A circle with radius 3 m has area 28.27 m². A triangle with base 10 cm and height 6 cm has area 30 cm².

Area units

Area uses squared units such as cm², m², ft², or yd². Length conversion factors must be squared for area.

Choosing measurements

For a rectangle use length and width. For a circle use radius; if only diameter is known, radius = diameter ÷ 2. For a triangle use the perpendicular height to the chosen base.

How to calculate area

Area measures the amount of two-dimensional surface inside a boundary. The formula depends on the shape: a rectangle uses length × width, a triangle uses ½ × base × height, and a circle uses πr².

Worked examples

A rectangle measuring 8 m by 5 m has an area of 40 m². A circle with radius 3 m has an area of 9π m², approximately 28.27 m².

Area units

Area is expressed in squared units such as cm², m², ft² or acres. When converting a length unit, remember that the conversion factor is squared for area.

Area calculator FAQ

Why can't I convert m² like metres?

Because area has two dimensions. A linear conversion factor must be applied twice.

Can this be used for irregular land?

Break the shape into measurable sections or use an appropriate surveying method; a simple geometric formula may not represent an irregular boundary accurately.
