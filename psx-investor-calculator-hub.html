Find the right PSX calculation

For a completed or planned trade, use the PSX Profit Calculator. When adding shares to an existing position, use the Average Price Calculator. For several holdings, use the Portfolio Calculator. Dividend and position-size tools are useful for their respective scenario calculations.

How to use the PSX calculator toolset

  1. Choose the calculator that matches the exact task: trade, portfolio, average cost, position size or dividend reinvestment.
  2. Enter prices and share quantities from your own broker records rather than invented or stale market data.
  3. Include actual transaction costs wherever the calculator supports them.
  4. Keep dividends, bonus shares and rights issues conceptually separate from ordinary price profit.
  5. Use the portfolio calculator for multiple holdings and the average-price calculator for additional purchases.
  6. Verify tax, brokerage, settlement and corporate-action treatment against current official information and your broker statement.

Core PSX calculations explained

Gross P/L is sale value minus purchase value. Net P/L adjusts that result for entered costs and tax assumptions. Weighted average price is total purchase cost divided by total shares. Dividend income is dividend per share multiplied by shares held. Dividend yield is dividend per share divided by the reference share price, expressed as a percentage.

Why these pages are separated

PSX searches represent different jobs. A trader checking one transaction needs trade profit and break-even; an investor reviewing several holdings needs portfolio allocation and return; an investor adding to a position needs weighted average cost; and a planning exercise may need position size or dividend reinvestment. Dedicated pages make each answer more direct and reduce duplicated search content.

Using authoritative PSX information

PSX investor guidance explains that taxes and commissions affect investment costs and returns, while brokerage commissions can vary by firm. PSX also describes dividend yield as dividend cash value relative to share price and highlights measures such as EPS and P/E for investor research. Use the official PSX resources and your broker records when a calculation depends on current market or transaction information.

Which PSX calculation do you need?

Different PSX questions call for different numbers. Use the profit calculator for a trade result, average price when you add to an existing holding, dividend tools for income scenarios, and position sizing when you are working out how much capital to put at risk.

A practical order: calculate profit or loss → review average cost → estimate dividends or reinvestment → plan position size and risk → review the overall portfolio.

PSX calculator coverage

Calcora separates PSX calculations by task: trade profit and return, portfolio value and allocation, weighted average cost, position sizing, dividend income and reinvestment, and capital-gain tax assumptions. This structure helps visitors reach the calculation that matches their question instead of combining unrelated assumptions into one result.

All calculators work from figures entered by the visitor. They do not provide live PSX prices or personalized investment recommendations. Verify broker charges, corporate actions, dividend announcements and applicable tax rules before relying on an estimate.
