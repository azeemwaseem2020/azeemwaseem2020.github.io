Core PSX calculations explained

Gross P/L is sale value minus purchase value. Net P/L adjusts that result for entered costs and tax assumptions. Weighted average price is total purchase cost divided by total shares. Dividend income is dividend per share multiplied by shares held. Dividend yield is dividend per share divided by the reference share price, expressed as a percentage.

Why these pages are separated

PSX searches represent different jobs. A trader checking one transaction needs trade profit and break-even; an investor reviewing several holdings needs portfolio allocation and return; an investor adding to a position needs weighted average cost; and a planning exercise may need position size or dividend reinvestment. Dedicated pages make each answer more direct and reduce duplicated search content.

Using authoritative PSX information

PSX investor guidance explains that taxes and commissions affect investment costs and returns, while brokerage commissions can vary by firm. PSX also describes dividend yield as dividend cash value relative to share price and highlights measures such as EPS and P/E for investor research. Use the official PSX resources and your broker records when a calculation depends on current market or transaction information.
