How the advanced TMR check works

Each ingredient contributes dry matter and nutrients according to its DM weight. The calculator multiplies each ingredient's dry-matter contribution by its entered nutrient percentage, adds the contributions, and divides by total batch dry matter to produce a weighted nutrient percentage.

Weighted nutrient % DM = total nutrient kg ÷ total DM kg × 100. This makes the calculation transparent and keeps nutrient values on the same dry-matter basis.

Actual dry matter intake

When you enter feed delivered, refusals, animal count and TMR dry matter, the calculator estimates actual DMI per animal per day as consumed as-fed feed × TMR DM fraction ÷ animals. Penn State Extension uses the same basic approach for monitoring actual DMI.

Why dry matter matters

Dairy TMRs are commonly formulated on a dry-matter basis while feeds are loaded on an as-fed basis. Changes in moisture can therefore change the physical amount that must be loaded. University of Minnesota Extension recommends monitoring dry matter in ensiled forages and wet feeds because moisture changes can affect the delivered ration.

Use feed analysis, not guessed numbers

The nutrient fields are intentionally user supplied. Forage and feed composition changes with harvest, storage, moisture and other conditions. A professional check should use current laboratory or reliable feed-analysis results when available.

Forage and concentrate share

The calculator reports the as-fed and DM share of ingredients you classify as forage or concentrate. This is a descriptive check only. It does not decide what forage/concentrate ratio a particular cow, buffalo or production group should receive.

Target comparisons

Target fields are optional and user supplied. This prevents the calculator from pretending that one universal CP, NDF, calcium or phosphorus target applies to every animal, breed and production stage. If you have a documented specification, enter its applicable minimum and maximum and review the result with the person responsible for the ration.

FAQ

Does this calculator prescribe a ration?

No. It checks arithmetic and weighted nutrient values from data you provide. It does not create a universal ration recommendation.

Which nutrient values should I enter?

Use current feed-analysis values on a dry-matter basis where applicable. Laboratory or reliable feed-analysis data are preferred over guessed values.

Can I compare my TMR with a target?

Yes. Enter your own target minimum and maximum for a nutrient. The tool compares the calculated weighted value with those user-supplied targets.
