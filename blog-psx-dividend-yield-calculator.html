PSX Dividend Calculator Guide — DPS, Yield & Dividend Income

A useful PSX dividend calculation starts with dividend per share, the number of shares and a clearly defined reference price. This guide separates dividend income, dividend yield and yield on cost.

How to calculate PSX dividend income

Gross dividend income = dividend per share × number of shares. If you own 2,000 shares and the declared dividend is PKR 8 per share, gross dividend income is PKR 16,000.

PSX dividend yield formula

Dividend yield = annual dividend per share ÷ reference share price × 100. A PKR 10 annual dividend on a PKR 200 reference price gives a 5% yield. Always state whether the reference price is a current market price, purchase price or another valuation basis.

Yield on cost for PSX shares

Yield on cost compares annual dividend per share with your own average purchase cost. It can help with personal portfolio review, but it is not the same as the yield available to a new buyer at the current market price.

Annual dividend versus a single PSX payout

A single interim or final dividend is not automatically an annual dividend. For meaningful comparisons, use comparable periods and check the company's official dividend announcement and relevant dates.

PSX dividend tax and net income

Net dividend can be estimated as gross dividend minus the withholding amount you enter. Do not assume a universal tax rate: applicable tax treatment can change and may depend on investor circumstances. Use current applicable rules or verified broker records when estimating the cash amount received.

PSX dividend calculator workflow

  1. Verify the declared dividend per share.
  2. Confirm whether it is interim, final or another distribution and identify the relevant period.
  3. Enter the actual number of shares held.
  4. Choose and label the reference price used for yield.
  5. Calculate gross dividend income and dividend yield.
  6. Apply only a verified tax assumption if estimating net cash received.

Why a high dividend yield can be misleading

  • A falling share price can make a historical yield appear high.
  • A one-off distribution can distort an annual comparison.
  • Future dividends are not guaranteed.
  • Corporate actions can change the share count and cost basis.
  • Taxes and other deductions can reduce the amount actually received.

FAQ

Is PSX dividend yield the same as dividend income?

No. Income is the cash amount associated with your shares; yield is a percentage relating dividend per share to a stated reference price.

Can I calculate dividend yield for a PSX portfolio?

Yes. Add the estimated dividend income of each holding and compare it with the chosen portfolio cost or value basis. Keep the period and assumptions consistent.

Are future PSX dividends guaranteed?

No. A past or announced dividend does not guarantee future distributions. Check current company announcements and applicable dates.
