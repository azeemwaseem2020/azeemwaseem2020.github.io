Professional TMR workflow

  1. Identify the animal group and the documented feeding target.
  2. Use current feed analysis where available and keep as-fed and dry-matter bases separate.
  3. Scale the known ration with the TMR calculator.
  4. Check dry matter and feed cost independently.
  5. Review the final ration with appropriate livestock nutrition guidance before feeding a high-producing, sick or special-needs animal.

Why TMR calculations need clear assumptions

Feed moisture, ingredient analysis, animal class, intake assumptions and ingredient prices can change the practical meaning of a calculated ration. Calcora keeps these inputs visible rather than presenting one unexplained number as a universal feeding recommendation.
