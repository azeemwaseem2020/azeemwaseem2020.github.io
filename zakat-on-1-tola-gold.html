Zakat on 1 Tola Gold in Pakistan

The Zakat amount associated with one tola of gold cannot be stated as one permanent rupee figure: gold prices, purity, the Nisab and the method you follow all matter. This page explains the arithmetic and links to a calculator where you can enter the rate that applies to your date and location.

Quick calculation: if the value of the eligible gold you are assessing is PKR R, 2.5% of that value is R × 0.025. For example, if an eligible amount is valued at PKR 300,000, 2.5% arithmetic equals PKR 7,500. This is a worked arithmetic example, not a claim about today's gold price or a ruling that every person must pay Zakat on one tola alone.

How to estimate the value of 1 tola

Start with the gold rate for the correct purity and valuation date. Jewellery may be quoted at 24K, 22K, 21K or 18K; a 22K item should not automatically be valued at the full 24K rate. If the published rate is for one tola at the same purity, use that quote as the starting value. If you have a per-gram quote, multiply it by the gram conversion used for your tola standard. Shops may use different conventions, so confirm the weight basis before calculating.

Calcora's calculator uses 11.6638 grams per tola as its selectable conversion convention. Some local references use a different tola conversion. Match the scale and convention used for your actual jewellery or trusted valuation instead of mixing them.

Does owning 1 tola of gold mean Zakat is due?

Not automatically. Zakat assessment can depend on the total relevant wealth, cash and other assets, liabilities that may be deducted, ownership period and the scholarly method followed. Gold may be considered alongside other assets for a Nisab assessment under some methods. Treatment of personal jewellery also differs across scholarly opinions, so a single weight-based answer is not appropriate for every person.

For that reason, do not use a gold-only figure as a complete Zakat decision. Confirm the applicable Nisab and asset treatment with a qualified scholar or trusted religious authority, especially if your calculation involves regularly worn jewellery, mixed assets or special circumstances.

Frequently asked questions

What is Zakat on 1 tola gold?

There is no fixed rupee amount. If 2.5% applies to the eligible value being assessed, multiply that value by 0.025. The gold rate and purity determine the valuation.

Can I use the 24K rate for 22K jewellery?

Do not treat them as identical. Use a reliable quote for the correct purity or adjust the value appropriately, and confirm the method used by the jeweller or valuation source.

Is Zakat always due on personal gold jewellery?

Scholarly views differ on personal jewellery and the conditions for Zakat. Follow the qualified guidance relevant to your method rather than treating this calculator page as a fatwa.

Does the calculator show today's gold price automatically?

The calculator may offer an indicative third-party rate lookup, but external rates can be delayed or unavailable. Check the update time and verify the quote with a reliable local source; manual entry remains available.
