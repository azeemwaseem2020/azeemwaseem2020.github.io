SSP Fertilizer Calculator Guide

SSP is useful when the phosphorus target and the product analysis line up. The calculation itself is simple; the part that usually causes mistakes is mixing P with P₂O₅, area units, or an assumed product grade.

What is SSP?

SSP is a phosphorus fertilizer that can also contribute sulfur. Product grades vary by market, so do not assume one universal analysis. Enter the actual guaranteed analysis from the bag or supplier specification.

Calculate product from a phosphorus target

The basic formula is product required = nutrient required ÷ nutrient fraction. If a product contains 16% P₂O₅ and the target is 32 kg P₂O₅/ha, the arithmetic is 32 ÷ 0.16 = 200 kg product/ha.

Account for sulfur too

If the same SSP contains 11% sulfur, 200 kg/ha supplies 22 kg sulfur/ha. This is why a product can be evaluated against more than one nutrient target.

P₂O₅ is not elemental phosphorus

Fertilizer labels commonly report phosphorus as P₂O₅. For conversion to elemental phosphorus, P ≈ P₂O₅ × 0.44. Keep the target and product analysis on the same nutrient basis during calculations.

Field quantity and bags

  1. Choose acres or hectares.
  2. Enter the nutrient target for that same area basis.
  3. Enter the exact SSP analysis.
  4. Calculate product rate.
  5. Multiply by field area for total kilograms.
  6. Divide by bag size for exact bags and round up only when whole bags must be purchased.

Worked example

For 10 hectares at 200 kg SSP/ha, total product is 2,000 kg. With 50 kg bags, that is exactly 40 bags. If the product is priced at PKR 4,000 per bag, product cost is PKR 160,000 before transport and application costs.

SSP versus other phosphorus products

Do not compare products only by bag price. Compare nutrient concentration, total nutrient supplied, sulfur contribution, transport, application and the suitability of the nutrient combination for the crop and soil.

What Calcora does

Calcora can calculate product quantity, nutrient supply, field totals, bags and cost from user-provided inputs. It does not determine whether SSP is the right product for a particular field. Soil tests and local crop recommendations should guide the nutrient target.

Before you trust the number

Do one quick back-check: multiply the calculated product rate by the nutrient fraction and make sure it returns the intended nutrient rate. Then check the area conversion and the bag or volume conversion separately. Keeping those steps separate makes a wrong unit much easier to spot.

Common mistakes

  • Using a remembered SSP grade instead of the current label.
  • Mixing P with P₂O₅.
  • Mixing kg/ha and kg/acre.
  • Ignoring sulfur supplied by SSP.
  • Comparing price per bag instead of cost per nutrient unit.

FAQ

Does SSP supply nitrogen?

Some formulations may contain small amounts of nitrogen, but the guaranteed analysis on the product label should be used for calculation.

Can SSP supply sulfur?

Yes, SSP commonly contributes sulfur, but the exact percentage varies by product.

Can Calcora recommend SSP automatically?

No. It performs the arithmetic after the nutrient target and product analysis are supplied.
