Grade Calculator

Calculate marks percentage and estimate a letter grade.

Formula & assumptions

Percentage = marks obtained ÷ total marks × 100. Letter-grade cutoffs vary by school, board and university, so the displayed bands are illustrative only.

Worked marks-to-percentage example

If you score 78 marks out of 100, the percentage is 78 ÷ 100 × 100 = 78%. If you score 42 out of 60, the percentage is 42 ÷ 60 × 100 = 70%. The denominator must be the maximum marks for the same assessment, not the number of questions attempted unless each question has equal marks.

How letter grades are assigned

Schools, boards and universities set their own grade boundaries. A percentage that corresponds to a B at one institution may map to a different letter at another. Treat any letter band displayed by a calculator as illustrative unless it exactly matches your official grading policy. This calculator's marks percentage is the mathematical result; it is not an official academic decision.

Weighted assessments need a different method

If coursework, quizzes, practicals and final exams each contribute a different percentage to the final course grade, calculate each component's weighted contribution rather than combining raw marks directly. For example, an exam worth 60% and coursework worth 40% must be combined using those weights. Use a weighted-grade method when assessment weights differ.

Grade calculator FAQ

Can I enter a total other than 100?

Yes. Enter the actual maximum marks for the assessment, such as 40, 60 or 150.

Does this calculate my final course grade?

It calculates the percentage from the two entered values. A final course grade may also require component weights, grade boundaries and institutional rules.
