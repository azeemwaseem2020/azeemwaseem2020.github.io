Series resistance and circuit behavior

For ideal resistors connected in series, the total resistance equals the sum of the individual resistances. The same current passes through each series resistor in the simple circuit model.

Worked example

Three resistors of 2 Ω, 5 Ω and 8 Ω in series have a total resistance of 15 Ω.

Voltage division

The supply voltage is distributed across series resistors according to their resistance values. This makes series combinations useful in simple voltage-divider designs.

Series-resistance FAQ

Does adding a series resistor increase total resistance?

Yes, for ideal resistors in series, each additional positive resistance increases the total.
