Dice Roller

Roll virtual dice instantly with a selectable number of dice and sides.

How it works

Each die produces a random integer from 1 through the selected number of sides. The total is the sum of all rolls.

Calculation context

A dice roller generates outcomes for the selected number and type of dice. Each roll is an independent simulated outcome unless the tool's settings specify otherwise.
