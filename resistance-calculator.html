Resistance Calculator Guide

Resistance can be found from voltage and current using R = V/I.

Worked example

If a circuit has 12 V and 3 A, resistance is 12 ÷ 3 = 4 Ω.

Units and practical limits

Use volts and amps consistently. A real component may not maintain a constant resistance because temperature, material properties and operating conditions can affect its behavior.
