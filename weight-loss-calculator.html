Important assumption

This is an estimate, not a medical prescription. The page uses an approximate energy equivalent of 7,700 kcal per kg for a rough planning calculation. Real-world weight change varies with body composition, water balance, metabolism, activity and adherence.

For significant weight changes, pregnancy, eating disorders, chronic illness or medication-related concerns, use guidance from a qualified health professional.

Weight-loss calorie estimates

Weight-loss calculators generally estimate maintenance energy needs and then model a calorie deficit. The result is an estimate because actual energy expenditure changes with body size, activity, age, sex and adaptation. A calculator should support planning rather than prescribe a rigid intake.

Worked example

If estimated maintenance is 2,400 kcal/day and a planning deficit of 300 kcal/day is used, the modeled intake is 2,100 kcal/day. Real-world weight change will not necessarily follow the simple arithmetic because water, glycogen and metabolic adaptation also affect scale weight.

Health context

For sustainable weight management, diet quality, adequate protein, sleep and activity matter alongside energy balance. People who are pregnant, under 18, medically unwell or recovering from an eating disorder should use individualized professional guidance rather than a generic deficit.

FAQs

Is a calculator exact? No. Does a larger deficit always work better? No; excessively restrictive intake can be unsafe. Should daily weight be used alone? Trends over time are more informative.
