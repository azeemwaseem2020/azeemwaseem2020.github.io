DAP Fertilizer Calculator: How to Calculate 18-46-0

DAP calculations are easiest when the P₂O₅ target, nitrogen contribution, product analysis and field unit are kept on the same basis.

What does 18-46-0 mean?

A product labelled 18-46-0 contains 18% N and 46% P₂O₅ by weight, with 0% labelled K₂O. Always verify the guaranteed analysis on the actual product label.

Calculate P₂O₅ from DAP

P₂O₅ supplied = DAP weight × 0.46

For example, 100 kg of 18-46-0 DAP supplies 46 kg P₂O₅ on the labelled basis.

Calculate nitrogen from DAP

N supplied = DAP weight × 0.18. A 50 kg bag therefore contains 9 kg labelled N when the analysis is 18-46-0.

Calculate DAP from a P₂O₅ target

If a trusted recommendation requires 46 kg P₂O₅ for the selected area and the product is 46% P₂O₅, the mathematical DAP rate is 46 ÷ 0.46 = 100 kg for that same area basis.

Why DAP changes the remaining Urea requirement

If DAP already supplies part of the nitrogen target, calculate that contribution first. Only the remaining nitrogen should be supplied by another N fertilizer. This prevents overestimating the required Urea.

DAP bags and cost

Total bags = total DAP kilograms ÷ bag size. Cost = purchased quantity × applicable product price. Keep the calculated field requirement separate from the whole-bag quantity needed for purchasing.

Acres versus hectares

Use the same area basis as the recommendation. A DAP rate in kg/ha should not be multiplied directly by an acreage figure.

P₂O₅ versus elemental phosphorus

Fertilizer labels commonly express phosphorus as P₂O₅. If a recommendation is expressed as elemental P instead, use the appropriate conversion consistently rather than mixing the two units.

Common mistakes

  • Counting DAP only as phosphorus and forgetting its N contribution.
  • Confusing P₂O₅ with elemental P.
  • Using an assumed grade instead of the actual product label.
  • Mixing kg/acre and kg/ha.
  • Changing a recommended rate solely because of bag size.

FAQ

How much N is in a 50 kg bag of 18-46-0 DAP?

50 × 0.18 = 9 kg labelled N.

How much P₂O₅ is in a 50 kg bag?

50 × 0.46 = 23 kg P₂O₅.

Can DAP be combined with Urea in a fertilizer plan?

They can both appear in a nutrient plan, but the appropriate product combination and physical mixing or application method should be checked with current local guidance.

Does Calcora recommend DAP for my crop?

No. Use a crop- and soil-based recommendation, then use Calcora to perform the arithmetic.

Frequently asked questions

Are calculator results a professional recommendation?

No. The calculator applies the inputs and calculation method you provide. Field, financial or equipment decisions should be checked against current official information and the relevant professional guidance.

Why do units and assumptions matter?

A mathematically correct formula can still produce a misleading result when units, rate conventions, product analysis, efficiency or other inputs are inconsistent.

What should I verify before using the result?

Verify the input values, units, formula assumptions and any fees, product specifications or local conditions that can materially change the result.
