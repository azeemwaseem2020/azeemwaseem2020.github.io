How the PSX commission estimate works

Brokerage is commonly calculated as a percentage of transaction value, subject to the terms agreed with the broker. This calculator estimates each side separately so that a buy-side minimum or sell-side minimum can be entered independently.

Side commission = the greater of (transaction value × brokerage rate) and the entered minimum commission. Total estimated trading cost = buy commission + sell commission + other buy-side charges + other sell-side charges + additional charges entered by you. Net trade result = sell value − buy value − total estimated trading cost.

Worked example

If the buy value is PKR 100,000, the sell value is PKR 110,000 and both commission rates are entered as 0.15%, the estimated buy commission is PKR 150 and sell commission is PKR 165. With no other costs entered, the estimated net result is PKR 9,685. This is an illustration of the calculator's arithmetic, not a claim that 0.15% is every broker's current rate.

What this calculator does not assume

It does not automatically determine current PSX, CDC, SECP, exchange, clearing, settlement or tax charges. Enter verified amounts in the appropriate fields and confirm current rules with your broker and official sources. Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate PSX brokerage commission?

Multiply the transaction value by the brokerage percentage, then compare it with any applicable minimum commission. Check whether the broker applies the rate separately to buy and sell transactions.

Is the commission rate the same for every broker?

No. Rates, minimum charges and other costs can differ by broker and account agreement. Use the values in your own current schedule or contract note.

Does this result include capital gains tax?

Only if you enter a verified amount in the additional charges field. This tool does not determine your tax liability or automatically apply a current CGT rate.

Can I use this result as an investment recommendation?

No. It is an arithmetic estimate from the inputs you provide, not a recommendation to buy or sell securities.
