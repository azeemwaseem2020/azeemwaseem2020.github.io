VAT calculation

For a pre-tax price, VAT is price × VAT rate ÷ 100, and the tax-inclusive total is price plus VAT. To extract VAT from an inclusive total, use VAT = total × rate ÷ (100 + rate). The correct rate depends on the jurisdiction and transaction.

Worked example

At 15%, a pre-tax price of PKR 10,000 has PKR 1,500 VAT and a total of PKR 11,500. If PKR 11,500 is inclusive, the VAT component is PKR 1,500.

Pakistan context

Pakistan commonly uses sales-tax terminology and specific tax rules rather than a universal VAT rate for every transaction. For actual invoicing or filing, verify the applicable FBR rules and transaction category.

FAQs

Is VAT the same as sales tax? They are related consumption-tax concepts but legal treatment differs by jurisdiction. Can VAT be removed from an inclusive total? Yes, using the reverse formula. Does this file a return? No.

VAT and Sales-Tax Style Calculations

A tax calculator can add or remove a percentage from a stated price. To add a tax rate t to a net price P, tax = P × t/100 and gross price = P × (1 + t/100). If a product costs Rs 10,000 before a 15% tax, the tax is Rs 1,500 and the total is Rs 11,500.

To extract a tax already included in a gross price G, use net = G ÷ (1 + t/100), then tax = G − net. For a Rs 11,500 gross price at 15%, the net amount is Rs 10,000 and the tax component is Rs 1,500. This distinction matters because simply taking 15% of the gross price would overstate the tax.

Pakistan commonly uses sales-tax terminology rather than VAT in many contexts, and applicable rates can vary by goods, services, sector and legal status. The calculator performs percentage arithmetic; it does not determine the legally applicable tax rate or whether a transaction is taxable.

Practical use

Businesses should reconcile calculator results with invoices, accounting records and the applicable tax rules. Discounts, withholding, exemptions and tax-inclusive pricing can change the final amount. Keep the tax rate and pricing basis clearly documented when comparing quotations.

FAQs

How do I add tax? Multiply the net amount by the tax rate and add it to the net amount.

How do I remove included tax? Divide the gross price by 1 + tax rate as a decimal.

Does this calculator choose the correct Pakistani tax rate? No. Verify the current applicable rate for the transaction.
