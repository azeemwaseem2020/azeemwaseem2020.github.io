How VAT is calculated

Value-added tax is commonly calculated as a percentage of a taxable amount. A tax-exclusive price has VAT added to it, while a tax-inclusive price requires the VAT portion to be extracted from the total.

Example

At a 15% VAT rate on PKR 20,000 before VAT, the VAT is PKR 3,000 and the total is PKR 23,000. The correct result depends on the rate and the pricing basis entered.

Business records

For business accounting, the actual VAT treatment can depend on registration status, exemptions, input tax, output tax and local rules. This calculator is best used for straightforward estimates.

VAT FAQ

Can VAT be calculated from a tax-inclusive total?

Yes. The VAT portion must be separated using the applicable VAT rate rather than simply multiplying the total by that rate.

VAT Calculator: Add Tax or Extract It from a Total

This calculator supports two different tasks: adding a percentage to a net price, or separating the tax component from a price that already includes tax. These operations are not interchangeable. First decide whether the entered amount is before tax or tax-inclusive, then use the matching result.

Formulas

For a net price P and rate r expressed as a decimal, VAT = P × r and gross price = P × (1 + r). For a VAT-inclusive price G, the net amount is G ÷ (1 + r), and the VAT component is G − [G ÷ (1 + r)]. Do not calculate tax-inclusive VAT by simply multiplying the gross amount by the rate; that overstates the tax component.

Worked example

At an illustrative 18% rate, a net price of PKR 1,000 has VAT of PKR 180 and a gross price of PKR 1,180. If PKR 1,180 is already tax-inclusive, extracting the tax gives PKR 180 and the net amount is PKR 1,000. The example shows the mathematics only; it does not determine whether a product or service is taxable or what rate applies to a real invoice.

How to use the calculator

  1. Enter the amount shown on your quote or invoice.
  2. Enter the rate that is actually applicable to the transaction.
  3. Read both outputs carefully and identify which one corresponds to your input basis.
  4. Check invoice rounding rules and whether the quoted price includes other charges.

Pakistan tax note

VAT is commonly used as a general term internationally, while Pakistan's indirect-tax treatment may involve sales tax and separate federal or provincial rules depending on the goods, services and jurisdiction. Do not assume that the generic label “VAT” makes a transaction subject to one universal rate. For filing, invoicing or compliance, verify the current rule with the relevant tax authority or a qualified adviser.

Frequently asked questions

Why is tax extracted from a gross total different?

Because the rate applies to the net price, not directly to the tax-inclusive total.

Does this confirm the legal rate?

No. It calculates using the percentage you enter.

Can I use this for an official tax return?

Use it as an arithmetic check only and reconcile amounts with the applicable rules and records.
