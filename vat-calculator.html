VAT calculation

For a pre-tax price, VAT is price × VAT rate ÷ 100, and the tax-inclusive total is price plus VAT. To extract VAT from an inclusive total, use VAT = total × rate ÷ (100 + rate). The correct rate depends on the jurisdiction and transaction.

Worked example

At 15%, a pre-tax price of PKR 10,000 has PKR 1,500 VAT and a total of PKR 11,500. If PKR 11,500 is inclusive, the VAT component is PKR 1,500.

Pakistan context

Pakistan commonly uses sales-tax terminology and specific tax rules rather than a universal VAT rate for every transaction. For actual invoicing or filing, verify the applicable FBR rules and transaction category.

FAQs

Is VAT the same as sales tax? They are related consumption-tax concepts but legal treatment differs by jurisdiction. Can VAT be removed from an inclusive total? Yes, using the reverse formula. Does this file a return? No.
