SLEEP PLANNING

Sleep Calculator

Plan approximate bedtimes from your desired wake-up time. The calculation is a simple planning aid, not a medical assessment.

How to use the Sleep Calculator

  1. Choose your target wake-up time.
  2. Select Suggest Bedtimes.
  3. Review the approximate options for four, five and six sleep-cycle intervals.
  4. Choose a schedule that allows enough total sleep and fits your routine.

How this sleep calculator works

This tool counts backward using approximate 90-minute sleep-cycle intervals and adds about 15 minutes as a simple sleep-onset allowance. Real sleep cycles vary between people and across the night, so the times should be treated as approximate.

Example

If you need to wake at 7:00 AM, six 90-minute intervals plus 15 minutes gives an approximate bedtime of 9:45 PM. Five intervals gives about 11:15 PM.

Sleep duration matters

Sleep-cycle timing is only one way to plan a schedule. Consistent sleep and wake times and getting enough total sleep are generally more important than trying to match an exact cycle boundary.

Important health note

This calculator does not diagnose sleep disorders or determine an individual's medical sleep needs. Persistent insomnia, excessive daytime sleepiness, loud snoring, breathing pauses, or other concerning symptoms should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.

Sleep Calculator FAQ

Are sleep cycles exactly 90 minutes? No. Ninety minutes is a convenient approximation; actual cycles vary.

Does this calculator tell me how much sleep I need? No. It only suggests times based on the selected wake-up time and approximate intervals.

Why add 15 minutes? It is a simple planning allowance for falling asleep, not a personalized measurement.
