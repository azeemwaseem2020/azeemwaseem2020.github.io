PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy matters.

This policy explains how Calcora handles information when you use the website and its free calculators. We aim to collect as little information as reasonably necessary and to be clear about third-party services that may process information.

Last reviewed: October 9, 2026. Calculator inputs are primarily processed in your browser. Do not enter passwords, bank account details, identity documents or other sensitive personal information into calculator fields.

1. Website hosting and technical logs

Calcora is published using GitHub Pages. When a browser requests a page, the hosting and network infrastructure may process technical information such as IP address, request time, requested URL, browser or device details, and security-related logs. These records are governed by the hosting provider’s own practices. Read the GitHub Privacy Statement for more information.

2. Calculator inputs

Calcora's calculators are primarily designed to perform calculations in your browser. Values such as loan amount, interest rate, share price, appliance load or feed inputs are used to produce the requested result. The website is not intended to collect those values as a personal profile.

3. Information you provide

Calculator fields are not intended to be a submission form. If a page offers a feature that sends information to a third party, that feature and its provider may have separate terms or privacy practices. Please review those terms before using such a feature.

If you contact Calcora by email or another available contact method, information you include may be used to understand and respond to your request. Please avoid sending confidential information unless it is necessary for the request.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

Calcora may use basic browser functionality needed for the site to work. Third-party services used on the site may use cookies or similar technologies for advertising, measurement, security or other service functions. You can usually manage or block cookies through your browser settings.

5. Analytics

If analytics services are enabled, they may process information such as pages visited, approximate device or browser information, referral sources, general usage patterns, and technical information about the browser or device. Analytics is used to understand site usage and improve content and functionality. This helps us understand which pages are useful and where the website needs improvement.

6. Advertising and Google AdSense

Calcora may display ads through third-party advertising providers, including Google AdSense, when the service is enabled and approved. Depending on configuration, your region, and the consent or privacy choices available to you, these providers may use cookies, IP addresses, device or browser information, and other identifiers to serve, limit, measure, or personalize ads and to detect invalid activity. Third-party providers process information under their own privacy policies; Calcora does not control their independent processing.

Learn how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services in the Google partner-sites explanation. You can review or change Google ad-personalization settings at Google Ads Settings and read Google’s advertising technologies policy. These controls may not disable all advertising or all data processing by other providers.

Where required by law or by an advertising platform’s rules, a consent message or additional privacy controls may be shown. The options available depend on your location, browser, and the services currently configured on the site.

7. External links

Calcora may link to external websites, tools or resources. Those websites have their own privacy practices. Review the privacy policy of an external service before providing it with personal information.

8. Data security

Reasonable steps are taken to maintain the website, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Avoid submitting confidential information through ordinary website forms or email.

9. Children's privacy

Calcora is a general-purpose calculator website. It is not designed to intentionally collect sensitive personal information from children.

10. Your choices and privacy requests

You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings, though some site features may not work as intended. You can also use the Google controls linked in the advertising section and any consent or privacy controls presented on the site. These controls are provider-specific and may not apply to every technology used by every third party.

For a question or request concerning information you sent directly to Calcora, use the contact page. Please identify the request clearly and do not include passwords, identity documents, or unnecessary sensitive data. We will review requests and respond as reasonably possible, subject to applicable law and the limits of information actually held by Calcora.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, analytics, advertising or applicable requirements change. The review date at the top of this page shows when the policy was last checked.

Privacy question? Please use the Calcora contact page.
