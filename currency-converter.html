Currency Converter

Convert an amount between currencies using an exchange rate you can edit. Useful for USD, EUR, GBP, PKR and other currency pairs.

How the currency conversion works

Target amount = source amount × exchange rate. For example, if 1 USD = 280 PKR, then 100 USD = 28,000 PKR.

Important rate note

Rates vary by market, provider and time. This page intentionally makes the rate editable instead of presenting a stale fixed quote as a live market price.

Calculation context

Currency conversion applies an exchange rate to an amount in one currency to estimate its value in another. Rates can change over time, so a conversion result should be treated as rate-dependent.

Using an exchange-rate conversion

Currency conversion multiplies an amount in one currency by an exchange rate expressed in the chosen direction. The most important check is direction: PKR per USD is not the same number as USD per PKR. Rates also change over time, and a bank, card network or money changer may apply a spread or fee.

Formula

Converted amount = source amount × quoted rate when the rate is expressed as target currency per one source unit. If the quoted rate is reversed, divide instead.

Worked example

If the assumed rate is Rs 300 per US dollar, US$100 converts to Rs 30,000 before fees or spreads. The reverse quote would be 1 ÷ 300 = 0.003333 USD per PKR.

Pakistan-specific example

A person in Pakistan comparing an international purchase or remittance should distinguish between a reference market rate and the actual rate offered by a bank or exchange service. Transfer fees can materially change the amount received.

Common questions

Is the displayed rate guaranteed? No, unless supplied by the provider under a binding transaction.

Why did I receive less? Fees, spreads and timing can reduce the final amount.

Can I convert PKR to USD and back? Yes, but the two transactions may use different rates.

How often should rates be refreshed? For real transactions, use a current rate from the relevant provider.
