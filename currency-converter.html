Currency Converter

Convert an amount between currencies using an exchange rate you can edit. Useful for USD, EUR, GBP, PKR and other currency pairs.

How the currency conversion works

Target amount = source amount × exchange rate. For example, if 1 USD = 280 PKR, then 100 USD = 28,000 PKR.

Important rate note

Rates vary by market, provider and time. This page intentionally makes the rate editable instead of presenting a stale fixed quote as a live market price.

Frequently asked questions

Does this use live exchange rates?

This calculator uses the rate entered on the page. Check a reliable current financial source before making a transaction.

How do I convert currencies?

Enter the amount and target units per one source unit, then calculate.

Why can a bank quote differ?

Providers can add spreads, commissions and other fees.

How currency conversion works

A currency conversion multiplies an amount by an exchange rate between the selected currencies. Market rates can move continuously, while banks and payment providers may apply their own spreads or fees.

Check the rate before making a payment

This calculator is useful for estimates and comparisons. For an actual transfer, card purchase or settlement, confirm the rate, timestamp, fees and provider terms that will apply to the transaction.
