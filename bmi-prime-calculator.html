Calculate BMI Prime from a BMI value and understand the normalized ratio.

BMI Prime formula

BMI Prime = BMI ÷ 25. The index compares BMI with a reference value of 25. It is a mathematical transformation of BMI, not a separate diagnostic measure.

Worked example

If BMI is 22.5, BMI Prime = 22.5 ÷ 25 = 0.90. A value of 1.00 corresponds to BMI 25 under this definition.

Limitations

BMI does not directly measure body fat and can be interpreted differently across people and populations. BMI Prime inherits those limitations and should not be used alone for medical decisions.

What BMI Prime represents

BMI Prime is a normalized way to compare a person's BMI with a reference BMI of 25. The basic formula is BMI Prime = BMI ÷ 25. A value of 1.00 therefore corresponds to BMI 25. BMI Prime is derived from BMI and should not be treated as a separate clinical diagnosis.

Worked example

If height is 1.70 m and weight is 70 kg, BMI = 70 ÷ 1.70² ≈ 24.22. BMI Prime is 24.22 ÷ 25 ≈ 0.97. The calculation is mathematical; interpretation still depends on the appropriate health context.

A practical Pakistan example

A university student in Pakistan can use BMI Prime as a simple normalized comparison when reviewing a BMI result. It should be considered alongside age, sex, body composition and other relevant factors rather than used alone to judge health.

Common mistakes

Use kilograms and metres in the BMI formula, and remember that BMI Prime depends on the reference value of 25. It does not directly measure body fat.
