Roof pitch calculation

Roof pitch describes the slope of a roof. A common representation is rise over run, such as 6:12, meaning 6 units of vertical rise for every 12 units of horizontal run. The slope angle can be found from angle = arctan(rise ÷ run).

Worked example

A roof that rises 4 ft over a 12 ft horizontal run has pitch 4:12. Its angle is arctan(4/12) ≈ 18.43°. Keep rise and run in the same units.

Practical example

Roofing, solar-panel planning and drainage design may use different conventions. In Pakistan, confirm the local drawing or contractor's definition before ordering materials. The calculator does not determine structural safety or required load capacity.

FAQs

Is pitch the same as roof angle? No; pitch is commonly a rise/run ratio, while angle is measured in degrees. Can pitch be greater than 12:12? Yes. Does pitch determine roof area? It helps derive slope length, but plan dimensions are also required.
