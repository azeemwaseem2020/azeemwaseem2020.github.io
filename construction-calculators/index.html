What you can estimate

Concrete and bulk materials

Use volume and area calculations to estimate concrete, gravel, mulch or topsoil. Confirm depth, compaction and delivery units with your supplier.

Paint, flooring and tiles

Measure wall or floor area, account for openings and follow product coverage rates. Tile layout and cut waste depend on the room shape and pattern.

Measurements and costs

Convert room dimensions, square footage and local land measurements, then use the price-per-square-foot tool for a simple cost estimate.

How to choose the right calculator

Start with the quantity you need to estimate, use consistent units, and check the assumptions before purchasing materials or making a decision. Calculator results are estimates: site conditions, product labels, local rules and professional specifications can change the final requirement.

Accuracy tip: Keep a note of your dimensions, units, material type and any allowance for waste. Do not add a waste factor twice if the calculator already includes one.
