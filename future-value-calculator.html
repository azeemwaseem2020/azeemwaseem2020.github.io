Calculation context

Future-value calculations estimate how a current amount and optional recurring contributions may grow over time under an assumed return and compounding schedule. This is distinct from ROI, which measures return relative to cost, and from loan calculators, which model borrowing.

Understanding future value

Future value estimates what a current amount or a series of contributions could become under a specified rate and time period. The result depends heavily on the assumed return and timing of contributions.

Compounding and contribution timing

Money invested earlier has more time to compound. Regular contributions also produce different results depending on whether they occur at the beginning or end of each period.

Nominal value vs purchasing power

A larger future amount is a nominal result. Inflation can reduce what that amount can buy, so long-term planning should consider both projected balance and real purchasing power.

Future-value FAQ

Is future value a guaranteed amount?

No. It is a projection based on the rate, timing and other assumptions entered.
