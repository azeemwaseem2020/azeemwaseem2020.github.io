Calculation context

Future-value calculations estimate how a current amount and optional recurring contributions may grow over time under an assumed return and compounding schedule. This is distinct from ROI, which measures return relative to cost, and from loan calculators, which model borrowing.

Understanding future value

Future value estimates what a current amount or a series of contributions could become under a specified rate and time period. The result depends heavily on the assumed return and timing of contributions.

Compounding and contribution timing

Money invested earlier has more time to compound. Regular contributions also produce different results depending on whether they occur at the beginning or end of each period.

Nominal value vs purchasing power

A larger future amount is a nominal result. Inflation can reduce what that amount can buy, so long-term planning should consider both projected balance and real purchasing power. This calculator does not forecast inflation-adjusted value unless you separately adjust the assumed return.

Worked example

With a starting balance of 10,000, an annual nominal rate of 6%, monthly compounding, a 10-year horizon and a 100 contribution at each month's end, the estimate combines growth on the starting balance with the accumulated monthly deposits. The final value is a scenario, not a promise: changing the rate, duration or deposit timing changes the result.

How to compare scenarios

Change one assumption at a time to see what drives the difference. Compare the same starting amount, time horizon and contribution schedule when assessing two rates. Check whether a quoted return is nominal or effective and whether fees or taxes reduce the amount that remains invested. A constant-rate model is most useful for planning and sensitivity checks, not for predicting variable market returns.

Future-value FAQ

Is future value a guaranteed amount?

No. It is a projection based on the rate, timing and other assumptions entered.

Does the calculator include monthly contributions?

Yes. Enter a contribution amount and choose whether it is added at the beginning or end of each month. The contribution schedule is modeled monthly even when the starting balance uses another compounding frequency.

Does this include taxes or inflation?

No. The displayed result is nominal and excludes taxes, fees and inflation.
