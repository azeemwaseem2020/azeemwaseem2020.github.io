Pakistan Salary Tax Guide 2026–27 — Roman Urdu

Is guide mein salary tax ka basic hisaab, monthly aur annual salary ka farq, aur FBR se rate verify karne ka tareeqa asaan Roman Urdu mein diya gaya hai.

Salary Tax Calculator kholein

Gross salary, taxable salary aur take-home pay

Gross salary woh amount hai jo deductions se pehle hoti hai. Taxable salary woh income hai jis par relevant qanoon ke mutabiq tax calculate hota hai. Take-home pay woh raqam hai jo tax aur doosri payroll deductions ke baad milti hai. Ye teenon amounts mukhtalif ho sakti hain.

Tax Year 2027 salary tax slabs

Calculator mein diye gaye salaried-person slabs ke mutabiq annual taxable salary PKR 600,000 tak ho to displayed table mein tax 0% hai. Agli band mein PKR 600,000 se zyada amount par 1%, PKR 1,200,000 se zyada wali band mein PKR 6,000 plus excess par 11%, aur higher bands mein relevant fixed amount plus marginal rate apply hota hai. Mukammal slab table ke liye Urdu guide ya English guide dekhein.

Monthly salary ki misaal

Agar taxable monthly salary PKR 100,000 ho aur poore 12 months same rahe, to annual taxable salary PKR 1,200,000 hogi. Is page par diye gaye Tax Year 2027 slabs ke mutabiq estimated annual tax PKR 6,000 aur monthly average PKR 500 banta hai. Yeh simple calculation hai, guaranteed payroll deduction nahi.

Bonus aur allowances

Bonus, commission, arrears, accommodation, vehicle ya medical benefits ka tax treatment har case mein ek jaisa nahi hota. Payslip par likha naam akela decide nahi karta ke amount taxable hai ya exempt. Apni income ki classification aur current qanoon verify karein.

Filer vs non-filer

ATL status kuch transactions par withholding tax ko change kar sakta hai. Yeh salary slab calculation se alag cheez hai. Filer vs non-filer tax rate table mein bank profit aur property transaction ki selected rates compare karein.

Return filing deadline

Tax Year 2026 ki deadline ke bare mein 24 October 2026 tak report hui extension aur official verification steps ke liye FBR return deadline guide dekhein. Filing se pehle latest official notice check karna zaroori hai.
