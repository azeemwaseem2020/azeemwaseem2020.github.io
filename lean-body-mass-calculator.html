Lean Body Mass Calculator

Estimate lean body mass from body weight and body fat percentage.

Enter values and calculate.

Lean body mass formula

LBM = body weight × (1 − body-fat percentage ÷ 100). This simple calculation estimates the portion of body weight that is not body fat.

Worked example

At 80 kg body weight and 20% body fat: LBM = 80 × (1 − 0.20) = 64 kg.

How to use

  1. Enter body weight in your chosen mass unit.
  2. Enter body-fat percentage from a reliable measurement or estimate.
  3. Calculate lean body mass.
  4. Remember that the result includes water, muscle, bone and other non-fat tissues.
