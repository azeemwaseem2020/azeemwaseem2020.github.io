How elapsed time is calculated

The tool converts each clock time to seconds, subtracts the start from the end, and reports the result as hours, minutes and seconds. If the end time is earlier than the start time, it is treated as a next-day finish.

Time calculation examples

  • 09:00 to 17:30 = 8 hours 30 minutes.
  • 22:30 to 01:15 = 2 hours 45 minutes when the finish is on the following day.
  • Adding 1 hour 30 minutes to 10:20 gives 11:50.

Elapsed time and clock arithmetic

Time calculations are easiest when clock readings are converted to a common unit before subtraction. This avoids mistakes when minutes or seconds cross an hour boundary. Calcora treats an end time earlier than the start time as a finish on the following day for elapsed-time calculations. That assumption is useful for overnight shifts, travel and sleep schedules, but it should not be used when the two times belong to different calendar dates that are already known.

Formula

Convert a clock reading to seconds from midnight: total seconds = 3600 × hours + 60 × minutes + seconds. Elapsed time = end seconds − start seconds. For an overnight interval, add 86,400 seconds before subtracting. For addition or subtraction, convert the duration to seconds, apply the operation, then normalize back to hours, minutes and seconds.

Worked example

From 09:00:00 to 17:30:00, the elapsed duration is 8 hours 30 minutes. From 22:30:00 to 01:15:00, the next-day assumption gives 2 hours 45 minutes. Adding 1 hour 30 minutes to 10:20 gives 11:50.

Pakistan-specific example

A shop, factory or transport operator in Pakistan can use the calculator to check a shift such as 8:00 pm to 5:00 am. The overnight assumption gives 9 hours before any break is deducted. If a worker has an unpaid one-hour break, subtract that break separately from the gross elapsed time.

Common questions

Does this calculate dates? No. For multi-day intervals use a date calculator.

What if the end time is earlier? It is treated as the following day.

Are breaks included? Yes, unless you subtract them from the calculated duration.

Can seconds be included? Yes. The inputs support hours, minutes and seconds.
