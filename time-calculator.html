How elapsed time is calculated

The tool converts each clock time to seconds, subtracts the start from the end, and reports the result as hours, minutes and seconds. If the end time is earlier than the start time, it is treated as a next-day finish.

Time calculation examples

  • 09:00 to 17:30 = 8 hours 30 minutes.
  • 22:30 to 01:15 = 2 hours 45 minutes when the finish is on the following day.
  • Adding 1 hour 30 minutes to 10:20 gives 11:50.

How to calculate elapsed time

Enter the starting and ending times, or use the add/subtract controls when you want to build a duration. The calculator keeps hours, minutes and seconds in their proper ranges.

Crossing midnight

When an interval passes midnight, the calendar date matters. For example, the time from 23:30 to 01:15 is not a negative duration; it spans 1 hour 45 minutes when the second time is on the following day.

Common uses

Use elapsed-time calculations for work shifts, travel, study sessions, schedules, project tracking and event planning.

Frequently asked questions

Can a time calculation cross midnight?

Yes. The calendar day changes at midnight, so the elapsed interval should be interpreted across the following day.
