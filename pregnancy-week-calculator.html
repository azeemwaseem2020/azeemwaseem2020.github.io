Pregnancy Week Calculator: Gestational Age in Weeks and Days

Pregnancy is commonly tracked using gestational age, expressed as completed weeks and additional days. Clinicians usually count from the first day of the last menstrual period (LMP), rather than from the conception date. This means the gestational age can be about two weeks greater than the time since fertilisation in a typical cycle.

Calculation method

A basic LMP-based estimate counts the number of calendar days between the first day of the last menstrual period and the date being checked. Divide the elapsed days by 7: the whole-number quotient is completed weeks and the remainder is extra days. For example, 73 elapsed days equals 10 weeks and 3 days. If the calculator uses a due date, it may instead estimate remaining time and infer the current week.

Worked example

If 73 days have passed since the LMP, 73 ÷ 7 gives 10 full weeks with 3 days remaining. The gestational age is therefore 10 weeks 3 days, often written 10+3. Always check whether the page displays completed weeks or the week currently underway, because “10 weeks pregnant” can be used differently in casual conversation.

How to use this calculator

  1. Identify the date field: last menstrual period, due date or another reference date.
  2. Enter the date carefully, including the year.
  3. Calculate and read weeks and days together rather than rounding to a whole week.
  4. Compare the estimate with the gestational age recorded by your maternity provider.

Features and practical applications

Assumptions and limitations

LMP dating assumes the entered date is correct and can be less reliable when cycles are irregular, ovulation occurs earlier or later than expected, or the date is uncertain. Ultrasound measurements and clinical history may lead a clinician to adjust the estimated dating. If you are unsure of your dates, avoid treating a calculator result as definitive.

Do not use gestational age alone to interpret symptoms or decide whether a medication or procedure is appropriate. Ask a qualified maternity professional about recommended screening and urgent concerns. Severe pain, heavy bleeding, fainting or other alarming symptoms need timely medical assessment regardless of the week displayed by an online tool.

Frequently asked questions

Why is pregnancy counted from the last period?

The LMP is often easier to identify than the exact ovulation or fertilisation date, so it is the standard dating convention.

What does 10+3 mean?

It means 10 completed weeks and 3 additional days of gestational age.

Can this calculator tell whether my baby is developing normally?

No. It estimates timing only; growth and health require appropriate clinical assessment.
