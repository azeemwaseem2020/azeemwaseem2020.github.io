What is included?

The core payment calculation estimates principal and interest. Property taxes, insurance, HOA fees, lender fees and other closing costs are not automatically part of the principal-and-interest result unless the calculator explicitly asks for them.

Mortgage payment formula

A standard fixed-rate mortgage payment uses the reducing-balance formula M = P × r(1+r)n ÷ ((1+r)n − 1), where P is principal, r is the periodic interest rate and n is the number of payments. An annual rate must be converted to the payment period before use.

Worked example

For a PKR 5,000,000 loan at 12% annual interest with monthly payments over 20 years, the monthly rate is 0.12/12 and n is 240. The calculator uses those values to determine the scheduled monthly payment; total interest is the sum of payments minus principal.

Pakistan context

Home finance offers can differ in benchmark, repricing rules, fees, insurance, taxes and early-settlement conditions. A mathematical fixed-rate result should not be treated as a quotation from a bank. Compare the full repayment schedule and contractual rate structure.

Limitations

The standard formula assumes a constant rate and regular payments. Variable-rate or Islamic financing structures may require different calculations.

FAQs

Does a longer term reduce the payment? Usually, but it generally increases total interest. Does the rate need to be monthly? The formula needs a periodic rate. Are fees included? Only if the calculator explicitly models them.
