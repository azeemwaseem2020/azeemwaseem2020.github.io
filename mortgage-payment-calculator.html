What is included?

The core payment calculation estimates principal and interest. Property taxes, insurance, HOA fees, lender fees and other closing costs are not automatically part of the principal-and-interest result unless the calculator explicitly asks for them.

Mortgage payment formula

For a fixed-rate loan, the monthly payment is calculated from principal P, monthly interest rate r and number of monthly payments n: M = P × r(1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1). Convert an annual percentage rate to a monthly decimal rate by dividing by 100 and then by 12. If the interest rate is zero, payment is principal divided by the number of payments.

Worked example

For a hypothetical loan of 200,000 at 6% annual interest over 30 years, n = 360 and the monthly rate is 0.005. The principal-and-interest payment is approximately 1,199.10 per month. The exact lender payment can differ due to rounding, fees and loan terms.

What is not included?

This basic estimate does not automatically include property tax, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance, HOA fees, closing costs or variable-rate changes. Review the full payment breakdown and lender terms before comparing offers.
