Converting miles to kilometres

Use kilometres = miles × 1.609344. This is the exact international mile-to-kilometre relationship.

Worked example

A 60-mile journey is 60 × 1.609344 = 96.56064 km, commonly displayed as 96.56 km or about 96.6 km depending on the required precision.

A practical Pakistan example

If a foreign vehicle specification gives fuel economy or a route distance in miles, converting the distance to kilometres makes it easier to compare with locally used metric measurements.

Common mistakes

Do not confuse miles with nautical miles. This conversion uses the international statute mile.

Miles to Kilometres: Conversion Formula

To convert statute miles to kilometres, multiply miles by 1.609344. For example, 10 miles equals 16.09344 km. To convert back, divide kilometres by 1.609344. The calculator uses the exact standard conversion factor before rounding the displayed result.

For a practical travel example, a route listed as 25 miles is about 40.234 km. In Pakistan, road distances are normally presented in kilometres, so this conversion can help compare a foreign map, running plan or travel reference with local distances.

Do not confuse statute miles with nautical miles. One nautical mile is based on a different definition and is approximately 1.852 km. Also remember that converting units does not change the physical distance; it only changes the numerical representation.
