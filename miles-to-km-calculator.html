Converting miles to kilometres

Use kilometres = miles × 1.609344. This is the exact international mile-to-kilometre relationship.

Worked example

A 60-mile journey is 60 × 1.609344 = 96.56064 km, commonly displayed as 96.56 km or about 96.6 km depending on the required precision.

A practical Pakistan example

If a foreign vehicle specification gives fuel economy or a route distance in miles, converting the distance to kilometres makes it easier to compare with locally used metric measurements.

Common mistakes

Do not confuse miles with nautical miles. This conversion uses the international statute mile.

FAQ

How many kilometres are in one mile? 1.609344 km.

What is 100 miles in kilometres? About 160.934 km.

Does rounding matter? It depends on whether the result is for a rough estimate or precise reporting.

Conversion accuracy

One international mile equals exactly 1.609344 kilometres. Thus 60 miles is 96.56064 km, usually rounded to 96.56 km. Use the precision appropriate to the original distance. For road travel, route distance can differ from a simple conversion because the underlying route itself may be measured or rounded differently.

Practical example: if a vehicle journey is 120 miles, the unit conversion is about 193.12 km. Fuel consumption calculations should then use the converted distance with the vehicle's fuel-economy unit defined clearly.
