Formula and notes

This calculator provides a mathematical estimate based on the values entered. Real-world outcomes can differ.

Conversion formula

One international mile equals exactly 1.609344 kilometers. The calculator multiplies the entered mile value by 1.609344. For example, 5 miles equals 8.04672 kilometers.

Practical use

Use this conversion for travel distances, running routes, vehicle information and other measurements that use miles while your reference uses kilometers.

Worked conversion

For 5 miles, multiply by 1.609344 to get 8.04672 km. The same exact factor can be used for larger distances; round only at the final step when precision matters.

What the result means

The result is a distance conversion. It does not estimate driving time, walking time or fuel use because those depend on conditions beyond the distance value.

Common miles-to-kilometers conversions

Useful reference values include 1 mile = 1.609344 km, 5 miles = 8.04672 km, 10 miles = 16.09344 km and 26.2 miles ≈ 42.1648 km. The last example is close to the standard marathon distance, which is 26 miles and 385 yards. These values are conversions only; they do not indicate how long a trip or run will take.

International miles and rounding

This calculator uses the international mile. Multiply miles by 1.609344 to convert to kilometers, and keep the full result during intermediate steps before rounding to a useful number of decimal places. For a road sign, fitness summary or travel plan, one or two decimal places may be sufficient, but follow the precision requested by the source or application.

Choose the correct distance tool

A unit conversion does not account for route bends, traffic, elevation or GPS measurement error. If you need a road route, use a mapping service; if you are converting kilometers back to miles, use the Kilometers to Miles Calculator. For pace calculations, convert distance first and then use a pace or speed calculator with compatible time units.
