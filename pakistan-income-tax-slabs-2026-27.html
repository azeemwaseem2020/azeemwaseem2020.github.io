How the slabs work

Pakistan salary income tax is progressive: the marginal percentage applies only to the portion falling in that band, while the fixed amount carries forward the tax accumulated in lower bands. That is why the headline marginal rate should not be confused with the effective tax rate on the whole salary.

Worked examples: why the marginal rate is not the tax on your whole salary

Suppose taxable income is PKR 1,500,000. The first PKR 600,000 falls in the 0% band, the next PKR 600,000 is taxed at 1%, and the remaining PKR 300,000 is taxed at 11%. Under the listed slab formula, that gives PKR 42,000 of tax before any other applicable adjustments.

At PKR 3,000,000 of taxable income, the calculation is PKR 116,000 plus 20% of PKR 800,000, giving PKR 276,000. The 20% marginal rate therefore does not mean that the entire PKR 3 million is taxed at 20%.

How to use these slabs correctly

  1. Start with taxable income, not simply gross salary.
  2. Identify the band containing the taxable income.
  3. Apply the fixed amount plus the percentage shown for the excess over that band's threshold.
  4. Use the dedicated Pakistan Salary Tax Calculator for an estimate based on the page's stated assumptions.
  5. For filing or payroll decisions, verify the applicable law and current FBR material.
