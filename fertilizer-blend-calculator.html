Using the blend calculator

Enter the nutrient target and the analysis of the fertilizers you have available. The calculator works out the mathematical blend and lets you check the resulting quantities before you scale it to a field or batch.

Important: use a soil-test, crop recommendation or qualified agronomist's nutrient target. Calcora performs the blend arithmetic; it does not independently prescribe a fertilizer rate.

Fertilizer blend formula and method

For three products, the calculator solves three simultaneous equations: each product's N, P₂O₅ and K₂O contribution must combine to the entered nutrient targets. Product percentages are converted to decimal fractions before solving.

Worked example

Using Urea 46-0-0, DAP 18-46-0 and MOP 0-0-60, DAP supplies both N and P₂O₅ while MOP supplies K₂O. The exact blend depends on the target values you enter.

How the fertilizer blend calculation works

For three products, Calcora solves three simultaneous equations using the entered N, P₂O₅ and K₂O analyses. Percentages are converted to fractions before the calculation. The result is then scaled to the selected field area.

Urea, DAP and MOP example

The default example uses Urea 46-0-0, DAP 18-46-0 and MOP 0-0-60. DAP contributes both nitrogen and P₂O₅, so its nitrogen contribution is included before the remaining nitrogen requirement is assigned to the other products.

Oxide versus elemental nutrients

Fertilizer labels commonly express phosphorus as P₂O₅ and potassium as K₂O. If your recommendation uses elemental P or K, convert it to the same basis before entering the target. Do not mix elemental and oxide units in one calculation.

Important limitations

  • A mathematical solution is not automatically an agronomic recommendation.
  • Some product combinations cannot meet all three targets with non-negative rates.
  • Results depend entirely on the product analysis and target values entered.
  • Cost estimates use only the bag size and price entered.
  • Physical compatibility, particle size, application method and local agronomic guidance must be checked separately.

Field-ready blend workflow

  1. Start with a current soil test or trusted crop recommendation.
  2. Confirm whether targets use N-P₂O₅-K₂O or elemental N-P-K.
  3. Enter the exact guaranteed analysis printed on each fertilizer bag.
  4. Calculate and review the product quantities.
  5. Check practical blending, handling and application requirements before use.

FERTILIZER BLEND FAQ

Common fertilizer blending questions

What does an NPK blend target mean?

It describes the required amounts of nitrogen, P₂O₅ and K₂O that the selected fertilizer products must supply on the chosen area or batch basis.

Can the calculator use Urea, DAP and MOP?

Yes, when you enter the actual nutrient analysis for each product. Other products can also be entered when their analysis is known.

Why might a target have no practical blend?

The available product analyses may not mathematically provide the requested nutrient combination with non-negative quantities, or the resulting mix may not be practical to measure or apply.

Fertilizer blending calculation context

Fertilizer blending works backward from a nutrient target to the quantities of available fertilizer products. Product analysis, target nutrients, area and total blend weight must be kept consistent when checking the result.
