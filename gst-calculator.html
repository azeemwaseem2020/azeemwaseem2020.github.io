GST Calculator

Add GST to a tax-exclusive price or extract GST from a tax-inclusive amount using your chosen GST rate.

GST formulas

To add GST: GST = base amount × rate ÷ 100, then total = base amount + GST. To extract GST from an inclusive price: pre-tax amount = inclusive amount ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100), and GST = inclusive amount − pre-tax amount.

GST example

For a price of 1,000 and an 18% GST rate, GST is 180 and the tax-inclusive total is 1,180. If 1,180 already includes 18% GST, the pre-tax amount is 1,000 and the included GST is 180.

GST rates and location

GST/VAT rules and rates vary by country, jurisdiction, product, exemptions, and tax period. This calculator does not determine which rate legally applies to you; enter the rate relevant to your transaction.

How to use the GST calculator

Choose whether you are adding GST or extracting GST from an inclusive amount. Enter the price and rate, then calculate. Keep the currency and amount basis consistent when comparing results.

Understanding the result

A tax-exclusive amount and a tax-inclusive amount are not interchangeable. When GST is added, the calculator starts with the base price and calculates tax on that base. When GST is removed, it works backwards from the total and separates the included tax mathematically. This distinction is important when checking invoices, retail prices or business calculations.

The arithmetic does not decide whether a transaction is taxable, exempt, zero-rated, subject to a special rate or affected by registration rules. Those are legal and jurisdiction-specific questions. Enter the rate that actually applies to the transaction after checking the relevant tax authority or official invoice documentation.

Worked example and unit check

At an 18% rate, a tax-exclusive amount of 10,000 produces 1,800 GST and a total of 11,800. If 11,800 is already GST-inclusive at the same rate, dividing 11,800 by 1.18 gives a pre-tax amount of 10,000 and included GST of 1,800.

For business records, keep the original amount, tax rate and calculation basis visible. Rounding conventions can also matter when several line items are taxed separately, so a calculator result may differ by a small amount from an invoice that applies rounding at line level.

FAQ

How do I add GST to a price? Multiply the price by the GST rate, divide by 100, and add the tax to the original price.

How do I remove GST from an inclusive price? Divide the inclusive amount by 1 plus the GST rate as a decimal.

Is GST the same everywhere? No. Tax rules differ by jurisdiction and can change over time.
