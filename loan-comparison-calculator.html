Loan comparison formula

For each fixed-rate loan, monthly payment is M = P·r(1+r)n / ((1+r)n−1). Total interest is monthly payment × number of payments − principal.

Worked example

If two loans each finance 500,000 for 5 years, changing the rate from 10% to 12% changes both the monthly payment and total interest. Compare both figures, not payment alone.

Compare the right things, not just the rate

A loan comparison is meaningful only when the two scenarios use comparable inputs. Keep the principal, repayment frequency and term explicit, then account separately for fees, down payment, insurance or takaful and any variable-rate conditions. A lower monthly payment is not automatically a lower-cost loan.

Three questions this calculator answers

  • Payment: what scheduled monthly amount does each scenario produce?
  • Financing cost: how much interest is charged under the stated mathematical model?
  • Total repayment: how much is scheduled to be paid over the full term before extra lender charges?

How to compare loan offers

Compare the same assumptions across both offers where possible. A lower monthly payment can result from a longer term, so check total interest and total repayment rather than looking at the installment alone.

How to use

  1. Enter the amount, rate and term for Loan A.
  2. Enter the same values for Loan B.
  3. Compare monthly payment, total interest and total repayment.
  4. Check fees and lender terms separately.
