Worked examples

1 kilometre equals 0.621371 miles, while 1 kilogram equals 2.20462 pounds. Temperature conversions use scale-specific equations rather than a single multiplication factor.

Important unit check

Always confirm both the source and target units before using a converted value in a formula. Area, volume and temperature require particular care because their conversions are not interchangeable with ordinary length or mass conversions.

For technical work

Use the unit convention required by the formula or specification, and retain enough precision until the final step when accuracy matters.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are unit conversions?

The calculator uses explicit conversion factors and scale equations. Displayed results are rounded for readability, so retain appropriate precision for technical work.

Are US and UK units always the same?

No. Some units, especially gallons, differ between US customary and UK imperial systems. Check the selected unit label before using a result.

How should I convert temperature?

Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin use scale-specific equations rather than a simple multiplication factor.
