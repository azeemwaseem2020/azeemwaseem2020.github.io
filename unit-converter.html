How to use the unit converter

Select the quantity and units, enter a value, and review the converted result. Unit conversions are mathematical equivalences; they do not change the underlying measurement.

Common conversion groups

Use the converter for length, mass, temperature, area, volume, speed, pressure, energy and other supported units. Check the source and target units carefully before recording a result.

Temperature is different

Temperature conversions use an offset as well as a scale factor, so they are not handled like simple length or mass ratios.
