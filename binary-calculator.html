Convert binary numbers to decimal and understand the place-value method.

Binary to decimal formula

Each binary position represents a power of 2. Starting from the right, positions are 2⁰, 2¹, 2² and so on. Add the powers corresponding to digits equal to 1.

Worked example

101101₂ = 32 + 8 + 4 + 1 = 45₁₀.

Binary in computing

Binary is the base-2 number system used to represent digital states. Hexadecimal provides a compact way to display groups of binary bits.

FAQ

What digits are valid? Only 0 and 1.

How do I convert decimal to binary? Repeatedly divide the positive integer by 2 and read the remainders from last to first.
