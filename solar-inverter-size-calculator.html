Inverter sizing basics

Inverter sizing should consider continuous load and short-duration starting demand. A simple planning method totals the appliances expected to run together and adds a sensible margin. Motors, pumps, refrigerators and air conditioners can have startup demand above their running power.

Worked example

If simultaneous running loads total 2,000 W and a 25% planning margin is used, the modeled continuous requirement is 2,500 W. A real design must also check surge rating, battery voltage, PV input limits, efficiency and manufacturer specifications.

Pakistan solar example

List the appliances that must operate at the same time rather than simply adding every nameplate on the property. For a home system, pump and compressor startup can be more important than average daytime load.

FAQs

Does inverter wattage equal panel wattage? No. Does battery size determine inverter size? Not directly. Can a 3 kW inverter run 3 kW continuously? Check its rated continuous output and operating conditions.

Solar inverter sizing basics

An inverter converts the electrical output of a solar system into usable AC power. A basic sizing estimate starts with the simultaneous AC load and then checks the inverter's continuous and surge ratings. Panel capacity and inverter capacity are related but are not automatically identical.

Worked example

If the expected simultaneous load is 3,000 W and the design adds a modest operating margin, an inverter around 3.5–4 kW may be considered as a starting point, subject to the equipment specifications. Motors, pumps and compressors can require substantially higher starting power.

Pakistan context

For a home solar installation, list appliances, their rated power, expected simultaneous use and surge requirements. Also check local grid, battery, MPPT voltage range and protection requirements. The final selection should follow the inverter manufacturer's specifications and a qualified installer's design.

FAQs

Should inverter size equal panel size? Not necessarily. Why does surge matter? Some loads draw a higher starting current. Does this replace electrical design? No.
