Square Footage Calculator

Calculate the area of a rectangular room, floor, wall or plot and convert the result to square meters.

Formula

Square feet = length × width. Square meters = square feet × 0.09290304.

Practical use

Use the result as a starting point for flooring, paint, tile and space estimates. Irregular rooms should be divided into simpler shapes and added together.
