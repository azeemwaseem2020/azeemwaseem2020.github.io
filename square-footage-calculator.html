Square footage calculator: complete guide

Square footage measures the area of a flat surface in square feet (sq ft). It is commonly used for rooms, flooring, walls, patios and property measurements. This calculator multiplies length by width and also converts the result into square metres, which can be useful when comparing product specifications in different units.

Formula and conversion

Area (sq ft) = length (ft) × width (ft). To convert square feet to square metres, multiply by 0.09290304. One square metre is approximately 10.7639 square feet. Both dimensions should be entered in feet for the square-foot output to be correct.

How to use it

  1. Measure the length and width of the rectangular area in feet.
  2. Enter both positive measurements.
  3. Select Calculate to see square feet and square metres.
  4. For an irregular space, split it into rectangles and add their areas.

Example

A room 12 ft long and 10 ft wide has area 12 × 10 = 120 sq ft. In square metres, 120 × 0.09290304 ≈ 11.15 m². For flooring, order quantities may need extra material for cuts, pattern alignment and waste.

Common mistakes and practical limits

Do not multiply a length in feet by a width in metres. Convert both dimensions to one unit first. Area differs from perimeter: area measures the floor surface, while perimeter measures the distance around the room. This calculator assumes a rectangle and does not subtract closets, alcoves or openings automatically. Measure each relevant section separately and follow the flooring or construction supplier’s guidance for waste allowance. Results are estimates based on the measurements entered.
