Square Footage Calculator

Calculate the area of a rectangular room, floor, wall or plot and convert the result to square meters.

Formula

Square feet = length × width. Square meters = square feet × 0.09290304.

Practical use

Use the result as a starting point for flooring, paint, tile and space estimates. Irregular rooms should be divided into simpler shapes and added together.

Frequently asked questions

How many square feet are in one square metre?

One square metre is approximately 10.7639 square feet. To convert square metres to square feet, multiply by 10.7639; to convert square feet to square metres, divide by the same factor. Convert area units, not just the linear measurements.

How do I measure an irregular room?

Split the floor plan into simple rectangles, calculate each rectangle’s area, and add the results. Avoid overlapping sections and subtract openings that will not receive the material, if relevant. For a complex layout, sketch and label each measurement first.

Does square footage equal the amount of material to buy?

Not always. Flooring, tiles, paint and other materials can need extra coverage for cuts, pattern matching, coats, waste and packaging. Use the calculator for the basic area and follow product-specific coverage guidance for purchasing.

Square footage calculator: complete guide

Square footage measures the area of a flat surface in square feet (sq ft). It is commonly used for rooms, flooring, walls, patios and property measurements. This calculator multiplies length by width and also converts the result into square metres, which can be useful when comparing product specifications in different units.

Formula and conversion

Area (sq ft) = length (ft) × width (ft). To convert square feet to square metres, multiply by 0.09290304. One square metre is approximately 10.7639 square feet. Both dimensions should be entered in feet for the square-foot output to be correct.

How to use it

  1. Measure the length and width of the rectangular area in feet.
  2. Enter both positive measurements.
  3. Select Calculate to see square feet and square metres.
  4. For an irregular space, split it into rectangles and add their areas.

Example

A room 12 ft long and 10 ft wide has area 12 × 10 = 120 sq ft. In square metres, 120 × 0.09290304 ≈ 11.15 m². For flooring, order quantities may need extra material for cuts, pattern alignment and waste.

Common mistakes and practical limits

Do not multiply a length in feet by a width in metres. Convert both dimensions to one unit first. Area differs from perimeter: area measures the floor surface, while perimeter measures the distance around the room. This calculator assumes a rectangle and does not subtract closets, alcoves or openings automatically. Measure each relevant section separately and follow the flooring or construction supplier’s guidance for waste allowance. Results are estimates based on the measurements entered.

Area conversion example

A rectangular room 12 ft long and 10 ft wide has an area of 120 sq ft. Multiply 120 by 0.09290304 to get approximately 11.15 m². Area conversions use a squared conversion factor: converting each linear dimension and then multiplying should give the same result when units are handled consistently.

Estimating rooms and materials

  • Measure each rectangular section separately for an L-shaped or irregular room, then add the non-overlapping areas.
  • Keep a sketch with each dimension labelled so you do not count a section twice.
  • Use internal dimensions for floor coverage and clarify whether alcoves, closets or permanent openings are included.
  • Calculate material waste separately using the supplier’s installation guidance; this tool does not choose a waste percentage.

Square footage is an area measure, not a perimeter or property valuation. For legal, surveying or real-estate documentation, use the measurement method required by the relevant authority or professional.
