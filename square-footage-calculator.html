Measuring floor area

Square footage is an area measurement used for rooms, plots and building spaces. The basic calculation is straightforward, but irregular rooms should be divided into simpler shapes and then combined. Keep all dimensions in the same unit before multiplying.

Formula

Rectangle area = length × width. If dimensions are in feet, the result is square feet. For a rectangle measured in metres, calculate square metres and convert only after the area is found.

Worked example

A room 20 ft long and 12 ft wide has area 20 × 12 = 240 sq ft. If flooring is purchased, add an appropriate waste allowance separately rather than changing the room's physical area.

Pakistan-specific example

A homeowner in Pakistan planning tiles or carpet can measure each rectangular section of a room and add the areas. Columns, cupboards and openings can be deducted when they materially affect the actual floor coverage.

Common questions

Can I mix feet and inches? Convert them to one unit first.

Does area equal room perimeter? No. Area measures surface coverage.

Should waste be included? It is a purchasing allowance, not part of the measured floor area.

What about irregular rooms? Divide them into rectangles or other known shapes.

Square Footage: Measuring Areas Correctly

Square footage is area measured in square feet. For a rectangle, area = length × width. A 12 ft by 15 ft room therefore has 180 ft². For multiple rooms, calculate each area separately and add them. Irregular spaces can be divided into rectangles or other simple shapes.

For a circular area, use A = πr² with the radius in feet. A circle with a 5 ft radius has an area of about 78.54 ft². For a triangle, use A = ½bh. Choosing the correct shape formula matters more than rounding the final answer.

In Pakistan construction and property discussions, measurements may be given in square feet, square yards, marla, kanal or square metres. These units are not universally interchangeable because local conventions for marla can differ. Use the stated legal or project definition rather than assuming one conversion applies everywhere.

Material estimates

Square footage can estimate flooring, paint, tiles, carpet or roofing quantities, but practical ordering usually requires a waste allowance. For tile work, for example, cuts and breakage can mean buying more than the theoretical area. Follow the supplier's recommended wastage percentage and package coverage.

FAQs

How many square feet are in a square metre? About 10.7639 ft².

Should I include walls in room area? Usually floor area uses the relevant floor dimensions, not wall surface area.

Can I measure an L-shaped room? Yes. Split it into rectangles and add their areas.
