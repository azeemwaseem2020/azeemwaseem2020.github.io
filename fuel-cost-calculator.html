Fuel Cost Calculator

Estimate how much fuel a trip needs and its cost from distance, vehicle efficiency and fuel price.

How to use the fuel cost calculator

  1. Enter the trip distance.
  2. Enter your vehicle's fuel economy.
  3. Enter the current fuel price per litre.
  4. Calculate litres used and estimated trip cost.

Worked example

For 240 km at 12 km/L and a fuel price of 300 per litre: fuel used = 240 ÷ 12 = 20 L; estimated cost = 20 × 300 = 6,000.

Formula

Fuel used = distance ÷ km/L. Trip fuel cost = fuel used × price per liter. Actual consumption can vary with traffic, load, driving style and vehicle condition.

Estimating trip fuel cost

Fuel cost is driven by distance, fuel efficiency and the price entered per litre. The calculation is an estimate because real vehicles rarely maintain exactly the dashboard or laboratory efficiency figure on every trip. Traffic, tyre pressure, air conditioning, payload, road gradient, driving speed and engine condition can all change consumption. Use a measured average from several refuelling records when you need a more realistic planning number.

Formula

Fuel used = distance ÷ efficiency. Trip cost = fuel used × price per litre. If a vehicle travels 12 km per litre, each 120 km requires approximately 10 litres under the stated average efficiency.

Worked example

For a 240 km trip at 12 km/L and Rs 300 per litre, estimated fuel = 240 ÷ 12 = 20 L. Estimated cost = 20 × Rs 300 = Rs 6,000. A return trip would double the distance if the same route and conditions are used.

Pakistan-specific example

A driver travelling between Multan and another city can enter the planned route distance, the vehicle's realistic km/L figure and the current fuel price they expect to pay. If traffic is heavy or the vehicle is heavily loaded, using a conservative efficiency estimate can make the travel budget more useful.

Common questions

Should I use the manufacturer's mileage? A measured real-world average is usually more useful for trip budgeting.

Does this include tolls? No. Add tolls, parking and other travel costs separately.

Can I calculate a return trip? Yes. Enter the total return distance.

Why does actual fuel use differ? Driving conditions and vehicle state can materially change efficiency.
