Gold Price Calculator

Maintained by Abdul Qadir

Estimate the value of gold from weight, price per gram, and purity. Enter the current market price yourself because gold prices change continuously.

Gold value formula

Estimated gold value = weight × pure-gold price per gram × purity percentage ÷ 100. For example, 10 grams at $100 per gram and 75% purity gives 10 × 100 × 0.75 = $750.

Understanding gold purity

Pure gold is commonly represented as 24 karat. Lower-karat jewelry contains a smaller proportion of gold mixed with other metals. The purity input lets you approximate the gold-content value without assuming every item is pure gold.

Why your final selling price may differ

A calculator based on metal value is not a guaranteed dealer or jewelry quote. Actual prices can reflect the live market rate, currency conversion, assay, dealer spread, workmanship, taxes, and buying or selling conditions.

How to use the calculator

Enter the item weight, the current pure-gold price per gram in your chosen currency, and the estimated purity percentage. Check that all inputs use the same currency and weight unit basis.

Using the result responsibly

The calculator estimates the value of the gold content represented by your inputs. It is useful for checking arithmetic when comparing a quoted price, a known weight and an assumed purity. It should not be treated as a live market quotation or a guaranteed buy-back price. Gold markets move throughout the day, and a dealer may use a different benchmark, currency conversion, purity test or pricing spread.

For jewellery, the quoted amount can also differ from metal value because of making charges, wastage, stones, taxes, commissions and the dealer's buying or selling margin. If you are comparing offers, record the quoted gold rate, purity basis, weight and any deductions separately so that you are comparing like with like.

Worked example and unit check

Suppose an item weighs 12 grams, the pure-gold reference price is 30,000 per gram and the estimated purity is 75%. The estimated gold-content value is 12 × 30,000 × 0.75 = 270,000. The currency is not hard-coded into the calculation, so the same arithmetic works with rupees or another currency as long as the price and result use the same currency.

Before relying on the result, check that the weight is in grams, the price is for the same weight basis, and the purity is entered as a percentage rather than a decimal. For an actual transaction, verify the current market reference and the seller's or buyer's quoted terms independently.

FAQ

Does this use live gold prices? No. Enter a current price manually so the calculation can be updated without relying on a stale market quote.

How does purity affect value? At the same weight and pure-gold price, a lower purity produces a lower estimated gold-content value.

Is this the final jewelry price? No. It estimates metal value and does not automatically include making charges, dealer margins, taxes, or other costs.
