Core pressure relationship

Pressure is force distributed over area: P = F/A. Keep force and area in compatible units before interpreting the result.

ADVANCED PRESSURE MODEL CHECK

Pressure describes force distributed over area

Pressure is scalar even though the underlying force has direction. The simple P = F/A model is useful when the relevant force is normal to the area being considered.

Absolute vs gauge

Gas-pressure calculations should state whether the value is absolute pressure or pressure relative to atmospheric pressure.

Area selection

Use the actual surface over which the normal force is distributed. Changing the area changes the calculated pressure even when the total force stays the same.
