Water-intake estimates

Water needs vary with body size, climate, activity, diet, illness and other factors. A calculator can provide a general planning estimate, but it cannot determine an individual's exact hydration requirement. Food and other beverages can also contribute to total fluid intake.

Worked example

If a planning method uses 30 mL of fluid per kilogram of body weight, a 70 kg adult would have an estimate of 2,100 mL, or 2.1 litres, from that formula. This is an example of a calculation method, not a universal medical prescription.

Pakistan climate context

Hot weather, outdoor work and exercise can increase fluid losses, especially in summer. Thirst, urine concentration and medical guidance can be more informative than rigidly following a single calculator number.

FAQs

Is two litres right for everyone? No. Does exercise change needs? Often yes. Can excessive water be harmful? Yes; unusually high intake can disturb electrolyte balance.
