Momentum Calculator — Mass & Velocity

Calculate linear momentum using p = mv and solve the relationship in reverse for mass or velocity.

Calculate

Formula

p = mv. Momentum is a vector quantity, so the sign or direction of velocity matters when solving a one-dimensional problem.

Worked example

A 10 kg object moving at 5 m/s has momentum p = 10 × 5 = 50 kg·m/s in the direction of its velocity.

Momentum is not kinetic energy

Momentum changes linearly with velocity, while classical kinetic energy depends on velocity squared. They answer different physics questions.

Common mistakes

  • Using speed when direction/sign matters.
  • Mixing grams and kilograms without conversion.
  • Confusing momentum with force or kinetic energy.
  • Ignoring the system boundary when analysing total momentum.
