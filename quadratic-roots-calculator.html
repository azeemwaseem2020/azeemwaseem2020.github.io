Quadratic formula

For ax² + bx + c = 0, the roots are x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) ÷ 2a. The discriminant b² − 4ac tells you whether the equation has two real roots, one repeated real root, or complex roots.

Check the coefficients

The coefficient a must not be zero. Pay particular attention to negative signs when entering b and c, because a sign error changes the equation and therefore its roots.
