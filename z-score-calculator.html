How to interpret a z-score

A z-score tells you how far a value is from the mean in standard-deviation units. A positive score is above the mean, a negative score is below it, and a score near zero is close to the mean.

Example

If a test score is 80, the mean is 70, and the standard deviation is 5, the z-score is (80 − 70) ÷ 5 = 2. That means the score is two standard deviations above the mean.
