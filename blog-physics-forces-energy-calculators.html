Physics Force & Energy Calculators: A Problem-First Guide

Match the wording of a mechanics problem to the right calculator and check the units before trusting the result.

Force and motion

Use Force for mass and acceleration, Weight for gravitational force, Friction for resistance between surfaces, and Centripetal Force for the inward net force required by circular motion.

Energy and work

Kinetic Energy handles energy of translational motion. Potential Energy handles gravitational height-based energy. Work connects force, displacement and angle. Power describes the rate of energy transfer.

Momentum and impulse

Momentum uses mass and velocity, while Impulse connects force over time with momentum change.
