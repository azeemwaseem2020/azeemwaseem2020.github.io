Ovulation Calculator

Estimate an approximate ovulation date and fertile window from the first day of the last menstrual period and cycle length.

How the estimate works

The calculator estimates ovulation about 14 days before the next period and uses the cycle length you enter. The fertile-window result is intentionally approximate because ovulation does not occur on exactly the same day in every cycle.

Important health note

This calendar estimate is not a diagnostic test and should not be relied on as a contraceptive method. Cycle timing can vary, including with irregular periods. If you are trying to conceive, having difficulty conceiving, or need reliable contraception, a qualified healthcare professional can provide advice suited to your circumstances.
