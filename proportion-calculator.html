Checking a proportion

A proportion states that two ratios are equal. Cross-multiplication provides a simple way to verify a result when the quantities are non-zero and the ratio model is appropriate.

Worked check

For x/12 = 5/20, cross-multiplication gives 20x = 60, so x = 3. Substituting 3 into the original proportion confirms the equality.

Real-world use

Proportions can model rates and scaling relationships, but a real quantity does not automatically scale linearly just because two numbers can be written as a ratio.
