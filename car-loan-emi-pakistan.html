How car loan EMI works in Pakistan

Start with the vehicle price and the amount you will pay upfront. The simplified financed principal is vehicle price minus down payment. The monthly payment then depends on the financed amount, the customer rate used in the model and the number of monthly instalments. A larger down payment usually reduces the amount financed; a longer term may reduce the monthly payment while increasing total finance cost.

For a practical comparison, keep the vehicle price, down payment, tenure and rate consistent when testing different offers. A quoted instalment may also include or exclude takaful/insurance, tracker, registration, processing fees, documentation charges, balloon payments or other product-specific items. Ask for a complete written repayment schedule before relying on a headline monthly figure.

Worked example: a PKR 5 million car

Suppose a vehicle costs PKR 5,000,000 and you pay PKR 1,500,000 upfront. The simple financed amount is PKR 3,500,000 before fees and add-ons. Down payment, tenure and total cost

Compare the upfront payment with the total paid across the full term. Extending the tenure can make an instalment look more manageable but may increase total cost and keep you committed for longer. Include fees and insurance/takaful separately when they are not part of the financed principal. Also budget for running costs such as fuel, maintenance, registration and annual taxes; these are ownership costs, not EMI.

Conventional car loan versus Islamic car financing

Conventional lending and Islamic products may use different contractual structures. For example, Meezan Bank's Car Ijarah describes a lease-based product. This calculator applies a standard amortizing-payment formula and does not model the legal rental schedule, ownership transfer, takaful, residual value or Shariah contract of any specific product. Use the institution's official quotation and schedule for an Islamic financing comparison.

Bank offers and KIBOR

When comparing offers from banks such as HBL, UBL or Meezan Bank, enter the rate and fees from the actual product documents. No current bank-specific rate is assumed here. SBP's financial-markets glossary defines KIBOR as a benchmark rate; a benchmark is not automatically the customer's final financing rate. A product may specify a benchmark, agreed spread and repricing schedule, or may use another pricing method.

The EMI formula used

For a standard reducing-balance loan, the monthly payment is EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1), where P is the financed principal after down payment, r is the annual customer rate divided by 12 and then by 100, and n is the number of monthly payments. If the entered rate is zero, the calculator divides principal equally across the payment months.

The schedule assumes regular monthly payments and a fixed rate. It does not automatically add fees, insurance/takaful or other costs into the principal; the optional fee is shown separately in total cash cost.

Reducing balance vs flat rate

This page uses a reducing-balance formula. If a provider quotes a flat rate, do not compare the percentage directly with a reducing-balance rate. Ask for the full schedule and total amount payable. You can also read reducing balance vs flat rate in Pakistan.

What the amortization schedule tells you

Each row separates the scheduled payment into principal and finance cost, then shows the remaining balance. In a standard reducing-balance model, finance cost is higher when the outstanding balance is higher and generally declines as principal is repaid. Actual contracts can use different day counts, payment dates, rounding or rate-reset conventions.

Before comparing a bank or Islamic financing offer

  • Confirm the exact financed amount and the upfront contribution.
  • Confirm whether the quoted rate is fixed, variable, benchmark-linked or a flat rate.
  • Ask how KIBOR or another benchmark, any spread and the repricing frequency apply to your specific contract.
  • List processing fees, insurance/takaful, documentation and any compulsory add-ons separately.
  • Request the complete official repayment or rental schedule and early-settlement terms.
  • For Islamic financing, compare the actual contract structure rather than assuming a conventional EMI model fully represents it.

Frequently asked questions

How is car loan EMI calculated?

The estimate uses the amount financed after down payment, the entered annual rate and the number of monthly payments. Fees or takaful/insurance are only reflected if you include them in the inputs or financed amount.

Does this show current HBL, UBL or Meezan rates?

No. Enter the rate and charges from the institution's current official offer. Calcora does not publish or guarantee bank-specific rates on this page.

Can I use it for Islamic car financing?

Use it only as a rough payment-arithmetic comparison. Islamic Ijarah or other structures can have different rental, ownership, residual-value and takaful terms; compare the provider's actual schedule.

Is KIBOR the rate I should enter?

Not necessarily. Enter the customer rate stated in your offer. If the product is benchmark-linked, KIBOR may be one component plus an agreed spread, subject to contract terms.
