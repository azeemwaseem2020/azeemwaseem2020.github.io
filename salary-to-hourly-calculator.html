Converting annual salary to an hourly rate

An annual salary does not have one universal hourly equivalent because the result depends on paid working weeks, hours per week, leave treatment and whether the salary includes bonuses or allowances. This calculator is best used as a transparent planning estimate: choose assumptions that match the job you are evaluating.

Formula

Estimated hourly rate = annual salary ÷ (weeks worked per year × hours worked per week). If a schedule uses 52 weeks and 40 hours each week, the denominator is 2,080 hours.

Worked example

An annual salary of Rs 1,560,000 using 52 weeks × 40 hours gives Rs 1,560,000 ÷ 2,080 = Rs 750/hour. If unpaid leave reduces the actual working weeks, the effective hourly figure changes.

Pakistan-specific example

A Pakistani employee comparing a monthly salary with freelance or contract work can use the estimate after separating allowances, bonuses and employer benefits. A salary package may include benefits that are not received as cash, so an hourly comparison should not rely on the headline salary alone.

Common questions

Does this include bonuses? Only if you include them in the salary figure.

Should I use 52 weeks? Use the working-year assumption that matches the job.

Does paid leave reduce the hourly rate? It depends on whether the salary is paid during leave.

Is this a legal wage calculation? No. It is a planning conversion.
