Converting salary to an hourly rate

To estimate an hourly equivalent, divide annual gross salary by the number of paid working hours in the year. The result is an estimate because working hours, paid leave, holidays and unpaid time can change the effective rate.

Example

If annual salary is PKR 1,200,000 and the assumed schedule is 2,080 paid hours, the estimated hourly rate is about PKR 576.92.

Use the same assumptions

When comparing jobs, keep the hours, pay period and gross-versus-net basis consistent. Benefits, bonuses and commuting costs may also matter when comparing total compensation.

Salary to Hourly Calculator: Convert Annual Pay Fairly

This calculator converts annual gross salary into an estimated hourly, weekly and monthly equivalent. The hourly figure depends on the hours and weeks entered, so it is a planning comparison rather than a promise of the amount shown on a payslip. Make sure the salary and working-time assumptions describe the same job.

Formula

Estimated hourly rate = annual salary ÷ (hours worked per week × weeks worked per year). Weekly equivalent = annual salary ÷ weeks per year. Monthly equivalent = annual salary ÷ 12. Paid leave, unpaid breaks, overtime, bonuses and benefits can change the relationship between contractual salary and actual hours worked.

Worked example

For an annual salary of PKR 1,560,000, a 40-hour week and 52 working weeks, estimated hourly pay is 1,560,000 ÷ (40 × 52) = PKR 750. The weekly equivalent is PKR 30,000 and the monthly average is PKR 130,000. These are gross equivalents before tax or deductions; if some weeks are unpaid or hours vary, the effective rate will differ.

How to use the calculator

  1. Enter annual gross salary, excluding amounts you do not consider guaranteed salary.
  2. Enter normal paid working hours per week, not the full time you spend commuting or on unpaid breaks.
  3. Enter the number of weeks that best reflects the job arrangement.
  4. Compare hourly, weekly and monthly outputs and state the assumptions when comparing offers.

Comparing jobs in Pakistan

Two roles with the same annual salary may differ in working days, paid leave, bonuses, transport, medical benefits and overtime eligibility. Compare the full compensation package and the actual schedule. For a monthly salary, dividing by 12 gives an average month, not necessarily the amount paid on every payroll date. This calculator does not decide whether an employer's arrangement meets minimum-wage or overtime requirements; check applicable rules and your employment terms separately.

Salary to Hourly Calculator: Work Hours and Total Compensation

An hourly equivalent helps compare salaried and hourly roles, but it is only meaningful when annual salary and working-hour assumptions use the same basis. Divide annual gross salary by annual work hours to estimate gross hourly equivalent. Annual hours are often estimated from hours per week multiplied by working weeks per year, but paid leave, holidays, unpaid time and overtime can change the effective rate.

Formula and example

Hourly equivalent = annual gross salary ÷ (hours per week × weeks per year). For PKR 1,200,000 per year, 40 hours per week and 52 weeks, the assumed annual hours are 2,080 and the hourly equivalent is about PKR 576.92. If the employee works fewer paid hours, the effective hourly value is higher.

How to compare offers

  1. Use annual gross salary rather than mixing net and gross amounts.
  2. Enter realistic weekly hours and paid weeks for each role.
  3. Compare bonuses, employer contributions, leave, insurance and other benefits separately.
  4. Include commuting costs and unpaid overtime when assessing the practical value of an offer.

This conversion does not determine the legal minimum wage, overtime eligibility or the hourly rate required by a contract. Those depend on local rules and the employer's pay structure. If annual salary includes a discretionary bonus, calculate a base-salary-only comparison as well as a total-compensation scenario.
