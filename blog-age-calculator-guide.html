Age Calculator Guide: Exact Age, Birthdays, Dates and Date Differences

Exact age is a date calculation, not simply a subtraction of two calendar years. Months have different lengths and leap years add another calendar rule.

How age is calculated

Start with the year difference, then check whether the birthday has occurred in the target year. Years, months and days require calendar-aware arithmetic because a month is not a fixed number of days.

Why year subtraction can be wrong

If the target date is before the birthday in that year, simple year subtraction gives an age one year too high.

Leap years

In the Gregorian calendar, years divisible by 4 are generally leap years, except century years not divisible by 400. Thus 2000 was a leap year while 1900 was not.

Date difference versus age

Date difference measures separation between two dates. Age measures how old a person is on a particular target date. They are related but may be expressed using different conventions.

Future dates and privacy

For future milestones, enter the actual target date. For date-only calculations, avoid providing unnecessary personal information to online tools.

FAQ

Does age increase on the birthday?

Yes, the completed-year age increases when the birthday occurs on the target date.

Why does February affect some results?

February has 28 days in a common year and 29 in a leap year, changing some calendar intervals.

Can age be calculated in days?

Yes, using the exact two dates and a defined calendar convention.
