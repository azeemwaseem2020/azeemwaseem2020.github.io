Calculate sales tax, pre-tax price and final price from an amount and tax rate.

Sales tax

Formula and price basis

For a tax-exclusive price, tax = price × rate ÷ 100, and total = price + tax. For a tax-inclusive total, the embedded tax portion is total × rate ÷ (100 + rate); do not add the tax rate a second time to a price that already includes it.

For example, at a hypothetical 18% rate, a pre-tax price of PKR 10,000 produces PKR 1,800 tax and a PKR 11,800 total. This is an arithmetic example, not a claim that 18% applies to every product or transaction today.

In Pakistan, federal sales tax on goods, provincial sales tax on services, exemptions and special regimes may differ. Use the rate and taxable base that apply to the specific transaction and tax period; this calculator does not determine legal taxability.

Frequently asked questions

How is sales tax calculated?

Sales tax is calculated by multiplying the pre-tax price by the tax rate expressed as a decimal. The tax is then added to the original price.

Does this include local or regional tax rules?

No. Enter the tax rate that applies to your transaction. Actual rules, exemptions and combined rates vary by location.

Can I calculate the final price after tax?

Yes. The calculator reports both the estimated tax amount and the total price after tax.

How sales tax is calculated

Sales tax can be calculated by multiplying the taxable price by the applicable tax rate. The final amount depends on whether the entered price is before tax or already includes tax.

Example

If a taxable price is PKR 10,000 and the rate is 18%, tax is PKR 1,800 and the total is PKR 11,800. If a quoted price already includes tax, the calculation must reverse the tax-inclusive amount instead of adding 18% again.

Check the applicable rate

Tax rates and rules can differ by jurisdiction, product, service and tax status. Use the rate that applies to the transaction rather than assuming one percentage works for every sale.

Sales-tax FAQ

Is tax always added to the displayed price?

No. Some prices are tax-exclusive and others are tax-inclusive. Confirm the pricing basis before calculating.
