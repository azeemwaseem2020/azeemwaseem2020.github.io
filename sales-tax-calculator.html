Frequently asked questions

How is sales tax calculated?

Sales tax is calculated by multiplying the pre-tax price by the tax rate expressed as a decimal. The tax is then added to the original price.

Does this include local or regional tax rules?

No. Enter the tax rate that applies to your transaction. Actual rules, exemptions and combined rates vary by location.

Can I calculate the final price after tax?

Yes. The calculator reports both the estimated tax amount and the total price after tax.

Calculating sales tax

A sales-tax calculation is straightforward mathematically but depends on the tax rate and the taxable base. The rate shown on a receipt may not apply to every item, and some transactions can have exemptions or special treatment. Use the calculator for arithmetic after identifying the rate that actually applies to the transaction.

Formula

Tax = taxable amount × tax rate ÷ 100. Total including tax = taxable amount + tax. To remove a tax already included in a tax-inclusive price, the calculation is different: tax = total × rate ÷ (100 + rate).

Worked example

If a taxable amount is Rs 10,000 and the entered rate is 18%, tax = Rs 1,800 and total = Rs 11,800. If Rs 11,800 is already tax-inclusive at 18%, the tax portion is Rs 1,800 rather than 18% of Rs 11,800.

Pakistan-specific example

A Pakistani business should confirm the applicable sales-tax treatment, registration status, product category and jurisdiction before using a rate in an invoice. The calculator performs the percentage arithmetic; it does not determine tax liability or exemptions.

Common questions

Is the tax rate universal? No. Rates and treatment can vary.

Can I calculate tax-inclusive prices? Use the inclusive method when the displayed total already contains tax.

Does this file a tax return? No.

Can exemptions be handled automatically? Not unless the specific calculator explicitly models them.
