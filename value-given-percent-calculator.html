Finding the original value from a percentage

If a known amount represents a percentage of an unknown value, use original value = known amount ÷ (percentage ÷ 100). This reverses the usual percentage-of-a-value calculation.

Worked example

If Rs. 15,000 represents 25% of a total, the total is 15,000 ÷ 0.25 = Rs. 60,000. Checking the result, 25% of Rs. 60,000 is Rs. 15,000.

A practical Pakistan example

If a business says a Rs. 30,000 expense represents 12% of a project's total budget, the implied budget is 30,000 ÷ 0.12 = Rs. 250,000. The result assumes the stated percentage and amount refer to the same base.

Common mistakes

Do not multiply the known amount by the percentage when you are trying to recover the original base. Also ensure the percentage is not zero.

FAQ

What is the original value if 20 is 10%? 200.

Can the percentage be greater than 100%? Yes mathematically, although the interpretation depends on the context.

How can I check the result? Multiply the calculated original value by the stated percentage.
