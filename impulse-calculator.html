Impulse Calculator — Force, Time & Momentum Change

Calculate impulse from force and time, or solve for force or time using J = FΔt, with units, steps and momentum-change context.

Formula

J = FΔt. Impulse equals the change in momentum for a closed-system force interaction: J = Δp.

Worked example

A 50 N force acting for 0.2 s produces 10 N·s of impulse.

Common mistakes

  • Using total elapsed time instead of the interaction interval.
  • Confusing impulse with force.
  • Ignoring direction when impulse is treated as a vector.
