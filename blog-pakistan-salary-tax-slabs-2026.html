Pakistan Salary Tax Slabs 2026-27 Explained

For income earned from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027, Pakistan's salaried tax table has eight bands. The important point is that the percentage shown for a band applies to the amount above that band's threshold, not to your entire salary.

Updated 2 October 2026. This guide explains the published rules in plain language; it is not tax advice.

Why the 35% rate does not apply to the whole salary

Suppose taxable income is Rs. 7.5 million. The calculation is Rs. 1,424,000 plus 35% of Rs. 500,000, giving Rs. 1,599,000 before considering any other applicable rules. You do not multiply the full Rs. 7.5 million by 35%.

What changed in 2026?

The Federal Board of Revenue's Budget 2026-27 summary says salaried rates were reduced through restructuring, with additional intermediate slabs and the top 35% threshold moved from Rs. 4.1 million to Rs. 7 million.

Salary is not always the same as taxable income

A payroll figure can include components that require separate treatment under tax law. The tax table applies to taxable income under the relevant rules, so a simple annual salary multiplication is not always a complete return calculation.
