Down payment formula

Down payment = purchase price × down-payment percentage ÷ 100. The amount left to finance before other costs is purchase price minus the down payment.

Worked example

For a purchase price of PKR 10,000,000 and a 20% down payment, the upfront payment is PKR 2,000,000 and the remaining purchase amount is PKR 8,000,000. This example illustrates the arithmetic; it is not a quote or a recommendation about how much to borrow.

Planning a down payment

A down payment is the amount paid upfront toward a purchase, reducing the amount that must be financed. A larger down payment can reduce the financed principal, but it also requires more cash upfront.

Worked example

For a PKR 10,000,000 purchase with a 20% down payment, the upfront amount is PKR 2,000,000 and the financed amount is PKR 8,000,000 before other costs.

Budget beyond the down payment

Buyers may also need to plan for taxes, registration, insurance, fees, moving costs and other transaction expenses. Do not treat the down payment as the complete upfront budget.

Down-payment FAQ

Does a larger down payment always make a purchase better?

Not necessarily. It can reduce borrowing, but it also ties up cash that may be needed for emergencies or other costs. Compare the full financing and liquidity picture.

Budget for more than the deposit

The down payment is only one part of the cash needed to complete a purchase. Depending on the transaction, additional costs may include taxes, transfer or registration fees, valuation, legal services, insurance, moving expenses and lender charges. Keep these separate unless you deliberately choose to include them in the purchase price.

How the percentage affects borrowing

For a fixed purchase price, increasing the down-payment percentage reduces the amount left to finance. That may reduce the principal used in a loan calculation, but the eventual monthly instalment and total interest also depend on the interest rate, repayment term, fees and lender rules. Use the Loan EMI Calculator to estimate repayments after you establish the amount financed.

Check eligibility and cash reserves

Minimum down-payment requirements vary by lender, property or vehicle type, and local rules. A larger deposit can reduce borrowing but may leave less cash available for emergencies and other upfront costs. Confirm current requirements with the relevant lender or seller before making a commitment.

Budget for the full upfront cash requirement

The down payment is not necessarily the total cash needed to complete a purchase. Depending on the property, vehicle, lender and jurisdiction, additional costs may include taxes, registration, valuation, legal or processing fees, insurance and moving or delivery costs. Keep these separate from the amount financed so the deposit calculation is not mistaken for a complete affordability assessment.

For example, a PKR 10,000,000 purchase with a 20% deposit has a PKR 2,000,000 down payment and PKR 8,000,000 remaining purchase price before fees. Confirm current lender requirements and retain an emergency reserve before committing funds.
