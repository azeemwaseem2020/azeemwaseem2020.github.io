How refinance break-even works

Refinancing costs matter because a lower payment does not automatically mean a lower overall cost. A simple break-even estimate divides the refinancing cost by the monthly payment savings; changing the new term can also change total interest.

Evaluate mortgage refinancing

Refinancing replaces an existing loan with new financing. A useful comparison considers the new payment, remaining balance, new interest cost, fees and the time you expect to keep the loan. A lower monthly payment alone does not prove that refinancing saves money.

Worked example

Suppose a borrower owes PKR 4,000,000 and can refinance at a lower rate but must pay PKR 100,000 in fees. If the monthly saving is PKR 10,000, a simple break-even estimate is 100,000 ÷ 10,000 = 10 months. The full comparison should also account for the new term and total interest.

Pakistan context

Before refinancing, check bank processing charges, valuation or legal costs, early-settlement terms and whether the new facility is fixed or variable. For Shariah-compliant financing, use the institution's stated structure rather than applying a conventional interest formula automatically.

FAQs

What is break-even? The point where cumulative savings offset refinancing costs. Can refinancing increase total interest? Yes, especially if the new loan resets to a much longer term. Is a lower rate always cheaper? Not necessarily; fees and term matter.

Refinance break-even and total cost

A simple break-even calculation divides refinancing costs by monthly savings, but this is only a first check. A new loan can have a different term, payment schedule and total interest. For example, PKR 120,000 in refinancing costs with PKR 10,000 monthly savings gives a 12-month simple break-even, but extending the loan term can still increase lifetime interest.

Before refinancing in Pakistan, compare the remaining balance, new principal, rate structure, processing and valuation charges, early-settlement costs and any insurance. If the new rate is variable, model plausible rate changes rather than assuming today's rate lasts for the full term.
