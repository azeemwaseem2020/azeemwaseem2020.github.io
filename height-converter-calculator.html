Height conversion basics

Height can be represented in centimetres, metres, feet and inches. The important point is to preserve the same physical length when changing units. One inch equals 2.54 centimetres, while one foot equals 12 inches.

Worked example

A height of 170 cm converts to 1.70 m. Dividing 170 by 2.54 gives about 66.93 inches, which is approximately 5 ft 6.93 in. Rounding to whole inches can slightly change the displayed value.

A practical Pakistan example

Medical, school and identity records in Pakistan may use centimetres, while international forms sometimes ask for feet and inches. Convert the recorded measurement rather than estimating it visually.

Common mistakes

Remember that 1 foot is 12 inches, not 10, and that centimetres are converted to inches using 2.54 cm per inch. Keep decimal inches separate from feet when converting back to a combined feet-and-inches format.

FAQ

How many centimetres are in an inch? Exactly 2.54 cm.

How many inches are in a foot? 12 inches.

Can I enter decimal feet? Yes, but decimal feet must be converted carefully if you need feet-and-inches notation.
