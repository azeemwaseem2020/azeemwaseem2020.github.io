How to use the height converter

Enter a height in centimetres. The calculator converts the same measurement into metres and feet plus remaining inches; it does not estimate or assess health.

Common height conversions

1 metre equals 100 centimetres and 1 inch equals 2.54 centimetres. Feet and inches are often used together, so convert each component consistently before comparing measurements.

Worked example

A height of 175 cm is 1.75 m and about 68.90 inches. Dividing inches by 12 gives about 5 feet 8.90 inches.

Why unit consistency matters

Height conversion is a unit conversion, not an estimate of health or body composition. Keep the original measurement and the converted value when precision matters.

Feet and inches

For a measurement given as 5 ft 9 in, first convert feet to inches: 5 × 12 + 9 = 69 inches. Multiplying by 2.54 gives 175.26 cm. Keeping the steps explicit helps avoid treating the inch portion as a decimal fraction of a foot.

Conversion accuracy

For a precise conversion, keep the full factor through the calculation and round only the displayed answer. A difference in the final decimal places is normally a rounding choice rather than a different definition of the units.

Quick reference

100 cm = 1 m, 12 inches = 1 foot, and 1 inch = 2.54 cm. Use the same reference factors when converting between metric and imperial height units.

For measurements used in forms or records, keep the original unit visible alongside the converted result so the value can be checked later.

Convert decimal feet without mixing formats

Feet-and-inches notation is not the same as decimal feet. A height of 5 ft 6 in equals 66 inches, or 167.64 cm. Decimal feet such as 5.5 ft also equals 5 ft 6 in, but 5.6 ft equals 5 ft 7.2 in, not 5 ft 6 in. Convert the feet portion to inches and add the remaining inches before multiplying by 2.54 to obtain centimetres.

Measurement and rounding tips

Measure height on a level surface without shoes when a consistent body-height measurement is required. Posture and measurement technique can cause small differences. For forms that request whole centimetres or whole inches, round only after converting the full value and follow the form's instructions. Keep the source measurement recorded so another person can reproduce the conversion.

Height conversion formulas and examples

The exact relationships are 1 inch = 2.54 cm, 1 foot = 12 inches, and 1 metre = 100 cm. To convert centimetres to inches, divide by 2.54. To express the result in feet and inches, divide total inches by 12; the whole-number part is feet and the remainder is inches.

Worked example: 170 cm

170 cm ÷ 2.54 ≈ 66.929 inches. That is 5 whole feet (60 inches) plus 6.929 inches, so the height is approximately 5 ft 6.9 in, or 1.70 m. Rounding to the nearest whole inch gives 5 ft 7 in; keep the unrounded conversion if precision matters.

Decimal feet versus feet-and-inches notation

Decimal feet are a single decimal measurement: 5.5 ft = 5 ft + 0.5 × 12 in = 5 ft 6 in. But 5.6 ft = 5 ft 7.2 in, not 5 ft 6 in. The digits after the decimal point are fractions of a foot, not inches.

Conversion workflow and rounding rules

  1. Enter the measured height in the unit shown by the input field.
  2. Convert to centimetres or inches using the exact relationship 1 inch = 2.54 cm.
  3. For feet and inches, take the whole number of feet from total inches ÷ 12, then keep the remainder as inches.
  4. Round only the final displayed result to the precision required.

For another check, 160 cm ÷ 2.54 ≈ 62.99 inches, which is about 5 ft 3 in when rounded to the nearest whole inch. A small difference can appear when a decimal height is rounded to whole inches, so the metric and imperial display may not convert back to precisely the same printed number. That is normal rounding, not a change in the underlying height.

Do not read 5.3 feet as 5 feet 3 inches: 0.3 of a foot equals 3.6 inches. This distinction is especially important when entering heights on medical, sports or travel forms. If a form requests feet and inches, use that format rather than a decimal number of feet. For official records, follow the form’s rounding instructions and use the original measured value when available.

Height converter FAQs

Is 180 cm exactly 5 ft 11 in?

180 cm is about 70.87 inches, or 5 ft 10.87 in. Rounded to the nearest inch, it is 5 ft 11 in.

When should I round the converted height?

Convert using the full value and round only at the end, following the precision required by the form or measurement purpose.

Does converting units change the measured height?

No. It changes the unit and numeric representation, not the physical measurement.
