How to use

Enter a height in centimetres and calculate. The result shows metres plus a feet-and-inches representation so you can use the measurement in the format you need.

Reading the result

Feet and inches are displayed separately. For example, 180 cm is approximately 5 ft 10.9 in. A decimal value such as 5.91 ft is a different way of expressing the same length.

How to convert height measurements

Enter a height in the available unit and convert it to another supported unit. Common examples include centimetres, metres, inches and feet.

Feet and inches

When height is written as feet and inches, the inches portion is converted as part of the total measurement. Keep the distinction between a decimal number of feet and a feet-and-inches measurement.
