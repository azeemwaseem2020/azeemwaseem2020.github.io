Common height conversions

1 metre equals 100 centimetres and 1 inch equals 2.54 centimetres. Feet and inches are often used together, so convert each component consistently before comparing measurements.

Worked example

A height of 175 cm is 1.75 m and about 68.90 inches. Dividing inches by 12 gives about 5 feet 8.90 inches.

Why unit consistency matters

Height conversion is a unit conversion, not an estimate of health or body composition. Keep the original measurement and the converted value when precision matters.
